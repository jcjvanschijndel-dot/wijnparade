description_en: >-
  Novello is one of the eleven municipalities whose vineyards may produce
  Barolo, but in terms of tourism it is still the quietest and most authentic of
  them all. In the restored historic centre you'll find ANT: a restaurant in a
  Piedmontese farmhouse with unique architecture of interconnected vaulted
  rooms. From the moment you walk in, it feels like dining at friends' house
  rather than in a restaurant. The cooking here is at Michelin-star level, so
  this is the place to eat when you're in Piedmont! The most special experience
  is choosing your wine: you walk through the spectacular vaulted cellars and
  pick your bottle yourself, not from a list but literally from the shelves,
  guided by someone who knows every bottle. After dinner you can stay the night
  in one of the apartments in the same building. One set menu of 8 to 9 courses:
  no choice, no menu, except for intolerances. The menu changes every one to two
  months. Eclectic, international cooking with surprising combinations.
