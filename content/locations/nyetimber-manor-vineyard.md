description_en: >-
  One of the founders of English sparkling wine, and... with a Dutch owner!
  Eight vineyards of its own, together more than 150 hectares: six in West
  Sussex (on sandy greensand soils) and two in Hampshire (on chalk), all
  south-facing on a gentle slope for optimal sun and shelter. The Open Day tour
  (2.5 hours) starts in the "White Barn" with an introduction to the history of
  the estate, followed by a guided walk through the vineyards, including some of
  the oldest vines in England, with a ranger from the South Downs National Park.
  Along the way you learn about the soil, the microclimate and why southern
  England is so well suited to sparkling wine. The tour ends in the 15th-century
  Medieval Barn with a guided tasting of four top wines: the gold-medal-winning
  Classic Cuvée Multi-Vintage, Rosé Multi-Vintage, Cuvée Chérie Multi-Vintage
  and the Blanc de Blancs.
