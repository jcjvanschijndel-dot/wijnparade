description_en: >-
  On the corner of the Schieweg and the Teilingerstraat, seven minutes' walk
  from Rotterdam Central and tucked away in the Agniesebuurt, is Klaargemaakt. A
  restaurant that feels like a living room: leaded windows, burning candles,
  dark walls, lots of greenery. You're noticed and welcomed straight away. The
  atmosphere is homely and relaxed; the cooking is anything but ordinary.
  Italian-oriented, with vegetables as the main ingredient, but plenty of choice
  for meat lovers too. The combinations are surprising: salmon cured in
  liquorice and vodka, Roquefort with smoked beetroot and amarena cherries,
  home-made ravioli with cognac butter sauce. Everything is clearly labelled
  vegan or vegetarian. Choose à la carte or the chef's 5-course menu, with an
  optional matching wine pairing or alcohol-free pairing. The wine list is at
  least as special as the food: innovative wines, a wide selection of natural
  wines, good beers and partly home-made syrups and fermented drinks.
