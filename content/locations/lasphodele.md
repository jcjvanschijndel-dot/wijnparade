description_en: >-
  Just outside Lorient, on the lande du Courégant in Ploemeur, stands the
  concrete, bunker-like building of L'Asphodèle. This regenerative farming
  project, led by market gardener Grégoire Desnoulez and restaurateur Florent
  Piar (of Les Résistants in Paris), transformed five hectares of farmland and
  the abandoned buildings of a former campsite. From these ruins they built a
  restaurant that works in symbiosis with a farm and the natural surroundings.
  The restaurant is named after the Asphodèle d'Arrondeau, a protected plant
  endemic to the Breton coastal heathlands. The kitchen is supplied directly
  from its own organic kitchen garden and with wild plants from the heath. 95%
  of all other produce comes from the region through the Les Résistants network
  of 150 French peasant farms and producers. Chef Lucas Bahuon works with a
  seasonal menu, daily suggestions based on what arrives, and sharing dishes for
  big tables. Sommelière Marie Pérodeau put together a wine list of French
  natural wines from committed producers. Gault&Millau notes: "The wine list
  alone is worth the detour."
