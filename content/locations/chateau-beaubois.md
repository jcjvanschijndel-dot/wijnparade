  De naam Beaubois gaat terug tot de dertiende eeuw, toen de cisterciënzers van

  wijnen proeven en het domein bezoeken. Een aanrader tussen Nîmes en de
  Camargue.
description_en: >-
  The name Beaubois goes back to the thirteenth century, when the Cistercians of
  the abbey of Franquevaux cleared the forest here to plant vines and olive
  trees. The estate lies on the southern edge of the Costières de Nîmes,
  overlooking the lakes of the Camargue.


  Fanny and François Boyer, sister and brother, are the fourth generation.
  François looks after the vineyard, Fanny the wine. The estate has been
  certified organic since 2009 and biodynamic (Demeter) since 2020. You can
  taste the wines and visit the estate. Recommended between Nîmes and the
  Camargue.
