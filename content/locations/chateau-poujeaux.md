description_en: >-
  Back in the Middle Ages, Poujeaux belonged to the same seigneury as
  Latour Saint-Mambert, the estate that would later become the
  world-famous Château Latour. Today the eighteenth-century property
  is owned by the Cuvelier family, who also own Clos Fourtet in
  Saint-Émilion, and it was rated Cru Bourgeois Exceptionnel in the
  (now-discontinued) 2003 Cru Bourgeois classification, the highest
  honour for a Médoc château outside the 1855 classification. The
  68-hectare vineyard sits largely on the gravelly Poujeaux plateau,
  one of Moulis's best terroirs.


  A visit, by appointment, includes tasting both the grand vin and the
  second wine, La Salle de Château Poujeaux. Recommended for anyone
  wanting to discover Moulis-en-Médoc, smaller and less famous than
  its neighbours but with a history just as deep.
