description_en: >-
  Right on the Piazza Michele Ferrero, the bustling heart of Alba, the truffle
  and wine capital of the Langhe, is this elegant wine bar of the cantina of the
  same name in Neviglie. The Sarotto family has made wine in the Langhe for more
  than a hundred years and now has more than 90 hectares spread over the most
  prestigious production areas of Piedmont: Neviglie, Barolo, Novello, Neive and
  Mango, plus a Cortese cellar in Gavi. The bar in Alba is the estate's calling
  card, a place where the house wines are literally presented on the town
  square. Only their own wines, also by the glass: Dolcetto d'Alba, Barbera
  d'Alba, Nebbiolo, Barbaresco and Barolo, plus the fresh, accessible ILO range
  (low alcohol, white and rosé). The top wines are the Gaia Principe Barbaresco
  and the Chardonnay Puro. With every order you automatically get a plate of
  antipasti and grissini, a Piedmontese custom taken seriously here. Prices are
  strikingly reasonable for the quality and location.
