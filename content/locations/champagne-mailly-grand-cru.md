description_en: >-
  Mailly Grand Cru, founded in 1929 by a group of winegrowers, is one of the
  oldest cooperatives in Champagne and one of the few houses with exclusively
  grand cru. Its vineyards of more than seventy hectares surround the village of
  Mailly-Champagne, in the Montagne de Reims nature park; three quarters is
  pinot noir.


  Between 1929 and 1965 the founders dug their cellars out of the chalk by hand,
  over seven levels. You taste the Champagnes in a room overlooking the
  vineyards. A lovely address to taste grand cru Champagne between Reims and
  Épernay.
