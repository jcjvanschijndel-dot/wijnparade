description_en: >-
  On the Volkstraat, in the Zuid district, is the wine bar of sommelier Tawat.
  He came from Thailand to Belgium with his family, worked in hospitality for
  years and discovered wine there; after wine courses and a spell at an Italian
  wine bar he opened his own place. His partner is William Wouters, who makes
  wine in Portugal together with Filipa Pato.


  There are always around 25 wines open by the glass: classic regions alongside
  wines from Central and Eastern Europe and the New World, with natural wine
  too. With them you eat small dishes to share, with Japanese, Korean, Italian
  and Spanish influences. Recommended for anyone in Antwerp who wants to taste
  off the beaten track.
