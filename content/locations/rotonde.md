  wie in Rotterdam groen wil eten bij een goed glas naturelwijn.
description_en: >-
  On the Goudsesingel in Rotterdam, Roy Lammers, Jord Coree and Birk Heijkants
  opened Rotonde in December 2021. Chef Jord Coree cooks vegetarian and largely
  plant-based food over an open wood fire in the open kitchen in the middle of
  the restaurant. The vegetables come from nearby farmers and growers whom the
  chefs know personally.


  The wine is Birk Heijkants' domain: the list has only natural and biodynamic
  wines, from independent European winemakers. Rotonde is open in the evening
  only — during the day you can go to Bar Bocht, its sister next door, for
  coffee and later a glass of natural wine. Recommended for anyone in Rotterdam
  who wants to eat green with a good glass of natural wine.
