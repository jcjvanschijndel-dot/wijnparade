description_en: >-
  On the Amsteldijk in the Nieuwe Pijp, right on the water, Le Hollandais has
  been an inconspicuous but unforgettable bistro for years. Almost invisible
  from outside, a world of its own inside: on the left the bar with a mirrored
  wall, then a few steps down to the open kitchen with a table for eight, and
  upstairs a dining room with wood panelling and neatly laid tables with white
  linen. Chef Rebwar prepares every dish à la minute, from amuse to friandise,
  all home-made. A real bistro style in which you as a guest are taken along in
  the culinary process. No fixed menu: you choose the number of courses (three
  to ten) and the chef decides the rest, based on that day's seasonal produce.
  Fresh fish and organic vegetables take centre stage. Wine from every region of
  France, with special attention to small vineyards. Fair prices for the level.
