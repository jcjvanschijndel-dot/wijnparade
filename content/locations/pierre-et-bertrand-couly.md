description_en: >-
  The Couly family has been making wine in Chinon since the fifteenth century.
  Pierre and Bertrand Couly aim to show the diversity of terroirs and wines of
  the appellation, on about twenty hectares. The estate has been certified HVE
  since 2014. The winery on the edge of Chinon is strikingly modern.


  On a tour of just over an hour you see the winery and taste six Chinons. There
  are also pairings with regional produce or chocolate, an extended visit with
  the winemaker and even an escape game. Easy to combine with a visit to the
  fortress of Chinon.
