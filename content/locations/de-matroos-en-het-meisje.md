description_en: >-
  The name ("The Sailor and the Girl") refers to the past of Katendrecht, the
  district that was once the harbour area where sailors came ashore and the
  girls were there for their entertainment. That's long gone, but the name
  stayed. Eva Eekman took over the corner building on the Deliplein and turned
  it into a real neighbourhood bistro; later Taco de Vos joined her as sommelier
  and co-owner. By now the mix of neighbours and regulars from far beyond
  Rotterdam is a matter of course. Star Wine List named Taco de Vos its
  Rotterdam Ambassador. Refined but accessible: small, fine and nourishing.
  Seasonal, always with surprising combinations. The wine list is modest in size
  but carefully put together by Taco de Vos: mostly organic and natural wines
  from small artisan producers, for every taste and budget. Champagne and
  Burgundy are particularly well represented. Half glasses are always available.
  Two Gault&Millau toques.
