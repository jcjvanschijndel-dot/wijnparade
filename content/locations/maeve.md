description_en: >-
  On the Kromme Nieuwegracht, one of the quietest canals in Utrecht's old
  town, chef Tommy Janssen and host Vera Voorend run their own restaurant.
  Janssen cooks creative French food, with vegetables in the leading role and
  produce from Dutch soil; you eat a tasting menu. Maeve has a Michelin star.


  The wine list is extensive, with a strong focus on wines from the
  Netherlands and Belgium — you pass the wine cellar on the way to the
  toilets. With the menu you can take a wine pairing by the glass. Book
  directly with the restaurant. Recommended for a special dinner in Utrecht,
  a few minutes' walk from the Dom.
