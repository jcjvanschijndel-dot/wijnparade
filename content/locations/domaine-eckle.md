description_en: >-
  Katzenthal is a small wine village in a side valley of the Alsace Wine Route,
  close to Colmar. Here the Ecklé family has been making wine since 1870,
  generation after generation. The estate has 9.5 hectares of vines, including
  plots in the grand cru Wineck-Schlossberg.


  On a visit of three quarters of an hour you see the estate and taste the wines
  in a friendly atmosphere. Groups are small, so it is best to book ahead. An
  affordable and informal address for a wine tasting in Alsace.
