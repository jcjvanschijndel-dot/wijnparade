description_en: >-
  Clos des Abbesses is a family estate in Saint-Sulpice-de-Faleyrens, in the
  Saint-Émilion appellation, a few minutes' drive from the village. On about
  sixteen hectares grow merlot and cabernet franc, from vines averaging almost
  fifty years old. The estate makes some 70,000 bottles a year.


  The winemaker gives you a tour, from harvest to glass, and you then taste
  three red wines. A personal address for anyone who wants to visit a smaller
  family estate in Saint-Émilion.
