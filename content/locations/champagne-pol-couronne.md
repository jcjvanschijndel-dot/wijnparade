description_en: >-
  Pol Auguste Couronne founded his Champagne house in 1887; the year still
  appears on the label of the Brut 1887. Since 2013 Jean-Baptiste Prévost has
  run the family business. The grapes come largely from grand cru plots, and the
  house works with respect for nature and traditional know-how.


  In the centre of Reims you can taste three grand cru Champagnes, take a
  sabrage workshop or do a masterclass. A convivial address for a Champagne
  tasting in the middle of the city, within walking distance of the cathedral.
