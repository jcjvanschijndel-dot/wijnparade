  A wine estate deep in the hills of Gaiole that combines wine with contemporary
  art: since 1999 it has housed the Castello di Ama per l'Arte Contemporanea
  collection, including an impressive mirror installation by Daniel Buren that
  reflects the rolling hills. Book a wine tour and tasting, come for lunch in
  the 18th-century villa with a balcony table and breathtaking views, visit the
  sculpture park, or go to the estate's Enoteca purely for the wine. Authentic,
  affordable dishes such as pasta with wild boar sauce or chicken with black
  olives in wine sauce.
