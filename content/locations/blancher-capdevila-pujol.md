description_en: >-
  In the centre of Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, on the Plaça del Pont Romà, stands
  the Cava Blancher building. The winery was founded by Antonio Capdevila
  Pujol and his wife Teresa Blancher Solanes, and is now run by the third
  generation: brothers Josep, Ramon and Enric Carbó Planas. The cellars extend
  over several underground levels, built with the typical Catalan vault. The
  house makes only reservas and gran reservas, handling everything in-house
  from pressing the grapes to the final labelling.


  Blancher was one of the pioneers of wine tourism in Sant Sadurní. There is a
  guided tour with a film, a small museum, a shop, a wine bar and a picnic
  area. The cellar is open at weekends and on public holidays. Recommended on
  a wine trip through the Penedès.
