description_en: >-
  Mooma is a unique address in the Baix Empordà: the only Mediterranean cider
  restaurant in the world. The family has run Mas Saulot in the hamlet of
  Palau-Sator, near Pals, for six generations, with extensive apple orchards.
  Grandfather was one of the founders of the local fruit cooperative. The
  current owner transformed the family estate into a culinary and educational
  experience around the apple, inspired by the English cider tradition but with
  an entirely Mediterranean character of its own. A gastro-educational
  experience: you learn everything about what you can do with an apple, see how
  apple juice and cider are made, and then eat on the big outdoor terrace
  surrounded by apple trees. There's also a chill-out zone for cider, apple
  juice, ice cream, sorbet and apple desserts. Family-friendly and perfect for
  groups too. Always busy, so booking is essential, especially in summer.
