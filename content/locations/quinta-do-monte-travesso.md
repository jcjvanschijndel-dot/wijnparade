  Quinta do Monte Travesso is a family business in the Cima Corgo near
  Tabuaço, close to the wine village of Barcos, making wine and olive oil

  award in 2019.


  A visit offers a choice of wine and olive oil tastings, a picnic or a
  walk, with transport in a typical 1976 Douro van. Recommended for anyone
  wanting to discover the Douro in an unusual way.
