description_en: >-
  Can Gelat is a thirty-hectare organic wine estate owned by a Dutch family,
  near the picturesque villages of Selva and Moscari, at the foot of the
  Tramuntana mountains (a UNESCO World Heritage Site). The vineyard is naturally
  cooled by the mountains and the constant breeze, an advantage in the
  Mediterranean climate. A visit starts with a tour of the vineyard, where you
  learn about organic viticulture (no synthetic pesticides or herbicides, with a
  focus on soil fertility and biodiversity), followed by a tasting among the
  vines or in one of the atmospheric tasting rooms, with local tapas or a paella
  cooked on the spot if you like. By appointment only, and places are limited,
  so book early. For those who want to go all out: a multi-day "Wine Tour"
  experience in which you stay in a luxury villa on the estate, take a
  masterclass in viticulture and blending, get to work in the vineyard yourself
  (and perhaps learn to drive a tractor), and enjoy dinners cooked by a private
  chef.
