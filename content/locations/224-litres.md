description_en: >-
  Antoine, Valentin, Alexandre and Adrien welcome you from Tuesday to Saturday
  in their unusual, warm establishment right next to the Place Nationale. The
  wine cellar holds 300 references (white, red and rosé), carefully dug up from
  all over France, with an emphasis on the South-West. 224 Litres works
  exclusively with French wines and puts the wines of the South-West centre
  stage. The approach is biodynamic and natural-wine oriented. Adrien, trained
  as a sommelier in the Rhône Valley, and Antoine, a chef, combine their
  expertise in this concept. In the evening the shop becomes a bar with tapas
  and grilled dishes prepared in the kitchen of the adjoining restaurant
  Antoine-Omnivore.
