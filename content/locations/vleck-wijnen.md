description_en: >-
  On the Nachtegaalstraat, in Utrecht-Oost, is the Utrecht shop of Vleck, an
  importer of natural wine that also has shops in Amsterdam and Zwolle. The
  wines come directly from small winemakers in France and elsewhere in Europe
  who work organically or biodynamically and intervene as little as possible
  in the cellar. Natural wine does not have to be wild or funky here: alongside
  adventurous bottles there are plenty of clear, classic-tasting wines.


  Owner Arjen Miedema is happy to help you choose, and on Fridays there is often a small themed tasting. Many Dutch wine bars also pour Vleck's wines. Combine a visit with Chatobi, a few doors down the same street. Recommended for anyone in Utrecht who wants to discover natural wine with good advice.
