description_en: >-
  Chef-owner Wim Severein had already won a Michelin star at the Wereldmuseum in
  Rotterdam. When that restaurant had to close, he opened The Millèn in July
  2018 in the iconic Millennium Tower next to Rotterdam Central. Barely five
  months later the star came, one of the fastest star wins in the Netherlands.
  Wim isn't one to puff out his chest and loudly promote himself: calmly and
  with drive he works in his kitchen on the most beautiful culinary creations.
  Wim Severein's refined flavour profiles are further enhanced by the sommelier,
  who presents an excellent international wine list. Sommelier Sanne Poppen, a
  finalist in a national sommelier competition, accompanies every menu with
  carefully selected wine pairings, showing real daring: even off the beaten
  track, special combinations are made that surprise guests. Wines are available
  by the glass with every menu, and the sommelier adjusts the pairing straight
  away if you have dietary requirements or preferences. Regulars know a hidden
  secret: ask the sommelier about cheese pairings with sweet Portuguese dessert
  wines. They're not on the menu, but they're always there.
