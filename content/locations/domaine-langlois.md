description_en: >-
  In 1996 Catherine and Michel Langlois settled in Pougny, in the Nièvre, and
  took over the grandfather's 0.3 hectares of vines. Today the family estate has
  about seventeen hectares in the Pouilly-Fumé and Coteaux du Giennois
  appellations. It has been certified organic since 2019.


  On a one-hour visit you hear the family's story and taste the wines: sauvignon
  blanc from Pouilly-Fumé, wines from the Coteaux du Giennois and even a crème
  de cassis. A personal address on the eastern side of the Loire, opposite
  Sancerre.
