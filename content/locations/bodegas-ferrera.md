description_en: >-
  Bodegas Ferrera sits on Finca Las Vigas, at around a thousand metres
  altitude on a lava tongue in the Valle de Güímar. The family bought the
  estate in the 1940s and sold only bulk grapes and wine for decades, until
  the first bottles under its own name appeared in 2007. The winery has
  been led by women in the family for three generations, now Carmen Gloria
  Ferrera.


  A visit includes tasting red wines from grapes such as Tempranillo and
  Vijariego Negro, organically grown on volcanic soil. Recommended for
  anyone wanting to get to know the role of women in Canarian winemaking.
