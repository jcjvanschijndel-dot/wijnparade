description_en: >-
  Weingut Sattlerhof in Gamlitz has been run by the Sattler family since the
  late nineteenth century and farms organically. Its signature wine is the
  "Gamlitz" Sauvignon Blanc, given a brief skin maceration before
  fermentation and then aged on the fine lees. The estate includes a
  Michelin-starred restaurant, open from early May to late October.


  A visit includes tasting the wines in the estate's cellar and a visit to
  the vineyards; there are also various activities on the farm. Besides the
  winery and the starred restaurant, Sattlerhof has a more casual Farmhouse
  Bistro and the Epicurean Hotel. Recommended for anyone combining South
  Styria with a good lunch or dinner.
