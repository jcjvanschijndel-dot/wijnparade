  The panoramic terrace, shaded by pines, olive and almond trees, looks out over
  the village and vineyards of Châteauneuf-du-Pape. The view stretches to the
  Comtat Venaissin and Mont Ventoux in the east, Avignon and the Palais des
  Papes in the south, and the Rhône and the first hills of the Gard in the west.
  Beneath the restaurant is an impressive cellar, 200 m² carved out of the
  limestone rock, with more than 200 wines from domaines and châteaux of
  Châteauneuf-du-Pape and other Rhône Valley crus. Visitors are invited to go
  down themselves to choose their bottle.
