description_en: >-
  Bodegas Montecastro was founded around 2000 by a group of friends with
  a shared dream: making quality wine in the heart of Ribera del Duero.
  The 27-hectare vineyard sits at 860 to 923 metres, one of the highest
  points in the appellation. Since the early 2010s, Hacienda Monasterio
  has been the majority shareholder.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's wines, after a tour of the
  concrete fermentation tanks and the barrel cellar. Recommended for
  anyone wanting to discover Ribera del Duero at one of its
  highest-altitude sites.
