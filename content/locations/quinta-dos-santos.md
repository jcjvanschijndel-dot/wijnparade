description_en: >-
  Between the fishing villages of Carvoeiro and Ferragudo, in the Algarve, lies
  Quinta dos Santos. The dos Santos family returned to their ancestral land in
  2016 and planted new vineyards and olive trees on eight hectares, among old
  almond, carob and cork oak trees. The old irrigation channels and stone walls
  have been restored with local stone.


  Besides wine, the family also makes craft beer and gin. You can taste, take a
  tour of the winery and brewery and eat in the restaurant overlooking the
  vineyard. A convivial address for a wine tasting on the Algarve coast, close
  to Portimão.
