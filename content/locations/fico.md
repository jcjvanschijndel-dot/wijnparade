  Aan de Veilinghaven, niet ver van de Jaarbeurs, zit Fico in een opvallend

description_en: >-
  On the Veilinghaven, not far from the Jaarbeurs, Fico occupies a striking
  little tower on the water, with a terrace right on the harbour. The
  restaurant is the successor to Restaurant de Veiling and serves Italian food.
  There is no à la carte: you choose a menu of four, five or six courses.


  When it opened, Fico chose to serve only Italian wines to match the kitchen, and the wine list is broad. On summer evenings you sit outside by the water. Recommended for an Italian dinner in Utrecht, away from the bustle of the city centre.
