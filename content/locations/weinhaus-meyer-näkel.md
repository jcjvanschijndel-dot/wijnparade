description_en: >-
  In 1984 Werner Näkel did something nobody in the Ahr had done before him: he
  made high-quality dry Spätburgunder, inspired by the great Pinot Noirs of
  Burgundy. Jancis Robinson: "It would not be exaggerating to say that
  Meyer-Näkel makes some of the most outstanding Spätburgunders of Germany."
  Eichelmann called Werner "der renommierteste Winzer an der Ahr" (the most
  renowned winemaker on the Ahr). Today his daughters Meike and Dörte are at the
  helm, both winemakers and both as driven as their father. Whichever wine guide
  you open, Eichelmann, Vinum, Gault Millau or Falstaff, Meyer-Näkel is always
  at the top, not just in the Ahr but in all of Germany. At Meyer-Näkel in
  Dernau you're received by the family themselves, and father Werner regularly
  joins his daughters' tastings. After a tasting of nine wines you can walk
  through the steepest slate vineyards in the valley towards Kloster Marienthal.
  The restaurant next to the winery serves a Vesperteller (a cold platter) with
  a glass of wine by the vineyard, one of the nicer stops in the region.
