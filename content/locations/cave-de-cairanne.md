description_en: >-
  In the heart of the village of Cairanne, at the foot of the medieval village
  with views of the Dentelles de Montmirail and Mont Ventoux, stands a
  cooperative that proves that working together produces excellent wine. Founded
  in 1929, Cave de Cairanne brings together 140 winegrowers who pass their
  passion for the land down from generation to generation and work 550 hectares
  of vines. In 2024 Cave de Cairanne "Camille & Cecilia" was named Cooperative
  of the Year by La Revue du Vin de France. In the heart of the village, the
  Maison du Cairanne lets you discover almost 50 producers and taste more than
  100 different wines. There are workshops and activities all year round. During
  your visit you can also see the exhibition in the Museum of Roman heritage:
  Roman remains found in the vineyards of Cairanne, witnesses to its rich
  history.
