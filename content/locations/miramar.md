description_en: >-
  Paco Pérez cooks in Llançà right on the promenade with a view of the sea:
  avant-garde cooking that takes the fishing traditions of the Costa Brava as
  its starting point. Pérez also runs the restaurant at Celler Peralada and has
  a star for his Miramar Berlin. Seafood, fish and shellfish from the Costa
  Brava are the common thread: lobster, sea urchin, anchovies from Cadaqués. The
  wine list combines the best local Empordà producers (Clos d'Agon and Mas
  Llunes are close by) with international top names. Llançà is close to Cap de
  Creus, the northernmost point of the Empordà and home to the strongest
  tramontana wind.
