description_en: >-
  Voramar is a small, carefully kept restaurant by the sea, run entirely by the
  Jamàs i Garbí family. Portbou is the last town on the Costa Brava before you
  cross the border to the French Côte Vermeille: a border town where the trains
  stop and most visitors don't stay long. That makes Voramar a special
  destination: a restaurant that has brought a Michelin star to a place without
  any fine dining infrastructure around it. Everything revolves around two
  unique tasting menus, Petit Voramar and Gran Voramar, based on a contemporary
  version of traditional cooking, both starting with an amuse section called
  Vora. The kitchen, in the hands of young chefs Guillem Gavilán and Pau Jamàs,
  shows enthusiasm, technical expertise and creativity, with seasonal produce
  and dishes that combine meat with fish or seafood, such as Cap i Pota veal
  with Mediterranean red tuna. The wine list has more than 250 references,
  carefully chosen to complement each dish.
