description_en: >-
  Bart Broshuis had already run around 28 hospitality businesses in Utrecht
  and Zeist before he opened The Winetable on the Biltstraat in February
  2025, together with his wife Jacqueline Fagel and chef Pascal Muller. The
  wine list is large, with many wines by the glass, and anyone wanting to
  look beyond the list can ask for Bart's booklet, with even more wines
  that aren't on it.


  You can drop in for a glass with a bite, or for a full dinner of dishes
  to share, such as oysters, tuna sashimi, Thai prawns or beef tenderloin
  with truffle risotto. With room for around eighty guests, it's an
  approachable address in Utrecht for combining wine and food.
