description_en: >-
  De 3 Wijntjes started out as a pop-up wine bar in the former café De 3
  Weesgegroetjes in Roosendaal; the name is a nod to the nickname many locals
  had for that café: the 3W. Founders Sebastiaan Waegemaekers and Rick Goris
  closed that location at the end of 2025, but they couldn't stay away.


  Since September 2026 the wine bar has been back, now at Eetboetiek Festina Lente. Note that De 3 Wijntjes is only open on the third Friday and Saturday of each month, so check the dates before you go. A fun spot for a glass of wine in Roosendaal.
