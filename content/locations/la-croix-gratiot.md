description_en: >-
  Between the Bassin de Thau and the pine-covered hills of the Hérault lies La
  Croix Gratiot, a domaine that transformed in one generation from a cooperative
  grape supplier into one of the most dynamic organic wine businesses in the
  Languedoc. The domaine welcomes visitors all year round for tastings, guided
  tours, a boutique and a reception room. Its proximity to the Bassin de Thau,
  ideal for oysters and Picpoul, makes a visit extra appealing. In summer there
  are concerts, exhibitions and themed evenings on the estate. The flagships are
  the Picpoul de Pinet Brechallume (citrus-fresh and mineral, with kumquat and
  ginger) and the Chant des Dolia range in white (Roussanne, full and generous).
