description_en: >-
  On the hills near Panzano, in Chianti Classico, lies Tenuta Casenuove. The old
  estate was bought in 2015 by Frenchman Philippe Austruy, who also owns wine
  estates elsewhere in Europe. Since then the vineyards have been renewed and
  converted to organic farming. The main grape is sangiovese, from which Chianti
  Classico, riserva and Gran Selezione are made.


  On a private tour you hear about the history of the old winery and see how it
  has been modernised, followed by a tasting; there is also a version with a
  light lunch. A lovely stop on a wine route through Chianti, between Greve and
  Panzano.
