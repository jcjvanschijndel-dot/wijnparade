description_en: >-
  Located on Calle Álamos in the historic centre, just around the corner from
  the Museo del Vidrio y Cristal. This elegant yet traditional wine bar has one
  of the most interesting wine cellars in the province, with national and
  international wines and a particular focus on Málaga wines and natural wines.


  In the kitchen, Álvaro Ávila offers a casual sharing menu, prepared with seasonal ingredients and designed to complement each glass of wine. There is always a new wine to discover, which encourages you to ask for advice and try something different. Tuna tataki, pork, classic Andalusian tapas.


  Most restaurants in Málaga stick to the well-known Rioja and Ribera labels — this is a top address for discovering something new!
