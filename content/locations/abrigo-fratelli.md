description_en: >-
  In Diano d'Alba, on the hills between Alba and the Barolo villages, lies the
  family business Abrigo Fratelli. It was founded in 1935 and is still run by
  the family, now by father Ernesto and son Walter. The estate's own vineyards
  produce the classic wines of the Langhe: Dolcetto di Diano d'Alba, Barbera,
  Nebbiolo and Barolo Ravera.


  On a visit you walk through the vineyard and the cellar. This is followed by a
  tasting of five wines, or a vertical tasting of Barolo Ravera with cured
  meats, cheese and hazelnuts from the estate. A welcoming address for a wine
  tasting in the Langhe.
