description_en: >-
  Mes Amis is the most remarkable wine restaurant in Maastricht, in two forms.
  The permanent restaurant in Maastricht, run by hostess Annaline and her son
  Patriek Doelen, cooks to the wine: the Limburg and international wines
  determine the menu, not the other way round. In addition, Mes Amis runs the
  "Wijnrestaurant op het Land" every year, a pop-up set up on a new Limburg wine
  estate each year; by tradition, the pop-up stays in the same place for at most
  two years. Refined dishes with local produce from regional suppliers, always
  matched to the Limburg wine list. The wine list focuses on South Limburg
  wines, supplemented with international selections.
