description_en: >-
  On the Schermersstraat, between the Meir and the Sint-Jacob quarter, a group
  of friends opened this natural wine shop, with a small bar too. The shelves
  hold more than 400 bottles, all made naturally or biodynamically. The owners
  import some of them themselves through their company Trubel, alongside wines
  from other natural winemakers.


  You can taste before you buy, or have a glass at the bar. Besides wine they sell cider and alcohol-free drinks. Recommended for anyone in Antwerp who wants to discover natural wine with advice from people who know the makers personally.
