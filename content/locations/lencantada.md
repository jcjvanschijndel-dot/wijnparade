description_en: >-
  L'Encantada is not a wine house but an Armagnac house, founded in 2011 by a
  group of friends in Vic-Fezensac, in the Gers. The house selects Armagnacs
  from small producers and bottles them in limited editions, often at cask
  strength. Since 2023 it has also been distilling its own, with a direct-fired
  still from the 1920s.


  The three-quarter-hour introduction includes three Armagnacs; "the keys of the
  cellar" includes five. There is also a workshop in which you put together your
  own blend. A special address for Armagnac lovers, between Auch and Condom.
