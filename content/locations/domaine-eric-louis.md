description_en: >-
  In Thauvenay, south-east of Sancerre, the Louis family has been growing
  sauvignon blanc and pinot noir for four generations. The estate has about 35
  hectares and makes Sancerre and wines from neighbouring appellations. The
  tasting cellar is called Les Celliers de la Pauline.


  On a tour of the winery and the barrel cellar you taste five wines, served
  with crottin de Chavignol. You can combine the visit with a nearby goat farm,
  or choose a trip through the terroirs of Sancerre with a tasting in the
  vineyard. A welcoming family address in the Sancerrois.
