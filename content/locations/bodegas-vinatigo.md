  druiven.
description_en: >-
  Juan Jesús Méndez started Viñátigo in 1990 with century-old vines in
  the Cabo Verde vineyard, making his first wines in the old cellar of
  his grandparents' house in La Guancha. The winery is considered a
  benchmark in reviving native Canarian grapes such as Gual, Marmajuelo
  and Malvasía.


  A visit includes tasting wines from rare Canarian grape varieties.
  Recommended for anyone wanting to look beyond the well-known Canarian
  grapes.
