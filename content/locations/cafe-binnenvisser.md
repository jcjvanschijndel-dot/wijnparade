description_en: >-
  In a former local pub on the Bilderdijkstraat in Amsterdam West stands this
  characterful corner building with leaded windows, creaky wooden floorboards
  and windows that open fully in summer. The three owners come from the music
  world: old-school hip hop, soul and funk play in the background, and at the
  weekend a party is guaranteed. The menu changes weekly and is entirely based
  on what's good that week: creative, seasonal dishes with plenty of vegetarian
  options, colourfully plated and with attention to detail. The wine list is
  carefully put together: natural wine dominates, but more classic choices are
  there too. Dining and chatting at the long bar is the recommended way to
  experience this café.
