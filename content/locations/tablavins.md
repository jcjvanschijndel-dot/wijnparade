  The restaurant is lined with wine and books. You can choose between a table in
  the library room or closer to the open kitchen. Every day Geoffrey uncorks a
  "bottle of the day" that he pours by the glass only then: it might be an
  organic wine from a small new producer or an established name from a
  particularly good year. Besides wines from Burgundy and Italy, Geoffrey is a
  big fan of the Loire, Rhône and Jura. More than thirty wines by the glass,
  with half glasses available.
