description_en: >-
  The house of Baur was founded in 1741 in Turckheim, a walled town at the
  entrance to the Munster valley. Pierre and Thomas Baur run the estate of just
  over twelve hectares, which has been farmed biodynamically for decades. A
  large part of the vineyards lies on the granite south-facing slope of the
  grand cru Brand.


  On a one-hour visit you see the cellar and taste the wines, with the riesling
  and gewurztraminer from Brand as the highlight. Recommended for lovers of
  biodynamic Alsace wine, ten minutes from Colmar.
