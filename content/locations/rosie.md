description_en: >-
  On the Lucasbolwerk, along the canal on the east side of the city centre, chef
  Jac Rijks and host Yora Galjaard opened this restobar in October 2022. Rijks
  cooks creatively and without fuss, with international influences, such as pork
  belly with chilli jam.


  Rosie is half restaurant, half bar: you can have a full meal, or just a glass
  at the bar with homemade bitterballen, charcuterie or oysters. The wine list
  is extensive, with plenty of natural wine. Book for a table, as it is often
  full. Recommended for anyone in Utrecht who wants to eat well with a glass of
  natural wine.
