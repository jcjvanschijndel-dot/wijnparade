description_en: >-
  A few minutes' walk from the Louvre, in an eighteenth-century mansion, lie Les
  Caves du Louvre. This was the home of Trudon, sommelier to Louis XV; the six
  hundred square metres of cellars served as the royal wine cellar.


  Today it is a visitor centre about French wine. On the tour you discover the
  terroirs, the aromas and the making of wine with all your senses, and then
  taste three wines with a sommelier. There is also a workshop in which you
  blend your own wine. A fun, accessible introduction to French wine, in the
  middle of Paris.
