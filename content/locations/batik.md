description_en: >-
  Batik is on the fourth floor of the Alcazaba Premium Hostel, with a view of
  the Alcazaba. Chef Iván Bravo's kitchen blends Mediterranean cooking with
  influences from around the world. Examples are prawns with curry and tandoori,
  and "VietnAndaluces" spring rolls. After dinner you can head up to the rooftop
  terrace for a cocktail with a view of the fortress.


  The wine list is broad, with plenty available by the glass, and the wines suit the mixed cuisine. Guests are divided about the service and the wine selection. The location and the view, however, consistently score well.
