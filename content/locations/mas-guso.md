description_en: >-
  Mas Gusó is a unique food and wine estate in a 16th-century masia in Sant Pere
  Pescador, close to the Aiguamolls de l'Empordà Natural Park and the beaches of
  the Costa Brava. Since 1990 the restaurant has cooked with fresh, local and
  seasonal produce. The estate combines four unique spaces. The restaurant has
  several outdoor terraces: the Mistral (in front of the masia), the Plataner
  (in the shade of a 200-year-old plane tree), the Tramuntana and the MiraGe
  terrace. The miraGe Cocktail Bar is an oasis within the estate, where a
  professional bartender makes exclusive seasonal cocktails with the latest
  techniques, with live music in summer. The Vinoteca offers more than 500
  wines, tastings with temperature-controlled self-service machines, a wine bar
  by the glass and a separate tasting room for groups or meetings. The Mas Gusó
  Market sells local products and the estate's own wines, cava and extra virgin
  olive oil.
