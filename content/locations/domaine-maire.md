description_en: >-
  The Maire family has been linked to winemaking in the Jura for
  centuries, and Henri Maire built the estate into one of the
  region's largest in the 1960s. Today, under the Boisset umbrella,
  the estate's heart sits right in the centre of Arbois, the village
  where Louis Pasteur carried out his research into fermentation.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's wines at the boutique in the
  centre of Arbois, including the region's characteristic Trousseau
  and Vin Jaune. Recommended for anyone wanting to get to know the
  Jura's unique wines, such as the oxidative Vin Jaune, right in the
  heart of Arbois.
