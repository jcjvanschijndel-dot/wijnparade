  ROUGE is the gastronomic restaurant of the Margaret - Hôtel Chouleur in the

  and velvet in shades of carmine and terracotta, and in summer also on the
  terrace by candlelight under the stars. Besides ROUGE, the hotel has a second,
  more informal brasserie: GIGI. The wine list of passionate sommelier Julian
  Rebollar focuses on local and regional wines (Côtes Catalanes, Tavel,
  Languedoc), supplemented with international names.
