description_en: >-
  In Villars-Fontaine, four kilometres west of Nuits-Saint-Georges, lies Domaine
  de Montmain, founded in 1970 by Bernard Hudelot. It is one of the rare estates
  in the Hautes-Côtes de Nuits still making wines for long keeping in the purest
  Burgundian tradition: rigorously vinified and aged for a long time in oak
  barrels, so that they can be kept for twenty years and more.


  What is special is that you can taste and buy older vintages here, going back
  to 1989. On a visit you see the cellar and taste a range of wines. A
  surprising address twenty minutes from Beaune and half an hour from Dijon.
