description_en: >-
  In autumn 2025 Restaurant Vermeer opened an accessible wine room next to the
  restaurant in the NH Collection Amsterdam Barbizon Palace, the successor to
  the former Bottles & Jars bistro. The same team and the same quality, but
  without the formality of a full dinner. Sommelier Dannis Apeldoorn curates a
  changing selection of wines by the glass from Vermeer's cellar; some bottles
  are one-offs, and when they're gone, they're gone. Each bite comes with two
  wine suggestions, one under €10 and one more expensive, so you can pair wine
  and food at your own level. Small, refined bites from Vermeer's kitchen: Dutch
  shrimps on brioche, pork terrine, seasonal creations. A High Wine arrangement
  is also possible. A calm, intimate, loungey interior with soft lighting.
