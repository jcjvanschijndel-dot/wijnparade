description_en: >-
  At the foot of Mont Brouilly, in Saint-Lager, the Jambon family has been
  making wine for five generations at the Domaine des Maisons Neuves. The house
  makes seven Beaujolais crus, all from gamay, with Brouilly and Côte de
  Brouilly as its home base.


  The owners, Emmanuel and Dominique, welcome you to the cellar themselves. On a
  visit of an hour and a half you taste six wines, served with a mâchon: the
  traditional farmhouse meal of Beaujolais. A warm family address for a wine
