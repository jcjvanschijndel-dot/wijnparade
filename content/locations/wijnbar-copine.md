description_en: >-
  At the foot of the Grote Kerk, Wijnbar Copine occupies one of the oldest
  buildings in Breda: the former home of the church's sexton. Sisters Simone and

  luxurious bites.


  Since the summer of 2025 there has also been a terrace on the Kerkplein, looking out over the church; it can't be reserved. Inside it is often busy at weekends, so booking then is a good idea. Recommended for a glass of wine in the centre of Breda.
