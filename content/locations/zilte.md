  Op de bovenste verdieping van het MAS, op het Eilandje, kijk je bij Zilte uit

description_en: >-
  On the top floor of the MAS museum, on the Eilandje, Zilte looks out over the
  port and the city. Self-taught chef Viki Geunes started in Mol, where he won
  two Michelin stars; since moving to the MAS, Zilte has held three. His
  cooking is refined and precise, with great attention to flavour.


  The wine is in the hands of sommelier Aaron Moeraert, the partner of Geunes' daughter Gitte. Instead of a cellar there is a wine attic you can see from your table. Moeraert pours several wines by the glass with each dish, so you can choose for yourself, and there is also a wine pairing. Book well ahead; Zilte is closed at weekends. Recommended for a special occasion on a visit to Antwerp.
