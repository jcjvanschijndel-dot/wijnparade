  Arcs.
description_en: >-
  Château de Saint Martin in Taradeau has belonged to the same family since
  1740, now for eleven generations. It is a cru classé of the Côtes de Provence.
  On the estate you find the remains of a Gallo-Roman villa, vaulted underground
  cellars from the Middle Ages, a rarity in Provence, and an eighteenth-century
  château. It also has its own distillery.


  On a visit you see a film about the history of the château and the old
  cellars, and taste the wines, optionally four crus classés with a platter of
  four local cheeses. Recommended in the Var, between Draguignan and Les Arcs.
