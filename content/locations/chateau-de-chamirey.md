description_en: >-
  Château de Chamirey is the best-known estate in Mercurey, in the Côte
  Chalonnaise. It belongs to the Devillard family, now with Amaury and Aurore as
  the fifth generation. Its 37 hectares of vines are all in Mercurey, fifteen of
  them premier cru, with vineyards such as Clos du Roi, Les Ruelles and Champs
  Martin.


  You taste in the shop by the eighteenth-century château, which also stocks the
  wines of the family's other estates, from the Côte de Nuits to the Mâconnais.
  A highlight is the 4x4 trip through the vineyards, rounded off with a tasting
  of five wines. Recommended between Beaune and Chalon-sur-Saône.
