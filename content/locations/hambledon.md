description_en: >-
  In 1952 Major-General Sir Guy Salisbury-Jones planted vines on the chalk
  slopes of the South Downs in Hampshire, creating England's first commercial
  vineyard. Seventy years later, in the care of Berry Bros. & Rudd and the
  Symington Family Estates (the Portuguese port family behind Graham's and
  Dow's), the house still runs on the same pioneering spirit and the same chalk
  terroir. The cellar door and restaurant opened in 2025 in the South Downs
  National Park, a short drive from London, Winchester and the whole of the
  South East. Visits include guided vineyard and winery tours, tastings of
  award-winning sparkling wines, and the special Vineyard Safari Tour, an
  extended behind-the-scenes experience including lunch. Special events change
  through the season: Fire Feast with chef Luke Holder of Hartnett, Holder & Co
  (Lime Wood), Jazz & Fizz evenings, Seven Seas dinners and WSET wine courses.
  Walking routes through the vineyard are available. The restaurant (listed in
  the Michelin Guide) overlooks the vineyard and the South Downs. The kitchen
  works with seasonal produce from Hampshire and pairs it exclusively with the
  estate's wines.
