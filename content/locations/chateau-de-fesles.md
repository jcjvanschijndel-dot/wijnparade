description_en: >-
  Château de Fesles is one of the best-known wine estates in Anjou. It lies in
  Thouarcé, in the heart of the small Bonnezeaux appellation, on one of the
  highest points of the Loire Valley, with a sweeping view over the vineyards.
  The estate is certified organic.


  The star is chenin blanc, used here to make the sweet Bonnezeaux. The estate
  also makes dry Anjou blanc, red and rosé Anjou and crémant de Loire. You can
  taste freely; guided tours are available on request. A lovely address for
  lovers of chenin and sweet Loire wine, south of Angers.
