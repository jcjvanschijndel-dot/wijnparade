description_en: >-
  Julien Maillet turned a former betting shop in the Belleville neighbourhood
  into one of the coolest bars in Paris. A concrete floor, sanded walls,
  industrial furniture, neon light from the Chinese restaurants outside, and 24
  pages of liquids on the menu. Popular with 20- to 40-year-olds and wine and
  beer professionals who come here on their day off. Recognised by Star Wine
  List. 400+ natural wines and 200+ craft beers: a combination you won't find
  anywhere else in Paris done so consistently. A whole wall of taps, with
  natural wine and craft beer on draught. Julien deliberately works with low
  prices, rare in Paris: Riesling from Les Vins Pirouettes on tap for €4.50,
  Gamay from Jean Maupertuis for €29 a bottle, Sauvignon from François Blanchard
  for €34. Beers: IPA from Mont Salève, Cantillon gueuze, Citra pils from
  Popihn. Bites from close by: Taggiasca olives, cooked Aveyron sausage,
  finocchiona, sardines. There's a separate Liquiderie Cave wine and beer shop
  within walking distance in Oberkampf.
