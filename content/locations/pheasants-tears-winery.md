description_en: >-
  The most accessible address for wine tourism in Georgia. Founded by American
  artist John Wurdeman and Georgian winemaker Gela Patalishvili, in the
  picturesque UNESCO town of Sighnaghi in the Kakheti region. Pheasant's Tears
  combines authenticity with hospitality: a restaurant, a wine bar and a winery
  in the heart of Georgia's most visited wine region. The restaurant serves
  traditional Georgian cooking, khinkali (dumplings), lobiani (bean bread),
  grilled meat and pkhali (vegetable dishes), all designed to go with their own
  wines. The restaurant is open daily for lunch and dinner, the wine bar is also
  open to walk-ins, and the winery can be visited in season. Sighnaghi itself is
  well worth it: small cobbled lanes and panoramic views over the Alazani valley
  and the Caucasus.
