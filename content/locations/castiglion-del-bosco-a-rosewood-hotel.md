description_en: >-
  A 900-year-old, 2,000-hectare Tuscan estate in the UNESCO-protected Val
  d'Orcia Natural Park, within walking distance of Montalcino. Castiglion del
  Bosco is a unique combination: one of the largest private estates in Tuscany,
  an elegant Tuscan home-away-from-home with 42 suites and 11 exclusive villas,
  a culinary destination, an exclusive private golf club and a leading Brunello
  di Montalcino winery. Three Michelin Keys. Rosewood took it over and preserved
  the strong foundations of the place, without trading refinement for
  flashiness. The winery is the fifth-largest producer of Brunello di
  Montalcino: a guided tour is a must, and so is a tasting. Wine tasting
  seminars and tours of the historic vineyards, truffle hunting followed by a
  special Brunello tasting, a helicopter flight over the Val d'Orcia and Val di
  Chiana, or a 4x4 adventure through the countryside. The Culinary Academy at La
  Canonica offers cooking classes in fresh pasta, authentic pizza and
  traditional three-course menus. Two restaurants: Osteria La Canonica (relaxed,
  light Italian, all day from 12.00: pasta, pizza, salads, cheese, cured meats)
  and Ristorante Campo del Drago (Michelin-starred, rich Tuscan classics with
  ingredients from its own gardens, in the evening). The Lobby Bar, with an
  authentic Tuscan open fireplace, serves stuzzichini, afternoon tea and
  award-winning Brunello di Montalcino wines.
