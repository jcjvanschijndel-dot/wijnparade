description_en: >-
  Graham's Lodge has been home to the port house of the same name in Vila Nova
  de Gaia, across the Douro from Porto, since 1890. Vinum Restaurant & Wine Bar
  is at the top of the lodge, in a glass pavilion with panoramic views over the
  Douro river and the historic centre of Porto. The most complete visit combines
  a tour of the lodge with a port tasting, followed by lunch or dinner at Vinum.
  The most exclusive option is the Boardroom Tasting, a private tour including
  access to the house's directors' room, with rare ports by the glass. A special
  feature of Vinum is that you can order a 20- or 40-year-old Tawny as dessert.
  Vinum brings together the best dishes of the Douro, Trás-os-Montes, the Minho
  and the Atlantic coast in one menu. The kitchen works on "Portuguese cooking
  with a twist": classic dishes such as bacalhau and octopus prepared in a
  contemporary way, with international influences. There are several menu
  options: tapas and pintxos on the terrace, a lunch option, an extended Vinum
  menu with wine pairing, and an à la carte menu for a full meal.
