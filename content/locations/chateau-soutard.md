description_en: >-
  Château Soutard is one of the oldest estates in the region: a timeless journey
  combining history and modernity in the heart of the French art of living. The
  château itself is a listed French Historic Monument and stands on one of the
  highest points of the Saint-Émilion plateau, overlooking the village and the
  vineyards. Where many châteaux in Saint-Émilion focus solely on the wine,
  Soutard has built a whole ecosystem for visitors, from picnics to vertical
  tastings, from bikes to a guest house. The guest house at Château Soutard is
  also a listed French Historic Monument, and staying the night gives you access
  to the estate outside opening hours.
