description_en: >-
  On the Nachtegaalstraat, in Utrecht-Oost, Chatobi has had a permanent shop
  since September 2025. TV maker Tobias Camman started Chatobi as an online
  shop, opened a pop-up on the Twijnstraat at the end of 2024 and now runs the
  business with Marcel Swaghoven and Pim Rongen. Swaghoven brings a large
  network from the hospitality and wine world.


  The wines come from small wineries that Camman visits himself: only when he has tasted a wine on site and likes it does it make it into the shop. Recommended for anyone in Utrecht looking for wine from small producers whom the owners know personally.
