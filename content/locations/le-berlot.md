description_en: >-
  On the central square of Montlouis-sur-Loire, in a lovely building with tufa
  walls and a terrace, Patricia and Hervé Chardonneau, former owners of the
  much-loved Casse-Cailloux in Tours, run a bistro and wine bar that gets
  everything right. On one side is the restaurant (tufa walls, wood and steel
  furniture), where Hervé makes honest bistro food: focaccia with pastrami and
  pickles, rare Angus bavette with home-made fries and béarnaise, an apricot
  trifle to finish. On the other side is the wine bar, where you can also just
  walk in for a glass, order the restaurant's starters as tapas and discover
  local winegrowers through Hervé's personal picks.
