description_en: >-
  G-Wine is a neighbourhood wine bar with a restaurant, right next to the San
  Pedro Boulevard and close to the beach. The idea is simple: even the everyday
  wines have to be good. The kitchen and bar are open seven days a week, with no
  break between lunch and dinner.


  More than 25 wines by the glass. Guests describe the list as extensive and the prices as fair. One guest, himself a trained sommelier, was surprised by the choice. There are also organic wines and a small wine shop. Because so many wines are available by the glass, it's easy to try plenty of different ones.
