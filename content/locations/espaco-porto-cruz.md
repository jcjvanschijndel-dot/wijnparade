  Aan de oever van de Douro in Vila Nova de Gaia, tegenover Porto, staat Espaço

  een portproeverij in Porto, midden tussen de portkelders van Gaia.
description_en: >-
  On the bank of the Douro in Vila Nova de Gaia, opposite Porto, stands Espaço
  Porto Cruz. The home of the port brand Porto Cruz is entirely devoted to port
  culture, in a modern form, with changing exhibitions on art, fashion and food.


  On the ground floor you taste port by the glass or as a flight, and there are
  guided tastings. There is also a restaurant and, on top, a roof terrace with a
  bar and views over the river and the old town. An easy address for a port
  tasting in Porto, right among the port lodges of Gaia.
