description_en: >-
  In the hamlet of Biniagual, one of the smallest and best-preserved authentic
  Mallorcan settlements, consisting of just 14 houses, a chapel and extensive
  farmland, lies Finca Biniagual. After the phylloxera crisis devastated the
  island's vineyards, new vines were planted here in 1999. The Vinoteca
  Biniagual is the heart of the visit: an atmospheric tasting room on the finca,
  open all year round including weekends. You can walk in without a reservation
  for a glass à la carte, or book a two-hour guided tour of the vineyard, bodega
  and vinoteca. Sommelier Luis is consistently praised for his knowledge,
  charisma and professionalism, and visitors regularly call the visit
  "unforgettable." You can also buy wine directly at cellar-door prices, or have
  it delivered home after your visit.
