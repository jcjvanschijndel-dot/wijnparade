description_en: >-
  Wijnhoeve De Colonjes in Groesbeek is the largest organic vineyard in the
  Netherlands, with thirteen hectares spread across three south-facing
  slopes on the edge of the village. Freek Verhoeven founded the business
  in 2001; Adam Dijkstra has been the winemaker since 2011. The vineyard
  grows ten grape varieties, including Regent and Muscaris.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's organic wines. Recommended for
  anyone wanting to discover organic winemaking in Gelderland.
