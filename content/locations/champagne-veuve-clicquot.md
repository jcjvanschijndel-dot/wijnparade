description_en: >-
  Veuve Clicquot, founded in 1772, is one of the largest and best-known
  Champagne houses. The widow Barbe-Nicole Clicquot built the house up; she
  devised remuage, the turning of bottles in riddling racks, which made
  Champagne clear. Beneath the Saint-Nicaise hill in Reims lie the house's chalk
  quarries, the crayères, which are on the UNESCO World Heritage List.


  A tour takes you through the cool, deep cellars, explaining the history of the
  house and how Champagne is made, and ends with a tasting. Expect stairs and
  uneven ground. A classic for anyone visiting Reims; book well in advance.
