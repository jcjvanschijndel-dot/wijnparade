description_en: >-
  Maison Brédif was founded in 1893 in Rochecorbon, on the Loire near Vouvray.
  In 1980 Baron Patrick de Ladoucette took over the house and gave it new life.
  The wines age in impressive cave cellars, carved deep into the limestone,
  which also hold very old bottles of Vouvray.


  On a tour of the cellars you hear about the history of the house and how the
  wine is made. You then taste five wines in the Pavillon Fines Bulles,
  optionally with cheese and cured meats, or on the roof terrace overlooking the
  Loire. A classic address for Vouvray, ten minutes from Tours.
