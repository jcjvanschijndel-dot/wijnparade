description_en: >-
  At the foot of the ruined castle of Crussol, in Saint-Péray opposite Valence,
  lies Domaine Rémy Nodin. The Nodin family has owned the Domaine de La Beylesse
  since 1910; it was once a fortified farmhouse from the twelfth century. Rémy,
  the fourth generation, started at the age of twenty-two with one hectare of
  Saint-Péray.


  Today he makes wine in four northern Rhône appellations: Saint-Péray, still
  and sparkling, Cornas, Crozes-Hermitage and Saint-Joseph. You can visit the
  cellar and taste six wines, or join a walk to the La Beylesse hillside.
  Recommended for lovers of the northern Rhône.
