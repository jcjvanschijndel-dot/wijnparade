  The region's oldest bodega (founded in 1858) wanted its new building to make a
  statement nobody could top, and brought in Frank Gehry, the architect of the
  Guggenheim Bilbao. The result: an undulating roof of titanium sheets in pink,
  gold and silver above the traditional 19th-century winery, now one of the most
  photographed buildings in Spain. A tour covers both the historic cellars and
  the futuristic new complex, which also houses a luxury hotel and restaurant.
  The village of Elciego itself, with its narrow lanes, makes a lovely contrast
  with the ultra-modern building.
