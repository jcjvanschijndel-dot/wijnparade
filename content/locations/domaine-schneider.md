description_en: >-
  In Gertwiller, the gingerbread village near Barr, lies the Schneider family
  estate, founded in 1958. The third generation now runs it and has been
  bottling and selling the wines directly for some years. The estate farms
  organically and makes the classic Alsace wines: sylvaner, riesling, pinot
  gris, pinot blanc, gewurztraminer, muscat and pinot noir.


  It is known for the winegrower's "casse-croûte": two hours at the table with a
  tasting and a platter of cured meats, local cheeses and pretzels. There is
  also a shorter visit with four wines. The estate has a holiday rental. A warm
  address half an hour from Strasbourg.
