description_en: >-
  In Monthelon, on the Coteaux Sud d'Épernay, Julien Chopin makes Champagne and
  ratafia, the region's traditional fortified wine. The family estate takes a
  playful approach to wine tourism: with workshops on blending and sabrage,
  seasonal workshops during ripening and harvest, and even an escape room in the
  cellars.


  The tasting with an aroma game includes three Champagnes and two ratafias. You
  can also opt for a board of cheese and cured meats or a lunch with Champagne.
  A fun address for a Champagne tasting with friends or family, ten minutes from
  Épernay.
