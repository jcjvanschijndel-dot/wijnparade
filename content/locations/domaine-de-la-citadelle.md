description_en: >-
  At the foot of Ménerbes, on the northern slopes of the Luberon, the estate
  covers more than 40 hectares of organically farmed vineyards across four
  terroirs with 15 grape varieties. But the wine is just one of the reasons to
  come. The complex includes a contemporary tasting room, a botanical garden, a
  corkscrew museum, a gift shop full of wine accessories and two restaurants.
  The Musée du Tire-Bouchon shows more than 1,200 corkscrews from the
  seventeenth century to the present day: unexpectedly fascinating, even if
  you're not a collector. The estate's gastronomic restaurant is La Table
  Agricole Tayac: chef Nicolas Tayac works the edible plants from the botanical
  garden into a daily changing menu, with local farmers as suppliers and a view
  of the vineyards. Nicolas and Margaux work on their own: only 20 seats, open
  Monday to Friday from 11.30 to 14.00. Booking well in advance is essential.
