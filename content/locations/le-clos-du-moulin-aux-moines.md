Op de laatste zaterdag van mei is er jaarlijks een open dag met live muziek, wijnproeverij en hapjes. Overnachten in één van de gastenverblijven op het domein is mogelijk — bijzonder voor wie langer wil blijven.

description_en: >-
  In 962 the Cluniac monks of the abbey of Cluny planted their first vines here,
  more than a thousand years ago. The domaine in Auxey-Duresses, a quiet village
  five minutes' drive from Beaune, has remained in one piece ever since. The
  iconic windmill towering above the vineyards is the emblem of the house.

  16 hectares spread over three monopole plots: the Clos du Moulin aux Moines (3
  ha of Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, Auxey-Duresses appellation), the Clos de la
  Perrière (3 ha in Corpeau, south-facing, Bourgogne appellation) and a third
  clos surrounded by an old stone wall. Owned exclusively by the domaine, which
  is a rarity in Burgundy. All wines are organic-biodynamic, fermented with wild
  yeasts, undisturbed and unfiltered.


  Tour and tasting by appointment in the historic cellar. In summer (July and
  August) the restaurant L'Atelier Escoffier opens in the courtyard, ideal for
  lunch or dinner surrounded by the vineyard. Every year on the last Saturday of
  May there is an open day with live music, wine tasting and snacks. You can
  also stay in one of the guest rooms on the domaine, which is special if you
  want to stay longer.
