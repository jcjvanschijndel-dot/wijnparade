Het sommeliersteam kiest daar wijnen die het ritme van de seizoenen volgen, met hapjes en gerechten uit dezelfde keuken die met de wijnen meewisselen. Reserveren kan, maar binnenlopen ook. description_en: >-
  In the listed Polman's Huis on the Keistraat, close to the Janskerkhof, Hemel
  & Aarde opened in 2021 — under the same roof as the hotel The Nox. The kitchen
  follows the seasons, with fermentation as a common thread. Executive chef René
  van der Weijden previously cooked at Karel V in Utrecht. The restaurant is
  listed in the Michelin Guide.


  Next to the restaurant is Wijnbar Hemel & Aarde, its younger sibling. There the sommelier team chooses wines that follow the rhythm of the seasons, with snacks and dishes from the same kitchen that change along with the wines. You can book, but walking in is fine too. Recommended for a full dinner or just a few glasses of wine at the bar, in the heart of Utrecht's city centre.
