description_en: >-
  Marc and Eedsje Huynen's passion for wine grew out of tasting wines, wine
  trips and wine studies, so strongly that in 2016 they decided to plant a small
  vineyard of 325 vines in their back garden. Their first wine, Santé Gertrude
  Pinot Blanc, followed in 2018. The estate has since grown to 7 hectares with
  11 different grape varieties, spread over south-east-facing slopes in the
  municipality of Eijsden-Margraten: Sint Geertruid, Mheer, Banholt, Eckelrade
  and Mesch, the last of which runs seamlessly into the Belgian Voeren area. The
  Pinot Noir won a silver medal at the Mundus Vini Grand International Wine
  Award two years in a row. The tasting room looks straight out onto the
  stainless steel tanks and wooden barrels, so you literally drink the wine
  where it's made. Besides tastings there are vineyard tours, and the estate
  also hosts events, meetings and weddings. Het Wijnstekje d'Hunes is a holiday
  home for 8 people on the estate, and guests get 10% off the tasting. With the
  tasting: fresh rolls for lunch, sharing boards and changing bites.
