description_en: >-
  In 1964 Pedro Arregui opened Elkano in the fishing village of Getaria,
  becoming a pioneer in grilling whole fish to perfection over charcoal, without
  any manipulation of the fish and with the skin on to preserve its natural
  flavours. After his death in 2014 his son Aitor took up the torch. Elkano is
  now one of the culinary cathedrals to which the world's best chefs make a
  pilgrimage, and at the same time a place deeply rooted in the daily reality of
  the Basque fishermen and their catch. In 2025 Elkano was number 24 on The
  World's 50 Best Restaurants. The absolute centrepiece of the menu is the
  turbot, grilled over charcoal and served with reverence for every part of the
  fish: the tender fillet, the rich, collagen-filled neck, even the bones. Other
  signature dishes: kokotxas (the throat of cod and hake, a forgotten cut that
  Elkano elevated into a classic), bonito del norte, sea bass, red mullet, sea
  bream and barnacles. The restaurant asks little of its ingredients: everything
  revolves around total control of the grilling and the choice of produce. The
  wine cellar is excellent, and a good txakoli from Txomin Etxaniz or Makatzak
  Narrasti goes perfectly with the seafood.
