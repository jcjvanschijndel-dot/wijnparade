description_en: >-
  Mumm was founded in Reims in 1827 by three German brothers and grew into one
  of the best-known Champagne houses in the world. The house is best known for
  Cordon Rouge, with the red ribbon on the label. Beneath its headquarters in
  Reims lie extensive cellars carved into the chalk.


  The Cordon Rouge Experience starts with a short film, then takes you through
  the cellars and ends with a glass of Cordon Rouge. There are also tastings of
  the RSRV Champagnes, from grand cru vineyards, and a blanc de noirs with
  cheese. Tours are offered in languages including English, German, Italian and
  Spanish. A good first introduction to Champagne in Reims.
