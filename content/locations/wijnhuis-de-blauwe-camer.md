description_en: >-
  The Norbertine sisters of the priory of Sint-Catharinadal have lived on the De
  Blauwe Camer estate in Oosterhout since 1647. In 2014 the sisters planted a
  vineyard, now around 10 hectares with Chardonnay, Auxerrois, Pinot Blanc,
  Pinot Gris, Muscat Ottonel, Pinot Noir and Gamay, among others. They are
  helped by volunteers and a consultant winemaker.


  In the old farmhouse next to the convent, Wijnhuis De Blauwe Camer has been open since 2018: a restaurant with its own vegetable and herb garden, listed in Gault&Millau. You can drink the convent's wines there, alongside wines from elsewhere, and take bottles home. Recommended for lunch or dinner among the vineyards, close to Breda.
