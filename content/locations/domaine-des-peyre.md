description_en: >-
  In Robion, at the foot of the Luberon, lies Domaine des Peyre. Patricia
  Alexandre and Georges Antoun took over the neglected estate and restored it

  vines. The estate covers 32 hectares of vineyards and woodland and makes wines
  in AOP Luberon, AOP Ventoux and IGP Méditerranée.


  The tasting cellar is open all year round. You can taste the wines, take a
  guided tour and walk the art trail across the estate. You can stay in the
  gîtes on the property. A nice address for a wine tasting in Provence, close to
  Cavaillon, L'Isle-sur-la-Sorgue and the villages of the Luberon.
