  Four twenty-somethings with impressive CVs (ex-Rijsel, ex-Troef, ex-two-star
  Flore and ex-Bar du Champagne) opened De Badcuyp in April 2024 on the Albert
  Cuyp market in De Pijp. The name is a play on words on "badkuip" (bathtub) and
  the Cuyp market. It quickly became one of the most talked-about new
  restaurants in Amsterdam. À la carte, to share or not, your choice. Chef Joep
  works with open fire: whole turbot from the grill to order, sweetbreads cooked
  exactly right. Honest, no-nonsense and ingredient-driven. The wine list is
  young but has good references, including mature bottles.
