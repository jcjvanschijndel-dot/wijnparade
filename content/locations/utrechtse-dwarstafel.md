description_en: >-
  Tucked away in a side street off the Utrechtsestraat, among the canal houses
  of central Amsterdam, is one of the most remarkable restaurant experiences in
  the city. For almost twenty years Hans Verbeek and chef Esther van Meerten
  have run this small restaurant of at most 22 seats. Barts Boekje called it one
  of the best restaurants in Amsterdam. The concept is simple but rare: no menu,
  no stress over choices. You choose the number of courses, Esther decides what
  comes to the table, always seasonal, always with passion. Esther works with
  fresh seasonal produce and combines the traditions of French, Italian and
  Dutch cooking: wild Norwegian salmon on potato salad, cod with rocket pesto
  and pearl barley, fallow deer from the Waterleidingduinen with sauerkraut
  quiche and parsnip mousseline. Hans accompanies each dish with Old World
  wines; his extensive collection lines the walls of the intimate room. They
  call it a "ballet of wine and food" themselves. Recognised by Gault&Millau.
  Each course has its own wine pairing: no standard package but a hand-picked
  selection Hans puts together each evening based on that day's ingredients.
