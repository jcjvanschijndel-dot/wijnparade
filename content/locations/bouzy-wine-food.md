description_en: >-
  On the corner of the Denneweg and the Mauritskade, overlooking the water,
  sommeliers Martijn Verbruggen and Stefan Verleers opened Bouzy in 2015. A
  year later it was already named wine bar of the year. Champagne is the
  common thread: there is an exceptional list of grower champagnes, and around
  20 wines by the glass, which you are welcome to taste first.


  To go with them: small bites, cheese and charcuterie boards, bitterballen,
  and brunch at weekends. A second Bouzy is on the Prinsestraat. Recommended
  for Champagne lovers in The Hague.
