description_en: >-
  The Sumarroca family originally comes from Llimiana, in the Pallars Jussà,
  but in the 1980s Carles Sumarroca settled in the Penedès and bought estates
  in Subirats and Banyeres del Penedès. Later came Finca Sumarroca, the former
  estate of the Marquis of Monistrol between Sant Sadurní and Monistrol
  d'Anoia. The vineyards are certified organic, and on the largest estate the
  surrounding forests and two ravines have deliberately been left intact.


  A visit starts with a welcome drink and an introduction to the family's
  history, followed by a tour and a tasting. In good weather you sit on the
  covered terrace overlooking the vineyards. Recommended on a wine trip
  through the Penedès.
