description_en: >-
  Opposite the town hall park in Épernay, the family house of Pierre Mignon has
  a tasting room and boutique. The house itself comes from Le Breuil, in the
  Vallée de la Marne, an important area for meunier, and has been making
  Champagne for five generations.


  Here it is all about Champagne and food. Sommelier Denis Garret puts together
  pairings of the Champagnes with cheese and cured meats, salmon or caviar, and
  there are masterclasses with six Champagnes. You can also simply drop in for
  an introductory tasting. A great place to combine Champagne and food in the
  centre of Épernay.
