description_en: >-
  Where the Tedo flows into the Douro, near Folgosa, lies Quinta do Tedo. The
  eighteenth-century estate has belonged since 1992 to Vincent Bouchard,
  originally from Burgundy, and his Californian wife Kay. On 18 hectares of
  terraces with vines and olive trees, farmed organically, they make port and
  Douro DOC wines, as well as extra virgin olive oil.


  A tour takes in the vineyard, the lagares where the grapes are still trodden
  by foot and the old cellars, and ends with a tasting of wines and ports. In
  summer you can eat at the Bistro Terrace, overlooking the river. There are
  guest rooms and walking trails across the estate. Recommended for a port
  tasting in the Douro Valley, between Peso da Régua and Pinhão.
