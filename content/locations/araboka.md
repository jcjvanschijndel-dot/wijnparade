description_en: >-
  Araboka used to be called Eboka and describes itself as a "casa de vinos y
  comidas": a house of wine and food. It sits on a quiet pedestrian street in
  the old centre, close to the cathedral and the Picasso Museum. The kitchen is
  based on family recipes from Málaga and Andalusia, which they call "cocina de
  herencia" (heritage cooking). There is a second branch on Calle Compositor
  Lehmberg Ruiz, with a charcoal grill.


  Wine is the main focus here. Peñín gives the list its top score ("excellent wine list"). The emphasis is on wines from the province of Málaga, supplemented with other Spanish labels and organic wines. The staff actively advise and match the wine to the dishes. The 7-course tasting menu comes with a wine pairing, with a sweet Málaga wine served alongside the dessert. There is also a small wine shop inside the restaurant.
