description_en: >-
  The Metz family's wine story began in 1904 in Epfig. Today the house is based
  in Marlenheim, at the northern gateway to the Alsace Wine Route, twenty
  minutes from Strasbourg. Arthur Metz is one of the major producers of crémant
  d'Alsace and also makes Alsace wines and grands crus.


  The two-hour prestige visit includes a tour of the winery and a tasting of
  four wines and a crémant, served with foie gras and brioche. There is also a
  short tasting at the counter with a pretzel. A good starting point for the
  Wine Route from Strasbourg.
