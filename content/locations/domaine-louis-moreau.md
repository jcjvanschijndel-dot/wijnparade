description_en: >-
  The Moreau family has been in Chablis since 1814; their ancestors were coopers
  and wine merchants before becoming winegrowers. Louis Moreau has run the
  estate since 1994. It is based in Beine, close to Chablis, and has vineyards
  at all four levels of the appellation, with the Clos des Hospices, which the
  estate owns exclusively, as its showpiece.


  In the boutique in Beine you taste from Petit Chablis to grand cru, optionally
  with gougères, the region's cheese puffs. A good address to learn to taste the
  difference between the levels of Chablis, ten minutes from the town.
