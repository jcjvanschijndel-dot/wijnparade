description_en: >-
  Adega Regional de Colares, founded in 1931, is the oldest cooperative in
  Portugal. The cooperative processes the grapes of Colares' small
  winegrowers, in the historic wine region between the hills of Sintra and
  the Atlantic Ocean, playing an important social role in the area.


  A visit includes tasting the distinctive Colares wines, made from vines
  that often still grow on their own roots. Recommended for anyone wanting
  to get to know Colares' distinctive grape varieties.
