description_en: >-
  Louise Tieleman opened this restaurant on the Veerhaven in Rotterdam's chic
  Scheepvaartkwartier, creating a little piece of Paris on the Maas. Big red
  banquettes, classic paintings, a wooden bar, crockery from Lille, French
  chansons in the background and a baguette with real French butter: every
  detail is right. On the first floor the view over the busy Veerhaven is an
  extra gift. Next to the restaurant there's also the Bar à Vins, a wine bar
  with small bites where you can have a glass of wine without dining. Bistro
  classics without frills: escargots with herb butter, steak tartare, moules,
  sole meunière, côte de boeuf with béarnaise, oeufs mayo, cheese board. The
  wine list is mostly French (you wouldn't expect anything else), with a fine
  selection of Burgundy, Loire and Champagne. Listed by Gault&Millau. The Bar à
  Vins is open Wednesday to Friday from 16.00 and Saturday and Sunday from
  15.00, ideal for a cheese board with a glass of Loire or Champagne overlooking
  the Veerhaven.
