description_en: >-
  On the southern slope of the Saint-Émilion limestone plateau, in
  Saint-Laurent-des-Combes, lies Château Tour Baladoz. The De Schepper-De Mour
  family bought the five-hectare estate in 1996. It goes together with its
  neighbour Château La Croizille, owned by the same family, where a modern
  winery overlooks Saint-Émilion.


  On a visit you see both the traditional cellar of Tour Baladoz and the modern
  facilities of La Croizille, and taste three wines from the Saint-Émilion Grand
  Cru appellation. There are also versions with cheese and cured meats or a
  picnic. Recommended, five minutes from the village of Saint-Émilion.
