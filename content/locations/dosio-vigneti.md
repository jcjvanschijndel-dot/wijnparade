description_en: >-
  Founded in 1974 as a passion and a hobby, it has grown into a serious winery.
  Dosio sits at the highest point of the Barolo appellation, 500 metres up on
  the Serradenari hill in La Morra, a UNESCO heritage area. The view is
  dazzling: over the plains of Cuneo and Turin to the summit of Monviso. The old
  18th-century farmhouse is the beating heart of the estate. Barolo from the
  cool Serradenari slope, a distinct style within La Morra with more elegance
  and freshness thanks to the altitude. Also Barbera d'Alba, Dolcetto d'Alba and
  Langhe Nebbiolo. Visits include a tasting and a magnificent view. There's a
  hotel too, so if you're still looking for somewhere to stay, Dosio is
  definitely an option.
