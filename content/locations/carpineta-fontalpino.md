description_en: >-
  Near Montaperti, in the municipality of Castelnuovo Berardenga east of Siena,
  lies Fattoria Carpineta Fontalpino, owned by the Cresti family. Father Italo
  turned it into a wine business in 1967; since 2004 his children have run it:
  Gioia is the winemaker, Filippo handles sales. They farm organically and focus
  on single-cru wines such as Montaperto, Fontalpino and Dofana.


  Also well known is Do Ut Des, a Tuscan blend with sangiovese. On a visit a
  host takes you to the vineyard and the cellar, and you taste the wines in the
  tasting room. A good address for a wine tasting in the south of Chianti
  Classico, a quarter of an hour from Siena.
