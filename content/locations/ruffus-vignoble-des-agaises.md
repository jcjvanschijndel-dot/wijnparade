description_en: >-
  Vignoble des Agaises was the very first wine estate in Wallonia in
  2002, set up to produce sparkling wine exclusively. The estate grew
  to more than 35 hectares, the largest vineyard in the Kingdom, on a
  chalky soil that gives the Ruffus cuvées their minerality.


  The Ruffus sparkling wines have won multiple medals
  internationally. Recommended for anyone wanting to visit Belgium's
  largest vineyard, in Hainaut.
