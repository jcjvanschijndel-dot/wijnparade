description_en: >-
  The name that put the Nahe on the international wine map. For more than 250
  years the Dönnhoff family has been rooted in the picturesque valley between
  Niederhausen and Schlossböckelheim, one of the most beautiful wine landscapes
  in all of Germany. Tastings by appointment in Oberhausen. A special feature:
  the walking route past the top vineyards (Schlossböckelheimer Kupfergrube,
  Felsenberg, Oberhäuser Brücke), one of the most beautiful vineyard walks in
  Germany, which you can do in half a day. The wines range from the juicy
  Gutsriesling (the entry wine, one of the best in Germany in its class) to the
  extremely rare Schlossböckelheimer Kupfergrube GG and Oberhäuser Brücke
  Eiswein. Top tip: there's also a Chardonnay in the range, and it's
  surprisingly good.
