  personeel helpt je kiezen. Daarbij kleine gerechten, charcuterie en kaas.
  Een aanrader voor een wijnavond in het centrum van Utrecht.
description_en: >-
  Just round the corner from the Neude and the Oudegracht is VinVin, often
  described as Utrecht's first real wine bar. Owner Julian has run it since
  2016 and has even seen it voted the best wine bar in the Netherlands. The
  focus is on wines from French, Italian and Spanish producers who intervene
  as little as possible.


  There are more than thirty wines, but there is no printed wine list: the
  staff help you choose. To go with them there are small plates, charcuterie
  and cheese. Recommended for a wine evening in the centre of Utrecht.
