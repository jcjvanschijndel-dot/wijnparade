description_en: >-
  De Kaaf is the wine shop of Buurtcafé De Tros. The shop opened at the end of
  December 2025, just over three years after De Tros itself. The idea is simple:
  the bottles from the bar can also be taken home. De Tros is a cosy
  neighbourhood café on the Linnaeusstraat, and De Kaaf is just around the
  corner on the Reinwardtstraat.


  De Tros has around two hundred wines, and a large part of them is also in De Kaaf. The selection is broad: natural and organic wines, some with no additives, alongside classics from Germany and France, among others. Now and then they highlight a lesser-known region, such as Savoie. The wines come mostly from small farmers and are affordable where possible. There are two sommeliers in house, who are happy to help with a gift or a special bottle. In spring you can buy a bottle in the shop and open it straight away outside, on the wooden benches along De Tros.
