description_en: >-
  In the Las Salesas neighbourhood, close to Chueca, at the back of ACID
  Bakehouse, is this wine bar from Fede Graciano, also the man behind ACID
  Café. Gota is about two things: natural wine and vinyl. The list has more
  than 200 bottles, around seventy per cent from outside Spain, and about
  fifteen wines are open by the glass each day.


  Two turntables are built into the wooden bar, and records are played from start to finish. With the wine you can have oysters, pickles or a cheese board; the short menu changes every month. Book at the weekend. Recommended for an evening of natural wine and good music on a city break in Madrid.
