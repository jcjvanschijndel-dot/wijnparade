  is gecertificeerd HVE. Bij een bezoek aan de kelders, met een professional,

  voor liefhebbers van zoete wijn.
description_en: >-
  Château La Tour Blanche in Bommes was classified as a Premier Cru Classé of
  Sauternes in 1855. It is the only classified growth in Bordeaux owned by the
  French state: its last owner, Daniel "Osiris" Iffla, left it on condition that
  a school of viticulture be set up there. The school still exists, and the
  label still reads "Donation Osiris".


  The 46-hectare estate lies on one of the gravel ridges of Sauternes and is
  certified HVE. On a visit to the cellars with a professional, you taste the
  premier cru and the second wine, Les Charmilles. A special address for lovers
  of sweet wine.
