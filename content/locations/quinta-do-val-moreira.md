description_en: >-
  Quinta do Val Moreira lies on the south bank of the Douro, in the Cima
  Corgo, where the Douro and Tedo rivers meet. The estate was taken over by
  XVinus in 2018 and converted for wine tourism, with 23 hectares of
  vineyard alongside olive and almond trees.


  A visit includes enjoying the view over the valley, followed by a tasting
  of the estate's Douro wines. Recommended for anyone who likes a quieter
  spot in the Douro valley.
