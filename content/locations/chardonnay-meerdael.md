description_en: >-
  Chardonnay Meerdael is the pioneer of Belgian sparkling wine. In
  1994, Paul and An Vleminckx-Lefever planted 60,000 Chardonnay vines,
  aiming to make a Blanc de Blancs using the traditional method, as in
  Champagne — a first for Belgium.


  The wine has a subtle aroma of spring blossom and ripe fruit and is
  ideal as an aperitif or with seafood. A historic address for getting
  to know Belgian winemaking, close to Leuven.
