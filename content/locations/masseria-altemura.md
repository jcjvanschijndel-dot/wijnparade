description_en: >-
  In the heart of the Primitivo di Manduria area, in the Salento hinterland,
  lies Masseria Altemura. The seventeenth-century masseria of tufa stone, with a
  small chapel with frescoes, has been carefully restored. Next to it stands a
  modern winery built on the principles of bio-architecture. The vineyards are
  planted with primitivo, negroamaro, aglianico, fiano and falanghina.


  On a visit you see the old masseria, the vineyard and the winery, with a
  tasting of four wines or a light lunch with five wines. Groups are small, so
  book in good time. A lovely change from a beach holiday in Puglia, between
  Brindisi and Taranto.
