description_en: >-
  In Neive, one of the four Barbaresco communes, the Negro family has been
  making wine since the 1950s. Giuseppe Negro started with small quantities;
  today oenologist Giorgio Negro runs the business together with his son Marco.
  The vineyards lie in crus such as Gallina, Albesani and Pian Cavallo, and the
  wines go to fine restaurants in Italy and abroad.


  You taste in the tasting room or on the panoramic terrace overlooking the
  vineyards. There are several tastings, from Barbera and Barbaresco to a
  tasting of old Barbaresco vintages from the family's own collection.
  Recommended for a Barbaresco tasting in the Langhe.
