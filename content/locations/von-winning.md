description_en: >-
  Deidesheim is the most concentrated wine destination in the Pfalz: a medieval
  half-timbered town on the Deutsche Weinstraße where three top-level VDP
  estates are within walking distance. Von Winning is the most ambitious and
  headstrong of the three. Founded in 1849 by Friedrich Deinhard, the estate
  combines almost two centuries of winemaking tradition with an approach that
  breaks through traditional categories. The vinothek is open Monday to Friday
  from 8.00 to 18.00 and at weekends from 10.00 to 18.00. You can always walk in
  for a tasting, no reservation required. Monday to Friday, exclusive guided
  wine experiences with a tasting of six selected wines are available by
  appointment, subject to availability. A special option is the cellar tour +
  lunch at Restaurant Leopold: starting at 11.00 with a cellar tour of about an
  hour, followed by lunch from 12.30. Price: €20 per person for the tasting,
  plus the menu and drinks. Available Monday to Thursday, maximum 15
  participants, booking required. Restaurant Leopold is in a beautifully
  renovated former stable on the estate: fjord trout, guinea fowl, asparagus,
  dumplings, top-quality produce perfectly prepared. Von Winning's Rieslings are
  offered at friendly prices. Booking strongly recommended, especially at
  weekends.
