description_en: >-
  The Rosso family has been growing grapes in Serralunga d'Alba, one of the most
  famous Barolo communes, since the late nineteenth century. The family business
  has about twelve hectares of vineyards, all in Serralunga, with crus such as
  Cerretta, La Serra and Broglio. The crown jewel is a plot in the legendary
  Vigna Rionda. Davide Rosso makes the wines with as little intervention as
  possible.


  The modern winery, where tradition and design come together, sits in the
  middle of the Langhe landscape. On a visit you see the cellar and the barrel
  room and taste the Barolos, sometimes straight from the barrel. Booking is
  required. Recommended for Barolo lovers on a wine trip through Piedmont.
