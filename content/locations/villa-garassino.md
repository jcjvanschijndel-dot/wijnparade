  The farmhouse was completely renovated in 2018, and the result is a refined
  agriturismo on a hill with a spectacular panoramic view over the Barbaresco

  with great attention to the selection of ingredients and authenticity of
  terroir. Food and wine pairings are carefully put together to let guests
  discover the flavours and labels of the Langhe and Roero, with a focus on the
  wines of Tenuta Carretta.
