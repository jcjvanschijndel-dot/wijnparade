description_en: >-
  In Capestang, a wine village in the Hérault close to Béziers and the Canal du
  Midi, lies Domaine La Provenquière. The château owes its name to Jean
  Provenquier, its owner in the fifteenth century, and has almost five centuries
  of history. The Robert family has been making wine here for four generations.


  You can choose a short, free tasting, a longer tasting with a stroll across
  the terrace and through the park, or a "vine to glass" tour of the vineyard,
  the park and the cellars. An atmospheric stop for anyone cycling or boating
  along the Canal du Midi.
