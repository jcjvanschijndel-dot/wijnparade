description_en: >-
  In 2004, founder Andrew Weeber planted the first vines on the coastal
  landscape of Gusbourne Estate, in the far southeastern corner of
  England. The estate now farms 93 hectares across Kent and Sussex, with
  Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier for sparkling wine. Gusbourne
  was named WineGB Estate Winery of the Year three years running and
  placed 28th in the 2025 World's Best Vineyards.


  At The Nest, the award-winning tasting room, you can visit year-round
  for a tasting, a tour or lunch among the vines. Recommended for anyone
  wanting to get to know English sparkling wine, less than an hour from
  London.
