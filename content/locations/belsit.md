description_en: >-
  Just outside the village on the Via Alba lies Belsit in Langa. Chef-owner
  Roberto Conterno has turned this restaurant into a fine dining destination
  that combines the traditions of the Langhe with a contemporary approach. The
  wine cellar, designed in the style of Gaudí, is a story in itself: one of the
  most remarkable cellars in the whole of the Langhe.


  The kitchen serves dishes rooted in the most authentic traditions: vitello
  tonnato, battuta di fassona with black truffle, and pumpkin flan with Raschera
  fondue and parsley oil. Alongside the Piedmontese classics there are refined
  fish and vegetarian options too. The wine cellar is superb, extensive and
  carefully put together, with Barolo from all the great crus of La Morra as the
  common thread. Visitors describe the wine list as "a space to lose yourself
  in", from approachable Barbera to the most celebrated Barolo Riserva.
