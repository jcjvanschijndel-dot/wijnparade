description_en: >-
  In Saint-Sauveur, on the edge of Pauillac, lies Château Hourtin-Ducasse, owned
  by the Marengo family since 1976. On 23 hectares the family makes Haut-Médoc.
  It experiments freely: with treatments based on plant infusions, grassed
  vineyards, Landes sheep that keep the grass short and fertilise the soil, nest
  boxes for tits and bats, solar panels, fermentation in barrels and even music
  in the cellar.


  A member of the family shows you round. You can choose an aperitif in the
  cellar or among the vines, or a more extensive tour about the grape varieties.
  A surprising address in the Médoc, open all year round.
