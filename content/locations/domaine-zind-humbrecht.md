description_en: >-
  One of the biggest names in Alsace, and for many the reference point for
  biodynamic Alsace wine at Grand Cru level. Founded in 1959 through the
  marriage of two winemaking families with roots going back to 1620. Olivier
  Humbrecht, who runs the domaine, was the first Frenchman ever to earn the
  Master of Wine diploma, and since 2019 his son Pierre-Emile has worked
  alongside him. Visits by appointment in the modern winery in Turckheim, in the
  middle of the Herrenweg vineyard. Four Grand Cru sites owned: Rangen de Thann,
  Brand, Hengst and Goldert, plus the top sites Clos Windsbuhl, Clos Jebsal,
  Heimbourg and Herrenweg. A tasting of several terroir expressions side by side
  is one of the best ways to understand the difference between soils.
