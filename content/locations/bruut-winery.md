description_en: >-
  Bruut Winery grew out of the merger of two family vineyards in the
  Pajottenland, southwest of Brussels. Eric Geyssens planted the first
  vines of the Dappersveld estate in 2013, and the Dehandschutter
  family started their own vineyard in 2016; in 2022 the two estates
  joined forces under one name.


  You can taste wine in the rolling Pajottenland, within cycling
  distance of Brussels. A young address for discovering Belgian
  winemaking.
