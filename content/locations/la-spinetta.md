description_en: >-
  Giuseppe Rivetti started La Spinetta in 1977 after returning from Argentina,
  with Moscato d'Asti as his first wine. In the 1990s he moved into Barbaresco
  and Barolo, and added Tuscany too. The rhinoceros on the label is his
  trademark, a symbol of power and strength. La Spinetta grew into one of the
  most international and best-known names in Piedmont, now run by his sons
  Giorgio and Carlo and daughter Giovanna. One of the most extensive wineries in
  the Langhe: Moscato d'Asti, Barbaresco (Gallina, Vursù, Starderi), Barolo
  (Campè, Vigneto Garretti), Barbera d'Asti, Langhe Nebbiolo Pin (a
  Nebbiolo/Barbera blend), Chardonnay, Sauvignon, and Tuscan wines from Casanova
  della Spinetta. You can book several tastings through the website, from
  tasting 3 wines to a whole day visiting several locations with lunch. There
  are three locations, so check the website carefully before you go.
