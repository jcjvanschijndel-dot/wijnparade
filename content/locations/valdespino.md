description_en: >-
  Valdespino, with a history dating back to the thirteenth century, makes
  the Inocente, a single-vineyard fino that is the last to ferment in
  American oak barrels and ages through an unusually long ten-criadera
  solera system.


  A visit includes tasting the house's sherries, with a tour that also
  covers the horse stables and an art gallery. Recommended for anyone
  wanting to visit one of Jerez's oldest sherry houses.
