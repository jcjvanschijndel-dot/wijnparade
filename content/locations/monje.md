description_en: >-
  Bodegas Monje, in the La Hollera district of El Sauzal with views of
  Mount Teide, carries on a wine tradition the family traces back to
  1750. The current building dates from 1956 and opened for wine tourism
  in 1983; Felipe Monje, the fifth generation, now leads the house.


  A visit includes tasting the wines with Canarian cheese, in the
  estate's own shop and restaurant with terrace. Recommended for anyone
  wanting to discover Tenerife's most visitor-friendly winery.
