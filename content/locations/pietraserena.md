description_en: >-
  On the road from San Gimignano to Certaldo, with a view of the town's towers,
  lies Pietraserena, owned by the Arrigoni family. Since 1966 the estate has
  grown from seven to around forty hectares. The main grape is vernaccia, used
  for Vernaccia di San Gimignano; there is also sangiovese, for red crus such as
  Caulio and Poggio al Vento.


  Each vineyard has its own name and is vinified separately. You can choose from
  tastings of three to five wines, a blind tasting and even a truffle hunt. A
  nice address for a wine tasting near San Gimignano, easy to combine with a
  visit to the medieval town.
