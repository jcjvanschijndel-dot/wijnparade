description_en: >-
  In early 2025, oenologist and former sommelier Aude Henon opened her own bar à
  vins in the lively Boulingrin district of Reims, right next to Le Wine Bar by
  Le Vintage, where she used to work. Star Wine List immediately praised it as
  "Champagne's newest bar à vins on the block." The concept is clear:
  exclusively small micro-producers who match Aude's philosophy of terroir and
  craft. A wine list in constant motion: champagnes from the region, French
  wines from iconic terroirs and discoveries from the rest of Europe. Excellent
  value for those who want to look beyond a single bottle. Tapas-style small
  dishes: magret de canard and other local Champagne treats. A young, creative
  team that shares stories about every producer, accessible and without
  pretension.
