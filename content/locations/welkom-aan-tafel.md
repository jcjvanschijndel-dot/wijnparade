description_en: >-
  On the Lange Smeestraat, between the Oudegracht and the Singel, Nicole and
  Nicolai run this wine and delicatessen shop. They have been wine importers
  since 2017 and buy directly from the winemakers, now from more than 50
  wineries across Europe. You will also find olive oil, pasta, tinned fish,
  Italian coffee and products from Sicily, such as pecorino and salami.


  Below the shop is a wine cellar where tastings are held: Nicolai talks about the wines and Nicole makes snacks to go with them. A private tasting can be booked for groups of six or more. Recommended for anyone in Utrecht who wants to buy wine straight from the importer.
