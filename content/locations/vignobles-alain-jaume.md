description_en: >-
  The Jaume family has been working the land north of Châteauneuf-du-Pape since
  1826. Today Hélène, Sébastien and Christophe carry on the family business,
  from Orange. The best-known estate is Grand Veneur in Châteauneuf-du-Pape; the
  family also makes Lirac, Côtes-du-Rhône and Côtes-du-Rhône Villages. The
  vineyards are certified organic.


  You can come for tours of the vineyard and the winery with a tasting. There is
  also a lunch with matching wines, La Table d'Alain Jaume, and a trip by
  electric mountain bike through the vineyards of Châteauneuf-du-Pape.
  Recommended in the southern Rhône, between Orange and Châteauneuf-du-Pape.
