description_en: >-
  Paco is the only member of staff at Bar François: he runs the whole place
  solo, from the kitchen to the wine list. The Frenchman opened his wine bar in
  September 2026 on the Douwes Dekkerstraat in Oud-West, close to the Ten
  Katemarkt. The atmosphere is unmistakably French: a sturdy bar of blond wood,
  artichoke flowers in a vase, framed posters on the wall, free water in Ricard
  jugs on the table. It's like walking in on a friend who happens to run a wine
  bar. The food changes weekly depending on what Paco feels like: grandma's
  southern French cooking, made by someone who grew up with it. Duck, panisse
  with Comté, frittata with cheese and olives. The wine list consists
  exclusively of natural wines from small French producers you'll hardly find
  anywhere else in Amsterdam. Paco knows them all personally and gives advice
  without any fuss. An evening at Bar François feels like dinner at the home of
  a good friend who happens to have excellent wine.
