description_en: >-
  In the Garraf Massif, in Avinyonet del Penedès, Carlos Esteva (1950–2023)
  originally set out to restore a centuries-old masia, but the old vineyards
  around it soon caught his attention. The estate covers around 450 hectares,
  about 90 of them under vine. Esteva was a pioneer in fermenting xarel·lo in
  chestnut barrels, and his Xarel·lo Pairal is the best-known example. Today
  the estate is run by director Jordi Esteve and Julia Esteva, Carlos's
  daughter.


  The cellar is built into the rock, out of respect for the Garraf landscape.
  You can choose from several visits: with breakfast in the historic
  farmhouse, with a tasting of the three Gran Caus wines, or by 4x4 through
  the finest vineyards. Recommended for lovers of exceptional xarel·lo.
