description_en: >-
  In the De Koninck city brewery, in Antwerp's Harmonie district, is this
  restaurant run by chef Bert Jan Michielsen and sommelier Luc Dickens. Under
  the same roof is the butcher De Laet & Van Haver, which supplies most of the
  meat; at the open kitchen you can watch it being prepared. Red meat takes
  centre stage, such as Simmental fillet or a ballotine of pig's trotter. The
  Butcher's Son holds a Michelin star.


  Dickens' wine list runs to around 400 wines, with the emphasis on France,
  Italy and Spain. Recommended for meat lovers in Antwerp who want a good bottle
  to go with it.
