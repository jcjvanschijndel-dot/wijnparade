description_en: >-
  In November 2011 Kick Smol opened the first wine bar on the Witte de
  Withstraat, and it has been one of the most visited wine bars in the city ever
  since. The building dates from the 1940s and was designed by architect
  Leendert van der Vlugt as the headquarters of the Vereniging Eigen Huis (the
  homeowners' association). After a thorough restoration to its original state,
  the space feels warm and homely: tall windows, a spacious bar, atmospheric
  lighting. It's now run by Esther and Pieter den Haan-Mellegers. Named the best
  wine bar in the Netherlands by restaurant guide Grootspraak. 135 wines on the
  list, 35 by the glass, poured at the table and never just put down at the bar.
  Chef Etiënne Kool makes more than twenty small dishes that go perfectly with
  the wine: oyster soupe de poisson, foie gras, veal tartare, mackerel ceviche.
  Ideal for drinks, a petit dîner or a whole evening of wine.
