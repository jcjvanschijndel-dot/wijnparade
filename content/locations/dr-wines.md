description_en: >-
  This corner spot on the Anna Paulownaplein, in the Zeeheldenkwartier, used to
  be the wine bar Wicked Wines. Hidde-Jurre Griffioen and Annick van Bladel
  worked there while studying at Hotelschool The Hague, and they brought the
  place back to life as Dr. Wines; the name is a nod to the old wine bar.


  It is a relaxed wine bar and restaurant with a menu full of Asian, French and international influences, including sushi. Let yourself be surprised by the 'Doctor's advice': three wines to go with your dishes. Winemaker dinners are held here too. Recommended for an evening of wine and food in The Hague.
