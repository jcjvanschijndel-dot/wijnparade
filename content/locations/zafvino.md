description_en: >-
  In the Zeven Straatjes in the centre of Arnhem, everything revolves around
  Italy. Owner Zaf Göbel worked as a sommelier, mainly in Italian restaurants,
  and opened ZafVino in 2012. Every year he travels to Italy to buy wines that
  are hard to find in the Netherlands. Thanks to Coravin you can also taste fine
  bottles by the glass, from the house wine to a glass of Amarone.


  The kitchen sticks to Italian classics such as focaccia, burrata and tagliatelle with truffle, with homemade limoncello to finish. Recommended for lovers of Italian wine in Arnhem.
