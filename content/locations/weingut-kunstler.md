description_en: >-
  The Künstler family had been making wine since the mid-seventeenth
  century, then in Southern Moravia, until Franz Künstler was expelled
  after the Second World War and rebuilt the business in 1965 in
  Hochheim am Main. His son Gunter Künstler took over in 1992 and
  moved the estate in 1996 to the former Aschrott property. More than
  seventy percent of the vineyards carry VDP.Grosse Lage status,
  Germany's highest classification.


  A visit, by appointment, includes tasting the estate's Riesling and
  Spätburgunder wines. Recommended for anyone wanting to visit one of
  the Rheingau's top wine estates, with a history reaching back well
  before its current location.
