  On the Avenue de Champagne, the most famous champagne avenue in the world,
  stands a house that is deliberately different from its big neighbours. No mass
  production, no tours of a hundred people at a time: Leclerc Briant is a
  boutique champagne house that does things differently. By appointment only, in
  small groups of at most six people. Visitors discover the presses, the barrel
  cellars with contemporary architecture, the vats and the cellars 35 metres
  underground. Afterwards there's a view over Épernay and the surroundings from
  the rooftop terrace.
