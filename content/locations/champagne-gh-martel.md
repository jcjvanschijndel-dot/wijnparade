description_en: >-
  G.H. Martel & C° was founded in 1869 by a small grower and Champagne maker
  from Avenay-Val-d'Or. Today the house is owned by the Rapeneau family, who
  have been in Champagne for four generations. On the Saint-Nicaise hill in
  Reims the house has a cellar museum in eighteenth-century buildings and chalk
  quarries, partly medieval, which are on the UNESCO World Heritage List.


  After a tour of the museum and the crayères you taste two cuvées side by side.
  Visits are more affordable than at the big houses nearby. A great alternative
  for anyone who wants to visit Champagne cellars in Reims.
