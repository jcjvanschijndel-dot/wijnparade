description_en: >-
  The Bolet family has been making wine in Castellví de la Marca, in the
  Penedès, for more than seven generations. The cellar is in the family masia
  dating from 1619, and next to it stands the Modernista house from 1911,
  designed by the architect Santiago Güell from Vilafranca, where you can now
  also stay the night. The family has been fully organic since 2003 and has
  since started farming biodynamically as well. The wines and cavas come from
  the family estates of Mas Lluet, Can Planes d'en Perull and Les Conilleres.


  On a visit you see the masia and the Modernista house, with a small museum
  of old farm tools, and taste three wines or cavas. There is also a premium
  option with three top wines. Recommended on a wine trip through the Penedès.
