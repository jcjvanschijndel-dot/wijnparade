description_en: >-
  Wiston Estate is in the heart of West Sussex, on the chalk slopes of the South
  Downs: the same chalky bedrock as the Champagne region. The estate has been in
  one family's hands for centuries and covers more than 2,500 hectares. The
  first vines were planted in 2006, and since then Wiston has become one of the
  most respected English sparkling wine houses, with the Vintage Blanc de Blancs
  as its absolute top wine. Wine tours and tastings, walks on the South Downs
  along marked routes (some of the most beautiful in Sussex), and live music and
  food trucks at sunset (in the summer months). A self-contained holiday home is
  available on the estate if you want to stay longer. Restaurant Chalk at Wiston
  is the culinary heart of the estate: honest, delicious food celebrating the
  estate's own produce and local farmers and suppliers. Wiston makes only
  sparkling wine: a classic Cuvée (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier),
  Blanc de Blancs, Rosé and a single-vineyard Pinot Noir with five years of
  cellar ageing. The wines consistently receive high scores from international
  critics. Besides wine there's also Wiston Estate Gin.
