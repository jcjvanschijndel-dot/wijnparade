description_en: >-
  BAK started in 2012 as a pop-up in a squatted building and breathed new life
  into what was then a rather tame Dutch restaurant scene. Now it is co-owner of
  the warehouse building in the Houthavens (third floor, ring the bell in the
  evening), with a wooden-beamed ceiling and a spectacular view over the IJ. Set
  tasting menus that change daily depending on what their own suppliers deliver
  that morning. Local, seasonal, organic, from parsnip to skate wing. Chef Benny
  Blisto combines lightly sour rhubarb and beetroot sauce with venison into
  something that takes the whole dish to new heights. The wine list is varied
  and playful, with a good selection of natural wines and fair prices, strong in
  France and Italy, put together by owner-sommelier Alessandro da Fies.
