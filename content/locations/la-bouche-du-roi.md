  Op dertig kilometer van Parijs, in de vlakte van Versailles, ligt La Bouche du

  Versailles, voor wie wijn uit de regio van de hoofdstad wil proeven.
description_en: >-
  Thirty kilometres from Paris, on the plain of Versailles, lies La Bouche du
  Roi: the first professional wine estate in Île-de-France. The name refers to
  the court service that, from the sixteenth century, guarded the quality of the
  wine at the table of the French kings. The vineyard in Davron is farmed
  organically.


  On a tour you walk through the vineyard and see the winery, followed by a
  tasting; there is also a version in which the wines are paired with local
  produce. A surprising outing from Paris or Versailles, for anyone who wants to
  taste wine from the capital's own region.
