description_en: >-
  In the Barrio de las Letras, the district in the heart of Madrid that
  attracted writers and artists for centuries and is now the coolest restaurant
  neighbourhood in the city, owner Fernando opened his light, friendly wine bar.
  No pretension, no complicated concept: good honest wine, good honest food, and
  a team that knows what it's talking about. One of the staff, Elena, makes wine
  herself, one of those little details that make Ganz special. Recognised by
  Star Wine List. The wine list is deliberately well balanced: classic regions
  such as the Loire, Champagne, Rioja and Sierra de Gredos, plus young,
  up-and-coming producers using local grapes and offering excellent value.
  Fernando has a clear preference for small winemakers not everyone knows yet.
  The list changes regularly. The food is fresh from the market every day: small
  dishes to share, such as burrata, artichokes, tuna carpaccio and lamb. They
  also serve brunch, with fresh juices, croissants and home-made yoghurt.
