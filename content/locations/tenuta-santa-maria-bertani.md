description_en: >-
  In Arbizzano di Negrar, on the edge of Verona, lies Villa Mosconi Bertani, the
  private estate of the Bertani family. The villa with its frescoes, romantic
  park and historic cellar is considered the birthplace of Amarone. The family
  makes wines here from native Valpolicella grapes, using traditional methods.


  On a tour you see the frescoed rooms, the park, the drying lofts, the old
  distillery and the monumental cellar. You then taste Soave, Valpolicella
  Ripasso and Amarone, among others. A special address for a wine tasting in
  Valpolicella, twenty minutes from the centre of Verona.
