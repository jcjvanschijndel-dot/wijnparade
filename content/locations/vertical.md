description_en: >-
  Vertical is the wine bar of Julio Barluenga, a Barcelona sommelier with an
  impressive track record: he worked at elBulli, Azurmendi and Gastón Acurio's
  Peruvian restaurant, among others, before returning to Spain in 2017 and, via
  Noor and Kaleja, eventually opening his own place in the historic centre of
  Málaga. This isn't a place where you just order a glass: the sommelier (often
  Julio himself, sometimes the equally skilled Estrella) first asks about your
  taste and then brings you a bottle to try. Expect a personal, relaxed evening
  in which you learn a lot about lesser-known, small producers. Booking is
  almost essential, as the place is small and fills up quickly.
