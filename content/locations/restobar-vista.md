description_en: >-
  On the ground floor of De Rotterdam, on the Wilhelminapier, Ingrid and
  Henrie van der Heijden opened Restobar Vista in July 2024. It is their second
  restaurant, alongside Vista in Willemstad, which has a Michelin star. Through
  the tall windows you look out over the Nieuwe Maas, the skyline and the
  Erasmus Bridge.


  Chef Kimmo Geus, who previously cooked in Willemstad, makes French dishes in
  brasserie style with the finesse of a starred kitchen. Hostess Nikki
  Bijtenhoorn looks after the dining room. The wine list has special wines at
  fair prices. There
  are sixty seats at the tables and a dinner bar for ten. Recommended for a
  dinner overlooking the Maas in Rotterdam.
