description_en: >-
  One of the most underrated wineries in the Langhe. Palladino makes Barolo
  exclusively from its own vineyards in Serralunga d'Alba, the most powerful and
  tannic commune of the Barolo appellation. 15 hectares spread across the most
  famous crus of Serralunga: Cerretta, Vigna Rionda and Serra. Sandy marl with
  high concentrations of limestone on south-facing slopes between 300 and 400
  metres. Barolo Cerretta, Barolo Vigna Rionda and Barolo Serra: three of the
  most respected crus in Serralunga. The wines are powerful and tannic and ask
  for patience, but the reward is in proportion. Besides Barolo, also Barbaresco
  and Moscato d'Asti.
