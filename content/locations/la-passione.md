description_en: >-
  On the Noordeinde, the most beautiful and artistic street in The Hague, full
  of galleries, antiquarian bookshops and chic boutiques, La Passione has been
  running on genuine Italian hospitality for years. Chefs Paolo Tabaku
  (Albanian) and Nicolò Sammarro (Sicilian) cook with produce from their own
  home regions: fresh, seasonal and as organic as possible, some of it imported
  themselves. The pasta is made by hand every day. Hosts Tim and Tellis welcome
  guests like family: warm, personal and without distance. Every evening there's
  a changing surprise menu of several courses: no fixed menu, but whatever the
  chef likes best that day. Pappardelle with wild mushrooms, risotto with
  truffle and burrata, Sicilian sea bass, chocolate soufflé to finish, all
  breathing Italian tradition. The wine list is exclusively Italian, from north
  to south, from Piedmont and Alto Adige to Sicily and Campania. Small,
  little-known producers sit alongside established names, and Tim and Tellis are
  happy to guide your choice. La Passione also has its own wine club for
  regulars.
