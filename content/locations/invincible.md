description_en: >-
  On the Haarstraat, in the centre of Antwerp, Kenny Burssens has been cooking
  in his own restaurant for around twenty years. He is both chef and sommelier,
  and it shows: the wine matters as much as the food here. Burssens cooks
  contemporary French food with a solid base, including his own pâté en croûte
  and sausages; you sit at a long counter looking straight into the open
  kitchen.


  The wine list is extensive, with plenty of choice by the glass. Recommended
  for anyone in Antwerp who wants to eat well at the counter.
