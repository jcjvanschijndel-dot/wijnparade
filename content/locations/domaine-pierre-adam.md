description_en: >-
  In Ammerschwihr, on the Alsace Wine Route near Kaysersberg, lies Domaine
  Pierre Adam, a two-generation family estate with just over twelve hectares of
  vines. Its motto: let each terroir speak in the wine. The showpiece is the
  grand cru Kaefferkopf; the estate also makes crémant d'Alsace.


  On a one-hour introduction you see the cellar and taste five wines, including
  two grands crus. There is also a more extensive introduction to tasting with
  seven wines, and a ride through the vineyard in an electric vehicle. A
  welcoming address a quarter of an hour from Colmar.
