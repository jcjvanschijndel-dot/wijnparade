description_en: >-
  Château des Allues isn't a wine estate in the classic sense: it's a
  spectacular 16th-century castle in the middle of the Combe de Savoie, serving
  as a guest house and restaurant, surrounded by vineyards. Restaurant La
  Chapelle is housed in the castle's old chapel: an intimate place to eat with
  character. Local produce, Savoyard cooking and, of course, Savoie wines. The
  castle no longer makes wine itself, but it sits right in the middle of one of
  the best wine areas of Savoie: the vineyard of Saint-Jean-de-la-Porte is
  literally on the doorstep. An ideal base for a drive past Domaine Genoux in
  Arbin and Domaine Dupasquier in Jongieux.
