description_en: >-
  In a converted old warehouse on the Oudeleeuwenrui, on the Eilandje, chef
  Ingrid Neven cooks in a relaxed setting beneath the wooden beams. Her cooking
  is French and Italian, with the occasional Asian touch, and truffle dishes are
  a fixture.


  Wine takes centre stage at Pazzo. The restaurant's wine list runs to hundreds
  of wines, and above the restaurant there is a separate wine bar with more than
  170 wines, most of them also by the glass. That is where you have an aperitif
  before dinner or a last glass afterwards. Book for the restaurant. Recommended
  for anyone in Antwerp who wants to eat well with an extensive wine list.
