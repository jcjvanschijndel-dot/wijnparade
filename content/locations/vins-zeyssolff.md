description_en: >-
  The Zeyssolff family has been making wine in Gertwiller, near Barr and half an
  hour from Strasbourg, since 1778. Yvan and his wife are the eleventh
  generation and vinify the seven Alsace grape varieties. Beneath the family
  home is the shop and tasting room Au Péché Vigneron, completely redesigned in
  2015.


  The cellar tour is an experience in itself, with the family's story and the
  secrets of the cellar. You then taste the wines, optionally with an Alsatian
  meal, a brunch or a platter with a wine to match each dish.
