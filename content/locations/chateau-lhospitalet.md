description_en: >-
  Château L'Hospitalet lies in the middle of the protected La Clape massif,
  between Narbonne and the Mediterranean. Winemaker Gérard Bertrand bought it in
  2002 and made it the flagship of his estates. The vineyard is farmed
  biodynamically.


  It is a complete wine resort: you can visit the cellars and taste the wines,
  explore the vineyard on an electric scooter, and eat in the gastronomic
  restaurant L'Art de Vivre or in the bistro. In summer there is a private beach
  club in Narbonne-Plage. You can stay in the five-star hotel with spa. A
  luxurious base for a wine trip through the Languedoc.
