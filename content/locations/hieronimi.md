description_en: >-
  For more than 35 years the Hieronimi family has welcomed guests in their
  vaulted cellars on the bank of the Mosel in Cochem: six generations of
  winegrowing and wine trading, begun by Johannes Hieronimi in 1843. The
  absolute highlight is the Weingarten, a green garden with deckchairs right on
  the Mosel, lit with string lights after sunset, on the other side of the
  bridge, with a view of the Reichsburg Cochem on the hill. Their own wines come
  from 7 hectares in neighbouring Ellenz, mainly Riesling, supplemented with
  Chardonnay and Burgundy grapes. Buy by the glass or by the bottle: you get the
  glasses with it and bring them back afterwards. Small snack boards are
  available, but no full dinner.
