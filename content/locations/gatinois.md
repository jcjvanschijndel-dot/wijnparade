description_en: >-
  Perhaps the best-kept secret of Aÿ Grand Cru. The family has been rooted in Aÿ
  since the early 18th century. 7 hectares, exclusively Grand Cru Aÿ. Pinot Noir
  dominates (about 90%), with a small fraction of Chardonnay for freshness.
  Light, lively and wonderfully balanced: not the champagne for lovers of heavy,
  full styles, but for those looking for finesse and terroir expression. Only
  50,000 bottles a year. Four cuvées: Brut Réserve, Rosé, Millésimé and the
  Grand Cru Brut. The Millésimé is the most complex, an absolute bargain
  compared with the têtes de cuvée of the big houses at 2-3 times the price.
