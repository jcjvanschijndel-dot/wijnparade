description_en: >-
  Jelle Stelpstra had worked as a tax adviser for twelve years when, on holiday
  in Riga, he discovered a wine tap system and decided on the spot: I want this
  in Rotterdam. In September 2020 Walsjérôt opened on the Bentinckplein in
  Blijdorp. The concept is simple and brilliant: when you come in you load a
  card, then you walk to the imposing tap wall and pour your own, a sip, half a
  glass or a full glass. An evening here feels like a personal, no-barriers wine
  tasting. More than 70 wines from all over the world are open and at the right
  temperature at the same time, from fresh Albariño to powerful Italian reds.
  Alongside the tap wall there are Basque-style pintxos and tapas: oysters,
  cheese and charcuterie boards, Flammkuchen, beetroot couscous. Regular
  tastings and Vino & Pintxos evenings.
