description_en: >-
  This bodega in the centre of Jerez dates back to 1837, when the
  Andrada-Vanderwilde family turned to sherry. In 1972 Fernando
  Andrada-Vanderwilde created the brand "Fernando de Castilla", named
  after King Fernando III el Santo. Since 1999 the house has been
  owned by Norwegian Jan Pettersen, who previously spent fifteen years
  at sherry house Osborne.


  A visit, by appointment, takes about ninety minutes and covers the
  production of sherry, sherry brandy and vinegar. Recommended for
  anyone wanting to visit a small, quality-focused sherry house in the
  centre of Jerez.
