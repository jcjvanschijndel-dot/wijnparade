  Michael Mast runs this atmospheric restaurant in the heart of medieval
  Bernkastel, close to the famous half-timbered houses and the Graacher Tor
  gate. It's deliberately not a schnitzel-and-Flammkuchen place: the cooking is
  creative and at gourmet level, which immediately sets it apart from most of
  the tourist spots in town. Expect creative dishes such as a wild boar burger,
  menu combinations with regional produce, steak and classic soup. The owner
  goes round the tables himself to advise on wine pairings for each dish, a
  personal approach many visitors appreciate.
