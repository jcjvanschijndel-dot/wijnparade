description_en: >-
  In Edegem, just south of Antwerp, Stable occupies the former stables of Hof
  ter Linden, an eighteenth-century castle. Architect Dieter Vander Velpen
  designed the interior, with old brick walls and beams alongside dark wood and
  long curtains. Chef Njegos Kalicanin cooks creative, modern food, and you
  choose a menu of six or seven courses.


  The wine list has a sizeable Belgian selection, and the cellar is well
  stocked. Recommended for a special dinner in a historic building, a
  fifteen-minute drive from the centre of Antwerp.
