description_en: >-
  The Athenian House sits on the edge of the caldera in Imerovigli,
  overlooking Skaros Rock and the Santorini sunset. Chef Dimitris
  Skarmoutsos reinterprets Greek classics with local ingredients, such as
  grilled grouper, lamb moussaka and beetroot risotto. The wine cellar is
  considered one of the most extensive on Santorini, with rare bottles of
  Assyrtiko, Aidani and Mavrotragano.


  A visit includes a sommelier who helps choose a matching wine, with a
  tasting flight available alongside the menu. Recommended for anyone
  wanting to taste the local grapes with a view of the caldera during a
  visit to Santorini.
