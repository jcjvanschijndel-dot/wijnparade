  Tussen Silves, Monchique en Lagoa, in het binnenland van de Algarve, ligt

description_en: >-
  Between Silves, Monchique and Lagoa, in the Algarve hinterland, lies Morgado
  do Quintão. The family estate has been owned by descendants of the Count of
  Silves ever since it was founded. Its vineyards are among the oldest in the
  Algarve and are farmed organically. With winemaker Joana Maçanita the estate
  makes wines from native grapes such as negra mole and crato branco.


  A highlight is the lunch at the farmers' table: three courses with wine, under
  an age-old tree overlooking the vineyard. There are also holiday cottages and
  a swimming pool. Recommended for anyone looking for more than beaches in the
  Algarve: Silves and the beaches of Lagoa are close by.
