description_en: >-
  Less than 300 metres from the historic centre of Castiglione Falletto lies
  this quiet, atmospheric relais in the middle of the vineyards. The building
  houses a tasting room/enoteca, a panoramic terrace and an infinity pool with
  360-degree vineyard views: on clear days you can see from La Morra to
  Serralunga d'Alba. The adjoining "Valentino" vineyard supplies the Nebbiolo
  for the Barolo Ciabot Tanasio, the house's top wine. Tastings are possible in
  the cantina (Via Alba-Monforte 39, open daily including Sundays and public
  holidays, no reservation required). The cantina sells the full range: Barolo,
  Barbaresco, Nebbiolo, Barbera, Dolcetto, Chardonnay, Moscato, grappa and the
  classic Barolo Chinato. You can stay in one of the six rooms or an apartment,
  all designed to make the most of the panorama through large windows. Infinity
  pool, garden, children's playground, free parking. Guests of the relais can
  dine by the pool in the evening with bottles of Sobrero Barolo.
