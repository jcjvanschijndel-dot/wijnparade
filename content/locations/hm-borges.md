description_en: >-
  H.M.Borges was founded in 1877 by Henrique Menezes Borges, who devoted his
  life to selecting Madeira wines for long ageing. The company is still
  housed in a century-old building in the centre of Funchal, where
  production, ageing and bottling all come together.


  A visit includes tasting the different styles of Madeira wine, from
  sercial to malvasia. A good address in the centre of Funchal for getting
  to know the Madeira tradition without a trip into the countryside.
