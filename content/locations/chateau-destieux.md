  voor een proeverij bij een grand cru classé, op tien minuten van het dorp
  Saint-Émilion.
description_en: >-
  Château Destieux, an eight-hectare Saint-Émilion Grand Cru Classé, sits on one
  of the highest points of the appellation's limestone plateau, in
  Saint-Hippolyte. From the estate you look far out over the Dordogne valley. It
  belongs to the Dauriac family, who also own Château Montlisse and Château La
  Clémence in Pomerol.


  In the new tasting room, overlooking the vineyards and the valley, you taste
  three vintages of Destieux after a tour. There are also combinations with the
  wines of Montlisse or La Clémence. Visits by appointment only. A lovely
  address for a tasting at a grand cru classé, ten minutes from the village of
  Saint-Émilion.
