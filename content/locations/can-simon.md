description_en: >-
  The Lores-Gelpí family has run this restaurant with passion and dedication
  since 1988. In 2001 they earned their first Michelin star, and they have kept
  it for 22 years in a row since. The brothers Josep María and Xavier
  Lores-Gelpí, experts in front of house and kitchen respectively, welcome
  guests in an old fisherman's house from 1700, in the middle of the medieval
  Vila Vella of Tossa de Mar. Tossa de Mar, the "blue paradise" that so
  enchanted Marc Chagall, is a medieval town full of things to do: a walk
  through the lanes, a swim in a cove, and then lunch or dinner here. The
  restaurant has two levels, quiet and intimate, with views of the medieval
  walls. The gastronomic offering seamlessly combines modernity with traditional
  flavours and seasonal ingredients. Oysters, Catalan chestnut stew, monkfish,
  goat shoulder, and the traditional Catalan pijama for dessert. A tasting menu
  including wine pairing. Very affordable for what you get!
