description_en: >-
  In Sant Pau d'Ordal, in the heart of the Penedès, lies the family business
  Eudald Massana Noya. The thirty hectares of vineyards around Finca El Maset
  are farmed organically and biodynamically, without herbicides or synthetic
  products. The estate makes still wines and cava, including cavas aged for a
  long time on the lees.


  A visit starts with a walk through the vineyard and ends with a tasting of
  wine and cava, with local produce. There is also a wine bar in the winery
  garden. A good address for a cava tasting an hour from Barcelona.
