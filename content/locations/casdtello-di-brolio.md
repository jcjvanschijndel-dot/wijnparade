description_en: >-
  The Ricasoli family has been making wine from the vineyards around their
  castle since 1141, which makes Brolio the oldest wine-producing operation in
  the world to have remained continuously in family hands. Here, on the highest
  hill of the Chianti Classico area, Baron Bettino Ricasoli devised the original
  formula for Chianti in the nineteenth century. Tasting options range from a
  glass at the enoteca to extensive masterclasses. The absolute highlight is the
  Sunset Tour: an evening tour with a castle and vineyard visit, ending with
  dinner at the Osteria del Castello, including a wine tasting and the full
  dinner. Book well in advance. At the foot of the cypress avenue leading to the
  castle, L'Osteria del Castello serves skilfully reworked traditional Tuscan
  dishes. Dinner is included for those on the Sunset Tour; other visitors can
  book the osteria separately, at least two days in advance.
