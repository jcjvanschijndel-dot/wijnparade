description_en: >-
  At number 25 on the Avenue de Champagne, the most famous wine avenue in the
  world, lined with the palaces of Moët & Chandon, Perrier-Jouët and Pol Roger,
  stands a small, headstrong wine bar that deliberately does the opposite of its
  neighbours. No big names, no marketing budgets, no tickets for cellar tours.
  #Brut focuses exclusively on grower champagnes: small, independent winemakers
  who grow their own grapes and make champagne with a distinct terroir
  character. Biodynamic and organic are the norm here. A stylish modern interior
  with an industrial touch and a small cobbled area outside: the terrace
  overlooks the Avenue de Champagne, which on a sunny afternoon is one of the
  nicest spots in all of Épernay. Two champagne flights are available (three
  glasses each). A small boutique in the bar sells the same grower champagnes
  you taste, plus artisan local products: terrines, sausages, chocolate. Small
  bites to order individually: cheese and charcuterie boards, terrines, local
  bread.
