description_en: >-
  Eight hundred metres from the village of Saint-Émilion lies Château Balestard
  La Tonnelle, a Grand Cru Classé. The Capdemourlin family, in Saint-Émilion
  since 1647, bought the estate in 1923; today the twelfth and thirteenth
  generations run it. Next to the remains of a fifteenth-century tower stands a
  new, bioclimatic winery.


  You can taste on the panoramic roof terrace, with views all around over the
  vineyards, or choose a tour with a vertical tasting of six vintages. There is
  also a blind tasting. A lovely address you can walk to from the village of
  Saint-Émilion.
