description_en: >-
  In Annunziata, the hamlet below La Morra in the heart of the Barolo area, the
  Marrone family runs its wine business. Gian Piero and Giovanna do so together
  with their daughters Denise, Serena and Valentina, the fourth generation. They
  welcome guests themselves, in English, French or German.


  You taste in the tasting room with large windows overlooking the hills of the
  Langhe, with each wine paired with a traditional dish. You can also have lunch
  or take a cooking class with mother Giovanna and chef Paolo Garabello. A
  welcoming address for a wine tasting with food in the Barolo region.
