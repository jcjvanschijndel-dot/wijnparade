  bij een huis dat dicht bij de wijnboeren staat.
description_en: >-
  In 1956 a handful of winegrowers in Pierry, on the edge of Épernay, joined
  forces in a cooperative. From it grew the Champagne house Vincent d'Astrée.
  Together the members farm some 85 hectares of premier cru on the Coteaux Sud
  d'Épernay, with meunier, pinot noir and chardonnay.


  At the historic site in Pierry you see the cellars where the Champagne ages,
  followed by a tasting. There is also a blanc de blancs workshop. An
  approachable address for a Champagne tasting close to Épernay, at a house that
  stays close to its winegrowers.
