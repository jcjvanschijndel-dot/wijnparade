description_en: >-
  At the family house of Cuillier everything revolves around family and terroir.
  The Champagnes come from about six hectares of vineyards on sandy soil, which
  gives them a character of their own. The house recently moved to the La Grange
  le Comté estate in Brugny-Vaudancourt, south of Épernay, where it now welcomes
  visitors.


  You can choose from a tour with two Champagnes, a walk through the vineyard, a
  visit focused on barrel fermentation or a workshop in which you put together
  your own blend. Bring a warm jumper for the cool cellar. A nice address for a
  Champagne tasting close to Épernay.
