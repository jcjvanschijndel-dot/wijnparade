description_en: >-
  John Cox inherited his parents' farm in Eys and planted the first vines
  there in 2007, on a characteristic quadrangular farmstead with two
  hectares of vineyard. The name refers to the salamanders once common in
  the area; in 2013 a plot followed with the rare-for-the-Netherlands
  varieties Siegerrebe and Frühburgunder.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's wines, including Pinot Noir,
  Chardonnay and Riesling. Recommended for anyone wanting to visit a small,
  personal winery in South Limburg.
