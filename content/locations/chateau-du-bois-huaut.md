description_en: >-
  In Gorges, near Clisson in the Nantes wine region, lies Château du Bois-Huaut.
  Since 1897 the estate has passed from father to son. Unusually for the region,
  alongside Muscadet it grows fourteen different grape varieties, including
  sauvignon, pinot gris, gewurztraminer, pinot noir, merlot and cabernet.
  Everything is harvested by hand.


  The tastings are led by the winegrower, from a short tasting of six wines to
  an introduction to wine tasting with more cuvées and a tour of the cellar. An
  educational and welcoming address, half an hour from Nantes.
