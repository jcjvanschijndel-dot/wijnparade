  De kelders van Taittinger liggen onder de resten van de dertiende-eeuwse abdij

  kelderbezoeken van Reims.
description_en: >-
  The Taittinger cellars lie beneath the remains of the thirteenth-century
  Saint-Nicaise abbey in Reims. After the French Revolution the abbey was
  demolished, but its crypts and tunnels survived. Beneath them lie chalk
  quarries from Gallo-Roman times, eighteen metres underground; they are on the
  UNESCO World Heritage List.


  A guided tour of about an hour takes you through the quarries and the remains
  of the abbey and ends with a tasting of this family house's Champagnes. The
  best-known cuvée is Comtes de Champagne. One of the most beautiful cellar
  visits in Reims.
