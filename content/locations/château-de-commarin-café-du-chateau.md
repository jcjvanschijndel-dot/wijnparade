description_en: >-
  Château de Commarin has been owned by the same family for more than 900 years,
  currently the 26th generation, with Count and Countess Bertrand de Vogüé as
  its present residents. Originally a Roman villa, later a fortified medieval
  castle (already mentioned in 1214) under the powerful Dukes of Burgundy, the
  estate evolved in the 17th and 18th centuries into the elegant residential
  château you see today, resulting in a striking architectural mix: two massive
  medieval towers and a 14th-century chapel next to wings in Louis XIII and
  Louis XIV style. The château has been a listed Monument Historique since 1949.


  On the grounds is the Café du Château, a restaurant and wine bar with a
  terrace that is perfect for a wine tasting overlooking the château, where you
  can linger until sunset. The menu includes the local Vin de Pays des Coteaux
  de l'Auxois, served with salads, cold cuts and cheese, or a picnic option with
  organic regional produce. The shop also sells regional wines and delicacies to
  take home, plus a fine collection of books by and about the regional author
  Henri Vincenot.
