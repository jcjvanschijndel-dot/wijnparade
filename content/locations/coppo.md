description_en: >-
  Coppo has made wine in Canelli since 1892, with cellars in
  eighteenth-century tuff tunnels beneath the town that became a UNESCO
  World Heritage Site in 2014. The Pomorosso, a Barbera first made in
  1984, is considered one of the iconic wines of the Nizza appellation.


  A visit includes tasting the Pomorosso and other estate wines in the
  underground cellars. Recommended for anyone wanting to combine the
  UNESCO cellars of Canelli with a Barbera tasting.
