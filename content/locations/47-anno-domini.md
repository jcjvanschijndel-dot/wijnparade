description_en: >-
  The estate now covers 70 hectares of vineyards on fertile, clay-rich soil full
  of mineral salts: difficult terrain to work, which gives the wines structure
  and sweetness. All the vineyards are certified organic. The wines come
  entirely from the Veneto: Prosecco, Pinot Grigio, Chardonnay, Sauvignon,
  Glera, Raboso and Merlot, with the elegance and consistency that the clay soil
  and the local Libeccio wind give the grapes as their special signature. Tours
  and tastings by reservation. The winery is on the Treviso Mare road and is the
  ideal place to taste and buy the wines on the spot. Easy to combine with a
  visit to the historic centre of Treviso, the prosecco hills of
  Conegliano-Valdobbiadene (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) and the Venetian
  lagoon, all less than an hour's drive away.
