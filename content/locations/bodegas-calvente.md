description_en: >-
  The vineyards lie high in the Sierra del Chaparral, between 700 and 1,000
  metres. Old Moscatel de Alejandría vines grow here, some more than 90 years
  old, on steep, chalky slopes that give low yields but intense quality. Visits
  are personal and intimate: the winery is relatively small and tours are often
  given by members of the family themselves. The highlight is often the setting:
  tastings take place on a terrace overlooking the valley and the dramatic
  mountain landscape of the Sierra de Lújar. The tour lasts about two hours and
  includes a vineyard visit, a cellar tour and a tasting of five wines with
  local tapas for €25 per person. Children are welcome, with their own tasting
  programme for €10. Tours are available in Spanish and English.
