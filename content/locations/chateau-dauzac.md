description_en: >-
  Château Dauzac in Labarde was classified as a Fifth Growth of Margaux in 1855.

  in the middle of 120 hectares of meadows and woodland.


  On a guided tour you taste the house's wines. You can stay at La Chartreuse de
  Dauzac, a renovated guest house on the estate with four luxury rooms
  overlooking the park or the lake. Recommended for anyone who wants to explore
  Margaux and the Médoc from a classified growth.
