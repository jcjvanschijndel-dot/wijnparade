description_en: >-
  Fitapreta, near the Morgado da Oliveira palace in Évora, combines old and
  new: what is believed to be Évora's oldest wine cellar was discovered on
  the grounds. Fitapreta's new cellar building follows the proportions of
  the palace without copying its dimensions.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's wines, made by winemaker António
  Maçanita. Recommended for anyone wanting to see modern and traditional
  Alentejo winemaking side by side.
