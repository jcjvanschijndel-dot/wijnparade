  Named Winemaker of the Year twice, by Eichelmann and by Gault Millau, in 2014.
  Philipp Wittmann makes biodynamic Riesling in Westhofen, Rheinhessen, with a
  precision that amazes wine lovers worldwide. The family has grown wine since
  1663: 350+ vintages from more than 15 generations are rooted in the cellar,
  literally, as the casks date from 1890. Visits by appointment in the modern
  but warm cellar with vaults from 1829 and 80 wooden casks, each holding the
  harvest from a single plot. Four Grosse Lagen, Aulerde, Kirchspiel, Morstein
  and Brunnenhäuschen, each with an entirely different character.
