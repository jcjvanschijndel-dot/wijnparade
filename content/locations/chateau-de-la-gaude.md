description_en: >-
  Ten minutes' drive from the centre of Aix-en-Provence, Château de la Gaude is
  tucked away in the hills of the Pinchinats district, sheltered from the
  mistral and overlooking the Montagne Sainte-Victoire. The 18th-century bastide
  is a protected Monument Historique and combines a wine estate, a five-star
  hotel and Relais & Châteaux member, a spa, an artists' residency and four
  restaurants under one roof. Tours of the estate (32 hectares in total), the
  vineyards and the wine cellar with more than 40,000 bottles: the wine route
  takes you from vineyard to bottle, with a guided tasting of five wines in
  English. Four restaurants, something for everyone: Le Art (one Michelin star:
  seasonal Provençal cooking with Japanese influences, chef Matthieu Dupuis
  Baumal), Kaiseki (a gastronomic Japanese restaurant inspired by the Kyoto
  region), La Source (a chic brasserie with tapas and street food for a casual
  lunch or dinner) and B'Art (an outdoor bar with terrace for a relaxed aperitif
  in the shade). The estate's own wines (AOP Coteaux d'Aix-en-Provence,
  organically grown) are available in all the restaurants.
