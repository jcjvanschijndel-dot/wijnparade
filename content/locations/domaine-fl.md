description_en: >-
  Domaine FL, short for Fournier-Longchamps, has vineyards on both sides of the
  Loire near Rochefort-sur-Loire, on land that has been home to vines since the
  twelfth century. Here chenin blanc is king. From this single grape the estate
  makes dry Savennières and Savennières Roche aux Moines, Anjou blanc, and sweet
  Coteaux du Layon and Quarts de Chaume.


  On a tour of just over an hour you discover the cellar and taste five wines;
  there is also a version with lunch. Recommended for chenin lovers, twenty-five
  minutes from Angers.
