description_en: >-
  In the Uilenburg quarter, Stijn Kuijpers opened what is often called the first
  real wine bar in Den Bosch. In 2017 Wijn bij Stijn was named Wine Bar of the
  Year, after a vote by more than 10,000 wine lovers and a jury verdict. The
  list runs to more than 400 wines, with an emphasis on Germany, Austria and
  South Africa, and around 60 wines are available by the glass.


  To go with them: chorizo croquettes, cheese, a fish platter or charcuterie, and a daily special. Recommended for wine lovers in Den Bosch.
