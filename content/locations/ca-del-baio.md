  The cantina sits in the middle of the vineyards, in one of the few natural
  amphitheatres of the Langhe. A family business for four generations: Giulio
  and Luciana Grasso run it together with their three daughters Paola, Valentina
  and Federica, who manage every aspect, from viticulture to hospitality. In
  2016 Gambero Rosso named Giulio Viticoltore dell'Anno (Winegrower of the
  Year). The estate covers 28 hectares in Barbaresco and Treiso. Tours and
  tastings are often led by a family member: a personal, welcoming experience
  that stands in stark contrast to how closed off some other top names in the
  area are.
