description_en: >-
  In Twijzel, in the east of Friesland, lies one of the northernmost vineyards
  in the Netherlands. Douwe and Jantiene Broersma have owned De Frysling since
  2009. On one and a half hectares they grow six disease-resistant grape
  varieties: Johanniter, Solaris, Souvignier Gris, Pinotin, Cabernet Cortis and
  Cabernet Noir. From these they make white wine, rosé and the sparkling
  Brûswyn and Frysante.


  The wine shop is open on Friday and Saturday afternoons from April to December. A guided tour with tasting, lasting about an hour and a half, is available by appointment. Note: sat navs often send you to Egypte 3 — follow the sign at the entrance of number 7. Recommended for anyone in Friesland who wants to see how wine is made this far north.
