description_en: >-
  Prince Ivane Mukhranbatoni returned from France in the late
  nineteenth century and built Château Mukhrani, in the Mukhrani valley
  about 50 kilometres from Tbilisi. The estate fell into disrepair
  during the Soviet period; in 2002 a group of investors restored the
  château and the vineyards. Mukhrani placed 55th in the 2026 World's
  Best Vineyards.


  A visit includes tasting the wines in the restored château, with a
  tour of the park and the vineyards. Recommended for anyone wanting to
  discover Georgian winemaking in Kartli, an hour's drive from Tbilisi.
