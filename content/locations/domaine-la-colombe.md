description_en: >-
  Domaine La Colombe in Féchy has farmed gradually biodynamically since
  1999, and the whole vineyard is now cultivated this way. The soil is
  maintained only mechanically, without chemical weedkillers, and the
  harvest is done by hand in small crates.


  The wines ferment naturally and receive only a limited dose of
  sulphur at bottling, not as standard. An address for tasting natural
  and biodynamic wine from the La Côte region.
