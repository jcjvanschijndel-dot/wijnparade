description_en: >-
  Gosset is regarded as the oldest wine house in Champagne: in 1584 Pierre
  Gosset started trading still wines in Aÿ. Today the house is based in Épernay,
  on an estate with a wooded park that has been open to the public since 2021,
  and with about one and a half kilometres of nineteenth-century cellars.


  In the winter garden there is a Champagne bar that is open all year round,
  with a terrace in the park. You can taste by the glass or, by appointment,
  visit the cellars with a tasting. There is also a shop. A relaxed address for
  a glass of Champagne in Épernay, just away from the bustle of the Avenue de
  Champagne.
