description_en: >-
  GlouGlou opened in 2015 on the Tweede van der Helststraat in De Pijp, making
  it the first natural wine bar in the Netherlands. Ten years later nothing
  about the concept has changed, and that says enough. No cocktail list, no bar
  snacks, no DJ on Fridays. Just wine, from people who are serious about it. In
  a neighbourhood that has become ever busier and more touristy, GlouGlou stays
  exactly itself: a small, charming bar with a terrace among the flowers, a bit
  like stumbling into a side street in Paris. Exclusively vin naturel: organic,
  without artificial pesticides, filtration, sulphites or other additives. The
  list is compact and changing, with a selection from France, Spain and Italy,
  and most wines are available by the glass. No extensive snack menu: cheese and
  charcuterie boards are the limit. Whatever you like, you can take home too:
  the bottles are for sale.
