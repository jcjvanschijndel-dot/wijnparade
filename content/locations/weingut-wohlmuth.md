  wijnbouw van Oostenrijk wil ontdekken.
description_en: >-
  Weingut Wohlmuth has made wine in Kitzeck im Sausal, Austria's
  highest-altitude wine community, since 1803. The family owns the Ried
  Edelschuh, one of the country's oldest documented single vineyards: it
  is already mentioned in a document from 1322.


  A visit includes tasting Rieslings and Sauvignon Blancs from the
  Sausal's slate soils, generally on weekday afternoons. Recommended for
  anyone wanting to discover both Austria's highest-altitude winemaking
  and one of its oldest documented vineyards.
