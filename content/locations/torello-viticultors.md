description_en: >-
  The Torelló family's oldest document dates from 1395 and concerns the Mas de
  la Torrevella, the site of today's Can Martí estate in Gelida. Twenty-three
  generations of the family have since grown vines here. The estate covers 135
  hectares with two medieval masias, Can Martí de Dalt and Can Martí de Baix,
  and the vineyards and olive groves are farmed organically. Torelló left the
  DO Cava and co-founded Corpinnat; the house also makes still wines in the DO
  Penedès and olive oil.


  Visits are always in small groups: through the vineyards, the masia, the
  cellar and the underground galleries, ending with a tasting. There is also a
  version with brunch. Recommended on a wine trip through the Penedès.
