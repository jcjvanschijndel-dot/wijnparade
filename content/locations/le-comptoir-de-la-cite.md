description_en: >-
  At the foot of the medieval Cité of Carcassonne stands Le Comptoir de la Cité,
  in a former Belle Époque palace that is a listed monument. The Eiffel-style
  building is now the wine bar and shop of the winegrowers of Foncalieu, a
  cooperative from the Languedoc.


  Here you taste Foncalieu's wines from across Occitanie, alongside a selection
  of local produce and rare spirits. There is a free introductory tasting of
  three wines, with no booking needed, and a private tasting with local produce
  and a view of the Cité. A lovely place to settle after visiting the fortress,
  with a terrace.
