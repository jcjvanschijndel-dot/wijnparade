description_en: >-
  In Valgatara, near Marano di Valpolicella, lies the family winery Le Bignele,
  also known as Aldrighetti. The family has worked this land for five
  generations, on about eight hectares of vineyards at around 300 metres. From
  corvina, corvinone, rondinella and molinara they make Valpolicella Classico,
  Ripasso, Amarone and Recioto.


  A tasting in the cellar includes seven wines, among them the Amarone. The
  family makes jam from its own cherries, which you get at breakfast if you stay
  here: Le Bignele also has guest rooms. A pleasant base for a wine trip through
  Valpolicella, half an hour from Verona and Lake Garda.
