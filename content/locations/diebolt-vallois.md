  One of the better Blanc de Blancs we've tasted ourselves. Prices are a bit

  vinifying Chardonnay. The dosage has been deliberately and consistently
  lowered in recent years. The wines are on the lists of top restaurants
  worldwide. The range: Blanc de Blancs (the entry wine, 100% Chardonnay, fresh
  and mineral), Blanc de Blancs Millésimé, Prestige Blanc de Blancs, Fleur de
  Passion (the top wine, from the oldest Cramant vines), Brut Intégral (no sugar
  dosage) and Brut Intégral Rosé.
