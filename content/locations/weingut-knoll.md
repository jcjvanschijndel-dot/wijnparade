description_en: >-
  The Knoll family has been growing grapes in Unterloiben, in the
  heart of the Wachau, since 1825. The estate is now run by Emmerich
  Knoll III and his brother August; Emmerich has also chaired Vinea
  Wachau, the region's trade association, since 2012. Right next to
  the winery is the Loibnerhof, one of the Wachau's best restaurants,
  which features the estate's wines on its list.


  A visit, by appointment, includes tasting the estate's Rieslings and
  Grüner Veltliners, with the option to buy wine directly at the
  estate. Recommended for anyone wanting to combine the Wachau with a
  meal at the Loibnerhof.
