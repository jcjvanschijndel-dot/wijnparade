description_en: >-
  A quarter of an hour north of Carcassonne, at the foot of the Montagne Noire,
  lies Château La Villatade. The very old wine estate covers 130 hectares of
  vineyards, olive groves, woodland and garrigue. Richard and Sophie welcome you
  themselves.


  The estate is in the Minervois. On a visit of an hour and a half you see the
  winery and hear how the wines are made, followed by a tasting of rosé, white
  and red. There is also a shorter tasting and a gourmet version. A quiet and
  personal address, easy to combine with a visit to Carcassonne.
