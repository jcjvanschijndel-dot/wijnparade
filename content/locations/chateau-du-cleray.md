description_en: >-
  Château du Cléray in Vallet is one of the oldest and largest estates in
  Muscadet, in the Nantes wine region. The Sauvion family bought it in 1935. The
  nineteenth-century château has vaulted cellars that are among the oldest in
  Sèvre et Maine. Its 95 hectares are planted mainly with melon de Bourgogne,
  the Muscadet grape.


  On a visit you walk through the vaulted cellars and then taste three wines;
  there is also a more extensive private tour. A good address to get to know
  Muscadet Sèvre et Maine, half an hour from Nantes.
