description_en: >-
  Henri Giraud in Aÿ aims to make Champagne as a great wine: from pinot noir
  from the grand cru of Aÿ, aged in oak from the nearby Argonne forest. Since
  1990 Claude Giraud has sourced oak from the Argonne and revived the
  certification of this wood; for every bottle of the Argonne cuvée new oaks are
  planted. One rosé is made in sandstone amphorae.


  On a visit you see the cellars and the tasting room, with a table made from a
  single piece of Argonne oak, and taste the house's Champagnes. A special
  address for lovers of Champagne with character, in the heart of Aÿ.
