  Het verhaal van Maison Jean-Claude Boisset begon in 1961 in Gevrey-Chambertin.

description_en: >-
  The story of Maison Jean-Claude Boisset began in 1961 in Gevrey-Chambertin.
  Today the heart of the house beats at Les Ursulines, a former convent in
  Nuits-Saint-Georges that now houses the winery. Nathalie and Jean-Charles
  Boisset, the second generation, entrusted the winemaking to Burgundian Grégory
  Patriat.


  On a visit you see the vat room and the cellar and taste the house's wines,
  which are "tailor-made" in small quantities per plot. There is also a tasting
  in the garden and one with old bottles from the family collection. Groups are
  small. A lovely address in Nuits-Saint-Georges.
