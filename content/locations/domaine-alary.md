description_en: >-
  In 1692 André Alary settled as a winegrower in Cairanne, and the
  family has never left since: today the tenth and eleventh
  generations, brothers Denis and Jean-Etienne Alary, run the 35
  hectares, farmed fully organically since 2009. Cairanne is one of
  the smaller, still relatively unknown Rhône appellations, with
  robust, spicy reds that hold their own against their more famous
  neighbours in Châteauneuf-du-Pape.


  A visit, by appointment, includes tasting the estate's wines in the
  tasting cellar. Recommended for anyone wanting to get to know
  Cairanne, and with it, ten generations of Rhône wine history.
