description_en: >-
  Bodegas Balbás dates back to 1777, when ancestor Abundio Balbás, a
  bargeman until then, decided to start making wine. The house was one
  of the eight founding bodegas of the Ribera del Duero designation and
  is now run, after six generations, by Juan José Balbás.


  A visit includes walking through the tank room and the oak-barrel
  tunnel, followed by a tasting; a visit takes about an hour and a half.
  Recommended for anyone wanting to visit one of the founding bodegas of
  Ribera del Duero.
