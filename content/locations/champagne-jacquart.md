description_en: >-
  Jacquart, founded in 1964, has for years been based at the Hôtel de Brimont on
  the Boulevard Lundy in Reims: an elegant nineteenth-century mansion in Louis
  XV style, with wrought-iron gates through which carriages once passed. To mark
  its sixtieth anniversary, the house opened its doors to the public for the
  first time.


  A tasting room has been set up in the Hôtel de Brimont, open only in season,
  from spring to early autumn. Options include a tasting of three cuvées set to
  music and a prestige tasting. A stylish address in the centre of Reims.
