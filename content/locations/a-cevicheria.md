description_en: >-
  The restaurant that put Lisbon on the international culinary map. Chef Kiko
  Martins opened A Cevicheria in 2014 in the Príncipe Real district: a small,
  always packed restaurant with an octopus hanging from the ceiling, an open
  kitchen and a menu that crosses Peruvian and Portuguese traditions. Ceviche is
  the common thread: sea bass tiradito, sea bream ceviche, prawn tempura,
  grilled octopus, bacalhau with kimchi. The wines are exclusively Portuguese,
  one of the best Portuguese wine lists in Lisbon, with an emphasis on natural
  wine and small producers from Vinho Verde, Douro, Alentejo and Dão. The menu
  changes weekly.
