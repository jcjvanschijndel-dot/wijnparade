description_en: >-
  At the start of 2000, Jonnie and Thérèse Boer bought the Spinhuis in Zwolle, a
  former women's prison, and turned it into a restaurant and hotel. De Librije
  is one of the best-known restaurants in the Netherlands, with three Michelin
  stars.


  Wine gets as much attention here as the food: with the Librije menu you choose from two wine pairings, or you pick
  a bottle from the wine list with wines from all over the world. After dinner
  you sleep at Librije's Hotel, with nineteen rooms in the old prison. Book well
  ahead. Recommended for anyone who wants to really push the boat out in Zwolle
  for once.
