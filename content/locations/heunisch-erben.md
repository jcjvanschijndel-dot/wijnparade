description_en: >-
  A colossal wine list with around 100 wines by the glass, making it unusually
  easy to taste your way through. The categories go beyond white, rosé and red:
  there's also beige, orange and pale red, plus sweet and fortified, and even
  Japanese rice wine. Prices are strikingly accessible: an eighth of a litre of
  house white from Weingut Edlinger in the Weinviertel costs just €3, while
  classics such as Chablis and Sancerre, and top red cuvées, are well
  represented too. The very crème de la crème of Austrian winemaking is on the
  list, supplemented with German (Mosel-Saar-Ruwer) and international names. The
  cooking under chef Michael Gubik is contemporary and relaxed, true to regional
  and seasonal Viennese classics (the Wiener Schnitzel gets special praise).
