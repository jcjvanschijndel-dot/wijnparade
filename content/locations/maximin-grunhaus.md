description_en: >-
  Vines have been grown on the slopes of Maximin Grünhaus for almost 2,000
  years: first by the Romans, then by the Benedictine monks of the abbey of St.
  Maximin. Since 1882 the estate has belonged to the von Schubert family, now in
  its sixth generation with Maximin von Schubert. The historic castle complex,
  at the foot of three adjoining steep south-facing slopes, is one of the most
  impressive wine landscapes in the Mosel. Tastings take place in the modern
  Vinothek in the historic press house, overlooking the monopole sites and the
  castle, and you can visit every day. Special times to visit: the open day on
  the weekend after Ascension Day and the Christmas weekend in the festively
  decorated castle.
