description_en: >-
  On the Donkere Gaard, a little street between the Domplein and the
  Oudegracht, this coffee and wine bar has been open since 2012, in an old
  building with wooden beams and a long bar. The list has more than 50 wines by
  the glass and over 80 by the bottle, mostly from the Old World and partly
  organic, biodynamic or natural.


  During the day Talud9 is also a good spot for coffee. With the wine you can order snacks, or enough to make a meal of it; the High Wine is by reservation only. Upstairs there is a room for a private drinks party or wine tasting. Recommended for a glass of wine in the heart of Utrecht's city centre.
