description_en: >-
  Charles Gabriel de Cazanove founded his Champagne house in 1811 in Avize, in
  the Côte des Blancs. That makes it one of the oldest Champagne houses still in
  operation. In the nineteenth century it supplied the Russian court and
  aristocratic tables across Europe; later the house moved to Reims.


  On the Place de la République in Reims you can taste in a modern boutique, or
  take a visit with explanations about blending and ageing on the laths,
  followed by a tasting of Champagnes from the three grapes. A good address for
  a Champagne tasting in the middle of Reims.
