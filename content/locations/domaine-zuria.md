description_en: >-
  On the limestone plateau of Bonifacio, in the far south of Corsica, lies the
  young wine estate Zuria. The Zuria family began replanting vines on their
  ancestors' land in 2010. The eleven hectares of limestone soil are planted
  only with Corsican grape varieties, such as sciaccarellu and vermentinu, grown
  organically.


  The estate makes red, white and rosé. In the new winery at the entrance to
  Bonifacio, built in 2021, you can taste and buy the wines. A surprising
  address for anyone who wants to get to know the island's wines on a holiday in
  Corsica.
