description_en: >-
  In the small village of Ehrenhausen, in the middle of the South Styrian wine
  region, chef Gerhard Fuchs and sommelier Christian Zach have created something
  special. Die Weinbank combines a traditional Wirtshaus, a restaurant with four
  Gault Millau toques and an exceptional wine bar under one roof. The Vinothek
  is where wine lovers really fall silent. Voted the best wine bar in Austria
  (Falstaff Bar Guide 2026, 97/100) and with one of the best wine lists in the
  country (Star Wine List), the Weinbank has more than 5,100 wines on its list.
  Sommelier Christian Zach, also known as "the Zachinator", won Falstaff
  Sommelier of the Year 2017, Gault Millau Sommelier of the Year 2021 and the
  Michelin Sommelier Award 2026. He brings bottles to the table that you'll
  hardly find anywhere outside his own cellar.
