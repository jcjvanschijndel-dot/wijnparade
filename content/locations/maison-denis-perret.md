  Founded in 1974 on the initiative of five of the biggest names in Beaune
  (Bouchard Père & Fils, Chanson, Joseph Drouhin, Louis Jadot and Louis Latour),
  Maison Denis Perret is now an institution in the town. It is no ordinary wine
  shop: it is the joint calling card of the five largest négociants of Beaune, a

  Every Burgundian village is represented, from Beaujolais to Chablis via the
  Côte de Beaune and the Côte de Nuits, with more than 300 wines at domaine
  prices. A special display case shows exceptional vintages. You can also order
  online and have bottles shipped.
