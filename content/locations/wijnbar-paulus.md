description_en: >-
  An Amsterdam classic for more than ten years, although De Pijp more often
  gives its attention to the trendier natural wine bar GlouGlou round the
  corner. Unfairly so, says Star Wine List, because Paulus is a fixture that has
  earned its place. The atmosphere is like a cosy living room with a retro 1960s
  interior: you feel at home, as if you're visiting a friend. There are always 8
  to 10 whites and 8 to 10 reds by the glass, exclusively European, with a focus
  on classic regions supplemented with interesting finds from Israel, Lebanon
  and Greece. Ask for the "little black book" of special old vintages. Sharing
  boards, Ibérico ham and luxury bites alongside. Recognised by Star Wine List.
  A terrace on the street in fine weather.
