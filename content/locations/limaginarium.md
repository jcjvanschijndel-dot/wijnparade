  Grands Crus tussen Dijon en Beaune.
description_en: >-
  L'Imaginarium in Nuits-Saint-Georges is a playful introduction to the wines of
  Burgundy. In "La Magie des Bulles" you discover interactively how sparkling
  wine such as crémant de Bourgogne is made. This is followed by "Sacrée
  Vigne!", a sound and light show about the vine and the people who work with
  it, told through old winegrowing tools.


  The visit ends with a guided tasting, for example of five Burgundies from
  crémant to premier cru. There is also a large shop. A fun and accessible
  outing, even for those who know little about wine, on the Route des Grands
  Crus between Dijon and Beaune.
