description_en: >-
  Enoteca Alessi is in the heart of Florence's old town, a stone's throw from
  the Duomo. Opened in 1952 and always run by the Alessi family since. More than
  two thousand Italian and international wine labels are available to taste on
  the ground floor, while the upper floor houses a wine cellar reminiscent of
  old cellars. In the unique tasting room, surrounded by old vaults and wooden
  beams, you can sit, relax and chat over a lovely glass of wine and a plate of
  cured meats and Tuscan cheese. Vertical tastings of local wineries such as
  Mormoreto can regularly be booked. Chocolate, sweets, coffee, grappa, whisky,
  gin, extra virgin olive oil and balsamic vinegar are also for sale.
