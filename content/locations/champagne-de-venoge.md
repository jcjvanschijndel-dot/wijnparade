description_en: >-
  De Venoge was founded in 1837 in Mareuil-sur-Aÿ and is now based in a
  nineteenth-century mansion on the Avenue de Champagne in Épernay. The Princes
  Champagnes, in the carafe-shaped bottle, were created in 1864 as a tribute to
  the Princes of Orange. The top cuvée is Louis XV, named after the king who in
  1728 allowed Champagne to be transported in bottles.


  On a tour you see the mansion, the vinothèque with old vintages and the
  English garden, and taste the Princes and optionally the Louis XV in the
  salon. An elegant address for a Champagne tasting on Épernay's best-known
  street.
