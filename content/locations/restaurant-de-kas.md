description_en: >-
  In 2001 Gert Jan Hageman opened a restaurant in a fully restored 1926
  municipal nursery greenhouse in Park Frankendael. "From plant to plate" was
  already the motto then. What seemed a bold choice grew into one of the most
  respected restaurants in Amsterdam. One Michelin star + Green Star, 16.5
  points from Gault&Millau, Top 50 Best Discovery, and number 1 in Time Out's
  best restaurants in Amsterdam 2025. 300 varieties of vegetables, herbs and
  fruit, grown in the greenhouse next to the restaurant and on their own farm in
  the Beemster: harvested in the morning, served in the afternoon. The menu
  changes weekly. Wines and beers are selected with a focus on sustainability
  and minimal intervention, chosen from winemaker friends who work the same way
  as the restaurant. No wine pairing, but the sommelier guides you by the bottle
  or by the glass.
