description_en: >-
  This isn't a place full of bells and whistles, but a reliable address for
  anyone looking for traditional Langhe cooking without the top prices or the
  crowds of some of the better-known starred places nearby. The menu leans
  heavily on Piedmontese classics: vitello tonnato, knife-cut raw beef fillet
  (carne cruda), agnolotti del plin, taglierini with sausage ragù, gnocchi with
  Castelmagno or Raschera cheese, and stracotto (braised beef) in Nebbiolo. The
  wine list has more than 300 labels, with Piedmont (and especially La Morra
  itself, almost complete) naturally in the foreground, but also with excursions
  to Alto Adige, Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Austria and Germany: a surprisingly
  broad selection for an osteria of this kind, with many available by the glass.
