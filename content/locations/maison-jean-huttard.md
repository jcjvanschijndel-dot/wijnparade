  Since 1860 the Huttard family has been making wine in Zellenberg, a hilltop
  village on the Alsace Wine Route between Riquewihr and Ribeauvillé. Brother

  eight hectares of vines are farmed organically.


  The house makes the classic Alsace wines, including riesling from the grand
  cru Schoenenbourg. You can come for a tasting or a relaxed moment at the
