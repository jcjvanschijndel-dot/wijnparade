description_en: >-
  The Cattin family has been making wine in Voegtlinshoffen, ten minutes south
  of Colmar, since 1720. The family business makes a wide range of Alsace wines,
  including grands crus such as Hatschbourg, and crémant.


  The eye-catcher is Le Belvédère, the new winery with a wine bar on the roof.
  From the large terrace you look out over the vineyards, the Vosges, the Alsace
  plain, the Black Forest and on a clear day even the Swiss Alps. You can take a
  tour, with explanations of every step of winemaking and a guided tasting. The
  bar has limited opening in winter.
