description_en: >-
  The Martinenga vineyard has been owned since 1797 by the noble Cisa Asinari di
  Grésy family, one of the most respected names in the Langhe. Even the
  legendary Bruno Giacosa, one of the greatest Barbaresco winemakers of all
  time, regularly visited the estate to buy grapes, until the 1970s, when the
  young Alberto di Grésy decided the time had come to put the family name on the
  label. Surrounded by the enchanting vineyard amphitheatre of Martinenga, in
  the heart of the historic winery and barrique cellar, visitors can choose from
  several tasting experiences, for small groups only and by appointment only.
