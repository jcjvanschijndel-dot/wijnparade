description_en: >-
  Since 1920 the Bauchet family has worked its vineyards in Bisseuil, passed
  down from father to son. The estate has grown to about 34 hectares, spread
  over seven terroirs in the Côte des Blancs, the Montagne de Reims and the Côte
  des Bar. The main grapes are chardonnay and pinot noir.


  After a visit to the cellars you taste a selection of cuvées, including a
  vintage Champagne. There is also a sensory tasting. A good address near Aÿ and
  Mareuil-sur-Aÿ for anyone who wants to get to know a family estate with many
  different terroirs.
