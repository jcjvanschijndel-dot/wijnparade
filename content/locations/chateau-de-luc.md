description_en: >-
  Since 1605 the Fabre family has been making wine in the Languedoc, now for
  more than fourteen generations and at five châteaux. Château de Luc, in
  Luc-sur-Orbieu in the Corbières, is the family home. In 1991 Louis Fabre was a
  pioneer of organic viticulture in the region; all the wines are organic.


  On a visit you see the château, with an old castle kitchen, and descend into
  the centuries-old underground cellar. You then taste five organic wines in the
  former stables, from the Corbières, Boutenac and the Languedoc. Recommended
  between Narbonne and Carcassonne.
