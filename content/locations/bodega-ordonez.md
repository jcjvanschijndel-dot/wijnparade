description_en: >-
  Bodegas Jorge Ordóñez Málaga was founded in 2004 with the aim of reviving
  centuries of winemaking tradition in Málaga, destroyed by the phylloxera
  plague in the 19th century. The Moscatel de Alejandría grape was brought to
  Málaga by Phoenician merchants 3,000 years ago. Jorge Ordóñez has played a key
  role in promoting this fascinating region, where artisan grape growing is
  essential, by developing new wine categories.


  A walk through the vineyards reveals the steep slopes where the vines grow in this unique corner of the Axarquía. Guided tours available for groups of 4 to 60 people, by prior reservation.
