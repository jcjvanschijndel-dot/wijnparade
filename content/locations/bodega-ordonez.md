description_en: >-
  Bodegas Quitapenas was founded in 1880 by Francisco Suárez Pineda, who let
  everyone drink on credit — making it the meeting point for everyone travelling
  to Málaga from across the province. Hence the name: "the house of the one who
  takes our worries away." After nearly 150 years of history, Quitapenas is
  still 100% family-owned, now run by the fifth generation.


  Every visit takes you through the history of Málaga wines and of Bodegas Quitapenas itself, with stories about the cellar and production methods. Four visit formats: Visita Quitapenas (1 hour, 3 wines), Visita Momentos (1 hour, 3 wines + cheese), Jamón y Vino (1 hour, 3 wines + Ibérico ham) and the Initiation Tasting (2 hours, 4 wines + masterclass).
