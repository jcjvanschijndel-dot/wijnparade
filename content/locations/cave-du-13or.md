description_en: >-
  At the foot of Reims cathedral, in the former 12th-century Trésorier building,
  one of the oldest buildings in the city, Le 13OR opened as a crossroads of two
  worlds: champagne artisans and Champagne artists. An intimate champagne bar at
  the counter (by the glass or by the bottle), a boutique with 200 champagnes
  from growers and big houses plus local regional products, guided and
  self-guided tastings in three formats, and a changing art gallery with work by
  local artists. The atmosphere is reminiscent of the Montmartre cafés of the
  early 20th century, where ideas, encounters and collaborations are born. A
  refreshing alternative to the busy tourist cafés surrounding the cathedral.
