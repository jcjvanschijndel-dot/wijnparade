description_en: >-
  In the heart of Chiroubles, one of the ten Beaujolais crus, the Savoye family
  has been making wine for six generations, from father to son. The estate has

  makes Régnié and Morgon.


  In the tasting cellar in the village you taste these three crus side by side,
  a good way to learn the difference. There is also a walk through the vineyards
  with a picnic. A friendly and affordable address for a wine tasting in
  Beaujolais.
