description_en: >-
  An entire medieval hamlet has been transformed into a relais with 58 suites, 2
  restaurants, 4 pools and a wine cellar. Horse riding through the vineyards,
  truffle hunting, wine tastings in the historic cellar, tennis courts and a
  complete Tuscan village life. The architecture of the wine cellar is
  spectacular. Visitors who aren't staying can also come for a wine tour and
  tasting. Two restaurants: Il Borro Tuscan Bistro, the more informal,
  cosmopolitan part of the culinary offering, where the Tuscan cooking tradition
  meets a convivial, modern style with simple, elegant dishes rooted in the
  season (marinated beetroot carpaccio with ricotta, pappa al pomodoro, ricotta
  and spinach gnudi with pecorino and truffles), and the Osteria del Borro for
  more formal dining. 45 hectares of vineyards lie at 300 to 500 metres in the
  Pratomagno hills. For Petruna, both fermentation and ageing take place in
  amphorae, making it one of the reference wines of the Valdarno di Sopra
  region. The estate produces its own olive oil, vegetables and herbs.
