description_en: >-
  Doctor Schepel from Gulpen planted a thousand vines on the slopes by the
  Sinselbeek stream in Wahlwiller in 1971, one of the first modern
  vineyards in the Netherlands. After his death in 1985, management
  changed hands several times, until Wim Hendriks and Leny van Bavel took
  up the old traditions again from 1999.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's Wittemer wine. Recommended for
  anyone wanting to visit one of the oldest vineyards in Limburg.
