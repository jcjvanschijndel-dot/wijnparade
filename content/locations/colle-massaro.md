description_en: >-
  The estate covers 1,200 hectares, with 120 hectares of vineyard and almost 90
  hectares of olive groves, some trees more than 900 years old. Here you can
  book a cellar tour including a tasting. In addition, through the neighbouring
  Wine Relais, there's: a tour of the "cantina d'autore", part of the Toscana
  Wine Architecture circuit, with a guided tasting of 3 wines; a more extensive
  experience with a tasting of 4 wines from the various ColleMassari Estates
  together with the winemaker himself; and a tour of the estate by 4x4,
  accompanied by the estate's own agronomist, followed by a tasting of 3 wines,
  which is fun if you also want to see the landscape (with views towards Monte
  Amiata and even the island of Montecristo). In summer (late July/August) there
  are special evening tours with dinner, cellar visit and tasting from 19.30.
