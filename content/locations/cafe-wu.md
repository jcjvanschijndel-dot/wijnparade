description_en: >-
  In 2024 Timo de Beurs opened Café Wu on the Dapperstraat in Amsterdam East: a
  Chinese-inspired bistro with a distinctive voice of its own. Chinese finesse,
  French techniques and produce, and a wine list put together by sommelier Chi
  Ling Wu. Recognised by Star Wine List and by Gault&Millau. In the evening
  elegant vinyl plays in the background and the restaurant subtly turns into an
  intimate wine bar. Traditional Chinese cooking with a French interpretation:
  prawn broth with home-made wontons, bone marrow "Hawaii" with sweet-and-sour
  crispy fried sweetbreads, tiger choux bun with milky oolong and pistachio
  crème mousseline. The wine list is small but spot on: European, with an
  emphasis on France, Germany and Italy, and strong in natural wine and light
  French styles. The staff pour without fuss and know exactly what works with
  each dish.
