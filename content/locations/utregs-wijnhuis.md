  Achter de Dom, op een paar stappen van de Domtoren, zit sinds 2014 deze

Een aanrader voor wie in de Utrechtse binnenstad een fles wil uitzoeken met advies erbij.
description_en: >-
  Behind the Dom, a few steps from the Dom Tower, this wine shop has been open
  since 2014. The range runs from special, exclusive wines to affordable
  everyday bottles, with a wide choice of organic wines.


  You get personal advice here, and the shop regularly organises tastings, also at other places in the city — such as blind tasting sessions at the wine bar VinVin and a pop-up wine bar at Café-Restaurant Terroir. Recommended for anyone in Utrecht's city centre who wants to choose a bottle with some advice.
