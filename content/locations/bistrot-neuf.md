description_en: >-
  The concept is honest and clear: a French restaurant built on wine. Not the
  kind where wine is an afterthought; here the wine is the starting point of the
  menu. Star Wine List White Star, and By the Glass List of the Year at the
  International Open 2024. Open seven days a week, from lunch until late in the
  evening. Traditional French bistro cooking with seasonal specials: sea bream,
  leg of lamb, stuffed quail, clafoutis, eel mousse, tarte tatin. Every month a
  new chef's menu entirely tuned to seasonal produce. The wine cellar holds
  hundreds of top European wines, 65 of them by the glass, from simple to
  special. The staff are happy to help you choose, with Amsterdam cheek and real
  knowledge. You can also just have a glass and a French snack at the bar,
  without a full dinner.
