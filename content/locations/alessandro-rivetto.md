description_en: >-
  In Annunziata, below La Morra, Alessandro Rivetto welcomes visitors to his
  cellar with its large oak barrels. He makes Barolo from vineyards between La
  Morra and Serralunga d'Alba, including Lazzarito and Vigna Rionda, as well as
  Barbaresco and other wines from the Langhe.


  The tastings are personal, often with Alessandro himself, and relaxed: serious
  about wine, without taking themselves too seriously. There is a classic
  tasting of three wines, a tasting of Barolo and Barbaresco crus and a vertical
  tasting of Barolo. Booking is required. Recommended on a wine trip through the
  Barolo region.
