description_en: >-
  Just outside Radda in Chianti, in the heart of Chianti Classico, lies Castello
  di Radda. The estate has some forty hectares of vineyards and is fully
  certified organic. It works with native grapes, above all sangiovese, and
  vinifies them in the traditional way. The flagship is the Chianti Classico
  Gran Selezione Vigna il Corno, from a single vineyard.


  You can choose between a short tasting of three wines, a cellar tour with a
  tasting, or an extended tasting with a series of riservas and the Vigna il
  Corno. A good address for a wine tasting in Chianti, easy to combine with a
  walk through medieval Radda.
