  Château de Pommard ligt rond de Clos Marey-Monge: twintig hectare pinot noir

  Bij een bezoek leer je de verschillende terroirs van de clos kennen, van de

  de Route des Grands Crus, op een paar minuten van Beaune.
description_en: >-
  Château de Pommard surrounds the Clos Marey-Monge: twenty hectares of pinot
  noir within a single wall, divided into several plots, each with its own soil.
  Since 2014 the estate has belonged to the American Carabello-Baum family, who
  converted it to biodynamic viticulture. The 2021 vintage was the first to be
  certified organic and biodynamic.


  On a visit you get to know the different terroirs of the clos, from grape to
  glass, with explanations from trained wine advisors, and taste the château's
  wines. Booking is required. One of the best-known addresses on the Route des
  Grands Crus, a few minutes from Beaune.
