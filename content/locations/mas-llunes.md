description_en: >-
  Mas Llunes is a family business in Garriguella founded by the Roig family, who
  have a long tradition as winegrowers: their roots go back to 1331, and the
  family has worked the land of Garriguella for centuries. In 2000 they started
  a modern wine project, driven by the conviction that the Empordà is the
  perfect place to make wines of character and quality. The Sensory Wine Tasting
  concept was named the best innovative wine tourism experience at the Premis
  Vinari d'Enoturisme 2024, and the house received the award for Best Winery
  with a wine tourism offering from the Associació Catalana de Sommeliers. The
  most special experience is the sensory wine tasting with music, light and
  images: an immersive tasting that involves all the senses. There's also a
  historic walk to the airfield vineyard and the Spanish Civil War bunkers
  surrounding the estate, a unique combination of winegrowing and local history.
  An organic vineyard tour along the Albera mountain range is also possible,
  with an explanation of biodynamic winegrowing and the lunar calendar system.
