  Opgericht in 1908 (als fusie van de coöperaties van Gries en Santa Maddalena,

  Een rondleiding begint bij de plek waar de druiven tijdens de oogst binnenkomen, en voert je langs de verschillende wijngaardlocaties: de rode druiven (Cabernet tot Lagrein) rijpen lager, in Gries, San Maurizio, Settequerce, Santa Maddalena en Coste; de witte druiven (Pinot Grigio tot Gewürztraminer) iets hoger, bij Missiano, San Giorgio, Santa Giustina en Renon. Er worden zo'n veertig verschillende wijnen gemaakt, verdeeld over zes lijnen (Riserva, Election, Classici, Collection, Bio en Dessert) — tijdens een proeverij proef je een selectie uit meerdere collecties, vaak zo'n vijftien tot zestien wijnen in kleine slokjes. Veel van de wijnen zijn bekroond door onder meer Gambero Rosso, Robert Parker, Decanter en The Wine Hunter.

description_en: >-
  Founded in 1908 (as the merger of the cooperatives of Gries and Santa
  Maddalena, officially united in 2001), this is the beating heart of a
  cooperative of more than two hundred winegrowers who together farm over three
  hundred hectares of beautifully located vineyards around Bolzano. It is housed
  in a striking, modern cube in the middle of the city. A tour begins where the
  grapes arrive during the harvest and takes you past the different vineyard
  sites: the red grapes (from Cabernet to Lagrein) ripen lower down, in Gries,
  San Maurizio, Settequerce, Santa Maddalena and Coste; the white grapes (from
  Pinot Grigio to Gewürztraminer) a little higher, at Missiano, San Giorgio,
  Santa Giustina and Renon. Around forty different wines are made, divided over
  six lines (Riserva, Election, Classici, Collection, Bio and Dessert). In a
  tasting you try a selection from several collections, often around fifteen to
  sixteen wines in small sips. Many of the wines have won awards from Gambero
  Rosso, Robert Parker, Decanter and The Wine Hunter, among others.
