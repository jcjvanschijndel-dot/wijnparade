description_en: >-
  Ruinart was founded in 1729 by Nicolas Ruinart and is regarded as the oldest
  Champagne house. The house is famous for its chardonnay, with the Blanc de
  Blancs as its best-known wine. Beneath the house on the Rue des Crayères in
  Reims lie chalk quarries up to some forty metres deep, spread over three
  levels and kilometres of galleries; they are on the UNESCO World Heritage
  List.


  A visit takes you through these impressive crayères and ends with a tasting of
  two Champagnes, including the Blanc de Blancs. Places are limited, so book
  through Ruinart's own website and check availability in advance. A highlight
  of any Champagne trip to Reims.
