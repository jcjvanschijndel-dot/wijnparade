description_en: >-
  Just outside Lemelerveld, in Overijssel, Luigi Mascini and
  Annemarie Uhlenbeck run a B&B in the middle of their own vineyard. Six grape
  varieties grow here, from which they make white wine, a summery rosé and
  sometimes a red wine as well.


  You can taste and buy the wines at the estate. You can stay in one of the four
  rooms or in the detached wine chalet with two bedrooms, for up to four people.
  De Wijnstaete is a good base for walking and cycling: the Lemelerberg is less
  than three kilometres away. Recommended for anyone who wants to sleep among
  the vines in Overijssel.
