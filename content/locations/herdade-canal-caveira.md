description_en: >-
  Herdade Canal Caveira lies on the Alentejo coast, at the meeting point of
  the Setúbal and Alentejo wine regions, surrounded by the Serra de
  Grândola. The coastal maritime influence gives the 45-hectare property,
  of which 25 hectares are vineyard, its own microclimate.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's wines, among the olive trees,
  orange trees and cork oaks. Recommended for anyone wanting to discover
  the Alentejo's coastal winemaking.
