description_en: >-
  A champagne bar as atypical as its name promises. In the centre of Épernay, a
  few minutes' walk from the Avenue de Champagne, L'Atypique opened in a unique
  space: a 150 m² inner garden that feels like a green oasis in the middle of
  town. Not a tourist attraction but a real bar with live music, a terrace as
  soon as the weather allows, and a programme of concerts and events.
  Exclusively champagnes from independent growers, the small producers you won't
  come across at the big houses on the Avenue, supplemented with a selection of
  wines, cocktails, beers and hot drinks. The assiettes gourmandes are prepared
  to order with fresh produce: cheese and charcuterie boards and local regional
  products.
