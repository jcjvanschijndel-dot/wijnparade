description_en: >-
  Deep beneath Sant Sadurní d'Anoia lie the cellars that Joan Juvé Baqués and
  Teresa Camps Ferrer dug out under their house: today six levels of tunnels
  at a constant temperature, where the bottles in the racks are still turned
  by hand. The family firm's vineyards are mainly on Finca Espiells, around
  200 hectares of xarel·lo, macabeo, chardonnay and pinot noir on
  clay-limestone soils, sheltered by the Montserrat massif. Since 2010 the
  vineyards have been converting to organic.





  Its best-known label is the Reserva de la Familia Brut Nature Gran Reserva:
  originally made only for family and friends, on sale since 1976 and one of
  the pioneers of the brut nature style in Spain. On a visit you walk through
  the historic cellars and taste in La Capella. Recommended on a wine trip
  through the Penedès.
