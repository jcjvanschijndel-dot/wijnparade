description_en: >-
  The estate is 8 km from the medieval fortified town of Issigeac and 10 km from
  Bergerac, in a rolling landscape of vineyards, maize fields and oak woods.
  Guided tastings of the organic wines, across four cuvées with distinct
  character. Visitors are shown around the various plots and the wine cellar.
  Booking is recommended, especially in high season. The estate's Monbazillac is
  one of the best in the appellation: made from botrytis-affected Sémillon and
  Muscadelle, harvested in several selective passes through the vineyard, and
  aged in new oak barrels.
