description_en: >-
  In the hamlet of San Rocco Seno d'Elvio, a few kilometres outside Alba, lies
  Tenuta Baràc. The estate covers more than 25 hectares, 12 of which are
  organically farmed vineyards. Its wines include Barbaresco Rocche Massalupo,
  Langhe Nebbiolo, Barbera d'Alba Superiore and a Langhe Rosato.


  At its heart is a farmhouse from the early twentieth century, restored with
  the original materials. You can visit the cellar and taste the wines, with
  views over the hills of the Langhe. Baràc is also an agriturismo: you sleep in
  complete peace among the vineyards. Recommended for anyone who wants to
  combine Alba, Barbaresco and Barolo on a wine trip through Piedmont.
