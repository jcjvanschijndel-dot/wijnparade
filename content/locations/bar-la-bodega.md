description_en: >-
  The restaurant is housed in a historic building that once served as a
  storehouse and stable, at the foot of the iconic fortress walls leading up to
  Dalt Vila. It has since become one of the best-loved places on the island,
  with locals as well as loyal visitors who come back every year. The wine list
  has more than 60 wines, 80% of them Spanish, with a clear focus on the home
  product. Recommended from the menu: roast duck, provoleta, acorn-fed Ibérico
  ham from DO Los Pedroches, octopus, ceviche and tortilla. For a sweet finish:
  the home-made tarte tatin or artisan ice cream. Home-made Hierbas Ibicencas is
  a permanent part of the offering. A lightly chilled glass of Rioja while you
  watch the crowds: an evening at La Bodega needs nothing more.
