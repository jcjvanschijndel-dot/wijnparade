description_en: >-
  Quinta do Passadouro has stood on the banks of the Douro near Pinhão
  since the eighteenth century and has always been known for its Port
  wine. Since 2019 the estate has been owned by Quinta do Noval; the
  same property is also home to Wine & Soul, the small-scale project
  of Jorge Serôdio Borges and Sandra Tavares da Silva.


  A visit, by appointment, includes tasting the estate's Port and
  Douro wines amid the vineyards. Recommended for anyone wanting to
  see the Cima Corgo, the heart of Port wine production, up close.
