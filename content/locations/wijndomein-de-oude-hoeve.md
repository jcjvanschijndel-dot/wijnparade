description_en: >-
  On the edge of the De Groote Heide nature reserve, close to the Dutch border,
  lies the De Oude Hoeve wine estate in Achel. Daniëlla Habraken's parents
  planted the vineyard in 2000; she took it over together with Robert
  Schmiehusen, and they trained in viticulture to do so. On about half a hectare
  they grow disease-resistant grapes: from Seyval Blanc and Phoenix they make a
  fresh, dry white wine, and from Léon Millot and Maréchal Foch a red. The wine
  is made in stainless steel tanks, with as little sulphite as possible.


  You can stay in the guest apartment above the wine house, with a fireplace
  and a private terrace, or from April to the end of September in a glamping
  tent among the vines. The estate is close to the cycling network and the
  walking trails of De Groote Heide. A lovely address for a weekend of wine and
  nature in the Belgian Kempen.
