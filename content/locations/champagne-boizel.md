description_en: >-
  In 1834 Auguste Boizel and Julie Martin founded their Champagne house in
  Épernay. It is still in family hands, now run by the brothers Florent and
  Lionel, the sixth generation. In the historic cellars on the Avenue de
  Champagne, which are on the UNESCO World Heritage List, there are still
  bottles from the founding year.


  A visit starts in the cellar with its oak casks, passes the vat room and ends
  with a tasting, for example of the prestige cuvée Joyau de France. Groups are
  small. A lovely family house to visit on a walk along the Avenue de Champagne.
