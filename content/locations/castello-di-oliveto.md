description_en: >-
  On a hilltop near Castelfiorentino, between Florence and San Gimignano, stands
  Castello di Oliveto. It was built in 1424 for the Florentine Pucci family and
  is more a fortified villa than a castle, with moats, walls and towers. The
  name comes from the olive groves around it.


  Today the castle is at the heart of a wine and olive oil estate. On a guided
  tour you see the frescoed rooms and the historic cellars and taste five wines,
  from Chianti to Vin Santo. You can also stay here, as an agriturismo. An
  atmospheric address for a wine tasting in Tuscany, between Florence and Siena.
