description_en: >-
  Château Bouscaut in Cadaujac is one of the six châteaux that were awarded cru
  classé de Graves status for both red and white in 1959. It lies on the
  southern edge of the Pessac-Léognan appellation, a twenty-minute drive from
  Bordeaux. Sophie Lurton-Cogombles and her husband Laurent have run the estate
  since 1992. The red wine is a blend of merlot, cabernet sauvignon and malbec,
  the white of sauvignon blanc and sémillon.


  On a visit you see the winery and the new barrel cellar, followed by a
  tasting. There are also workshops, for example on food and wine pairing or on
  blending: putting together your own blend. You can stay at Château Valoux, the
  estate's holiday home overlooking the vineyards. A good address for a wine
  tasting close to Bordeaux.
