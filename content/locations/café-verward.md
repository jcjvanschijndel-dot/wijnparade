description_en: >-
  Ward de Zeeuw and Bas Fraterman have known each other for years: they worked
  together at IJsclub Kralingen and then at restaurant Dertien. Ward trained as
  a wine specialist and worked as a sommelier at In Den Rustwat and Eendracht.
  Bas found his calling in cooking. Their shared dream: a café that stands out
  for personality, knowledge and quality. Not a beer café that also pours some
  wine, but not a stiff wine bar either. In the summer of 2016 they opened
  Verward on the Hoogstraat, and filled exactly that niche: coffee, craft beer,
  good wine and honest food under one roof. Ward pours by the glass or advises
  based on what you want yourself: no fixed wine list but a personal approach.
  Many wines are available by the glass, exclusive bottles by the bottle.
  Particularly strong in natural wines. Bas makes bites that rise far above the
  café average: fish tartare, lamb meatballs with rosemary mustard, snails,
  squid, pork belly. Six beers on tap, all from Rotterdam breweries. Soft drinks
  of date-pomegranate and plum-cardamom.
