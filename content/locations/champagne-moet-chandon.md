description_en: >-
  Moët & Chandon is the best-known Champagne house in the world. Beneath the
  Avenue de Champagne in Épernay lie the house's cellars, which at around 28
  kilometres of galleries are the largest in the region. In the courtyard stands
  a statue of Dom Pérignon, the monk after whom the house's prestige cuvée is
  named.


  On a guided tour you see part of the cellars and hear about the work of the
  cellar masters through the generations, followed by a tasting. Bring a jumper,
  it is cool. Places are limited, so book online. A classic on the Avenue de
  Champagne.
