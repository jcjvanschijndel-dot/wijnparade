description_en: >-
  José María García, born in 1936 in La Horra, sold his grapes from 1968
  onwards to the region's best bodegas, together with his wife Milagros
  Figuero. Only in 2001 did they found their own bodega. The house's
  Tempranillo cuttings are traced back to pre-phylloxera vines, thanks to
  French monks who arrived in La Horra in 1908.


  A visit, every Saturday at noon, is personally guided by members of the
  García Figuero family. Recommended for anyone wanting to visit a
  family bodega with a striking vine history.
