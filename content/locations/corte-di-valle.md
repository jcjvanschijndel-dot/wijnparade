description_en: >-
  A five-minute drive from Greve in Chianti, in the heart of Chianti Classico,
  lies Corte di Valle. The estate has more than twenty hectares of vineyards,
  olive groves and woodland; the vineyard is planted mostly with sangiovese. It
  makes Chianti Classico DOCG and Tuscan IGT wine, as well as extra virgin olive
  oil and saffron from the Florentine hills.


  You can taste wine here, visit the cellar or take a cooking class. Corte di
  Valle is also an agriturismo, with rooms in the renovated historic villa and a
  swimming pool in summer. A pleasant base for a wine trip through Chianti, just
  over half an hour from Florence.
