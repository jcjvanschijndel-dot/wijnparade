description_en: >-
  On the village square of Castiglione Falletto, one of the eleven communes
  allowed to produce Barolo, Luca and Luisa have welcomed guests for twenty
  years in an elegant but family atmosphere. The restaurant overlooks the
  rolling UNESCO vineyards of the Langhe, with views of some of the most famous
  crus of the Barolo appellation. Traditional Piedmontese cooking at a high
  level: vitello tonnato with a subtle twist (basil sauce instead of the
  classic), ravioli del plin al fondo bruno, veal cheek braised in Nebbiolo, and
  traditional bonet to finish. Everything home-made, everything fresh. The
  absolute highlight is the wine cellar, the Crutin: an underground room carved
  into the rock, as local farmers once did to store wine and food. In it Luca
  and Luisa keep more than 1,800 labels, from the great names of the Langhe
  (Giacomo Conterno, Bartolo Mascarello, Vietti, Bruno Giacosa) to small
  producers of exceptional quality and fair price.
