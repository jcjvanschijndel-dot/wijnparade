description_en: >-
  Julia and Benedikt Baltes are the fifth generation to run this
  winery in the centre of Dernau, at the foot of the famous
  Pfarrwingert vineyard. The estate farms organically and follows
  permaculture principles, specialising in long-lived Spätburgunders
  from the valley's best sites.


  A visit, usually on the second Thursday of the month plus the
  following Friday and Saturday, includes tasting the estate's wines.
  Recommended for anyone wanting to discover a small, organic winery
  in the Ahr valley.
