description_en: >-
  On the hill above Parnay, seven kilometres from Saumur, lies Domaine de
  Rocheville, founded in 2004 by Philippe Porché. The estate makes wine in four
  appellations: Saumur-Champigny, Saumur blanc, Saumur rosé and crémant de
  Loire. It has been certified organic since 2019.


  From the estate you look out over the Parnay valley and the Loire. You can
  taste freely, or choose a tour of the winery, the cave cellar and the
  vineyard, optionally with a sharing board or a lunch in the cave. There is
  also a bike ride through the vineyards. Recommended for Saumur-Champigny.
