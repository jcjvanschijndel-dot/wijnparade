  tussen Beaune en Chalon-sur-Saône.
description_en: >-
  Antonin Rodet has been the historic merchant house of Mercurey, in the Côte
  Chalonnaise, since 1875. The house offers wines from all over Burgundy,
  including those of Château de Mercey.


  On the Grande Rue in Mercurey the house welcomes visitors every day, by
  appointment, for a one-hour tour with a tasting: from the vineyard to the
  glass, with the story of the house. Groups are small. A good address to get to
  know Mercurey and the wines of the Côte Chalonnaise, between Beaune and
  Chalon-sur-Saône.
