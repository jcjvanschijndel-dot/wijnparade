description_en: >-
  Since 1905 the Richard-Fliniaux family has been making Champagne from its own
  grand cru vineyards in Aÿ, whose slopes have been on the UNESCO World Heritage
  List since 2015. Mother Nadège and son James carry on the tradition.


  James is your guide and driver himself: in an electric car he drives you
  through the vineyards of Aÿ, with views over the Montagne de Reims and the
  Vallée de la Marne. Along the way you taste three of the house's grand cru
  Champagnes; there is also a version with a picnic. A special way to get to
  know the vineyards of Champagne.
