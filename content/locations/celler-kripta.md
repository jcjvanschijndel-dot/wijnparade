description_en: >-
  Just outside Sant Sadurní d'Anoia is the winery of Agustí Torelló Mata, now
  known as Celler Kripta. The name comes from the house's best-known wine:
  Kripta, a sparkling wine in an amphora-shaped bottle. Agustí Torelló Mata
  designed the shape himself, so that the lees in the bottle keep moving.
  Every bottle is riddled and disgorged by hand and spends no less than 96
  months on the lees. The house is now a member of Corpinnat.


  On a visit you walk through the cellars and the winery, followed by a
  tasting of three long-aged Corpinnat sparkling wines. Booking is required.
  Recommended on a wine trip through the Penedès.
