description_en: >-
  In 1934 Fernand Gasparoux bought vineyards in Pomerol, and Château Ferrand has
  been handed down from generation to generation ever since. Today three
  generations of the family work together. Besides Château Ferrand, the family
  also makes the wines of Château Haut-Ferrand and the prestige cuvée La Fleur
  Ferrand.


  Since 2019 the estate has had a new tasting room. You can visit the cellars
  and taste the wines. An approachable family estate in Pomerol, just outside
  Libourne and a quarter of an hour from Saint-Émilion.
