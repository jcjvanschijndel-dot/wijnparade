description_en: >-
  Roelof and Ilse Visscher founded Wijngaard Hof van Twente on 1 January
  2000, with the first vines going into the ground on 13 May that year.
  The estate, in the village of Bentelo in Twente, grows several PIWI
  grape varieties and makes more than twelve wines from them; son Mart
  Visscher became co-owner in 2024.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's wines. Recommended for anyone
  wanting to get to know winemaking in Twente.
