description_en: >-
  In 1980 agricultural engineer François Devictor settled in Provence and fell
  for Domaine de la Sanglière, on the coast near Bormes-les-Mimosas. He
  replanted the estate completely; today his sons Olivier and Rémy run it. The
  wines fall under Côtes de Provence and Côtes de Provence La Londe.


  A highlight is the "safari": a trip of an hour and a half through the
  vineyards, close to the sea, rounded off with a tasting. Groups are small.
  There is also a tasting cellar. A fun outing for anyone on holiday around Le
  Lavandou, on the way to the beaches of Cabasson and Léoube.
