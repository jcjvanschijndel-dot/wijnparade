description_en: >-
  South of Saint-Émilion, on the banks of the Dordogne in Vignonet, the Bardet
  family has been making wine since 1704. Philippe Bardet and his children farm
  some fifty hectares, spread over more than a hundred plots. They have followed
  the principles of agroecology since 1983 and are certified HVE.


  On a tour of an hour and a half you see the river, part of the vineyards and
  the cellars, and hear about innovative techniques for red wine. You can also
  explore the vineyards on an electric bike. A welcoming family address by the
  water, ten minutes from Saint-Émilion.
