description_en: >-
  In 2014 Silvio Denz, owner of the crystal brand Lalique, bought Château
  Lafaurie-Peyraguey, and since then two of France's oldest luxury names have
  been intertwined: Sauternes wine and Lalique crystal. The château is a Premier
  Grand Cru Classé of 1855 in the Sauternes appellation and has been producing
  for more than 400 years. In 2018, to celebrate that anniversary, it opened a
  boutique hotel and restaurant. The Lalique Femme et Raisins motif now adorns
  every bottle from the château, and the cellar even holds a unique crystal
  barrel made by Lalique. The tour covers the vat room (one of them underground,
  a unique feature), the chai with classic French oak barrels and the crystal
  Lalique barrel. Wine tastings are available in the boutique/tasting room, and
  Lalique crystal accessories are on sale; small crystal knife rests make a nice
  souvenir. Best time to visit: autumn, when botrytis (noble rot) affects the
  grapes, the visual highlight of Sauternes production. Booking in advance is
  recommended. Chef Jérôme Schilling, Meilleur Ouvrier de France, draws
  inspiration from the specifics of each vintage and puts winemaking technique
  at the service of his creations; he's also known as the "culinary winegrower."
  Two Michelin stars: the very first and only two-star restaurant in Sauternes.
  The cooking is determined and unusual, inspired by the terroir of the Gironde.
  The château's wines are exceptional: Lafaurie-Peyraguey and the second wine,
  La Chapelle de Lafaurie.
