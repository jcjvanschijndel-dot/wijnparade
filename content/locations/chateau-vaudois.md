description_en: >-
  On the hills of the Massif des Maures, between Saint-Raphaël and Saint-Tropez,
  lies Château Vaudois. The 93-hectare estate has eighteen hectares of vines and
  looks out over the Mediterranean. It makes elegant Côtes de Provence in red,
  rosé and white.


  On a tour of the winery you taste three wines with commentary. There are also
  an interactive visit, a tasting workshop, a gourmet visit and a picnic among
  the vines. A lovely stop on the Côte d'Azur, close to the beaches of Les
  Issambres.
