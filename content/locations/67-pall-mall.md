description_en: >-
  Close to St James's Palace, in a former bank building designed by Sir Edwin
  Lutyens, former City trader Grant Ashton opened a private club for wine lovers
  in December 2015. The idea was born at the dinner table: Ashton was annoyed by
  the high mark-ups on wine in restaurants. Here, top wines are poured at a
  modest mark-up.


  The wine list runs to thousands of wines and, according to the club, more than
  1,000 are available by the glass — with Coravin playing a leading role. A team
  of 19 sommeliers helps you choose. Note: 67 Pall Mall is a private members'
  club. There is now also a branch in Verbier. Recommended for anyone in London
  who wants to taste what the great world of wine has to offer.
