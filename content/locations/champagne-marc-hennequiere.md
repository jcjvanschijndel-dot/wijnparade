description_en: >-
  In the Sarce valley, in the Côte des Bar and close to Les Riceys, the
  Hennequière family makes Champagne exclusively from plots in Avirey-Lingey.
  Marc bought his first vineyards in 1980; later he and his wife Marie-Nelly
  began making their own Champagne. Their daughter Clothilde now works with
  them.


  On a tour of the vineyard and the cellar, with explanations from the family
  themselves, you taste two cuvées. Visits are by appointment and groups are
  small. An authentic address in the south of Champagne, in the Aube.
