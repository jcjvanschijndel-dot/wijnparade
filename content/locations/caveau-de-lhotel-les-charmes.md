description_en: >-
  On 21 June 2025, Domaine Guy Bocard in Meursault opened the Caveau de l'Hôtel
  Les Charmes. The concept is as simple as it is ambitious: all the Premiers
  Crus of Meursault, most of the climats, and a broad selection of wines from
  the village and the rest of Burgundy, at shop prices, with a fixed corkage of
  only €10 to drink them on site.


  Almost 500 wines, including a hundred Meursaults, big names, promising young
  winemakers and appellations confidentielles, from producers whose cellars are
  rarely open to the public: Sylvain Pataille, Georges Vernay, Philippe Pacalet.
  Alongside the wine: cheese from Alain Hess, charcuterie from the Boucherie de
  Meursault, smoked salmon, and seasonal dishes such as a truffle
  croque-monsieur, warm Mont-d'Or with Aligoté, and burrata.
