description_en: >-
  Owner David van Steenderen is sommelier, wine importer and entrepreneur all at
  once, a rare combination that puts Vineum in a class of its own in Rotterdam.
  The name comes from the Latin for "vineyard" and also refers to the intimate
  city garden behind the restaurant, where you sit under the greenery in summer.
  The wine bar is on the ground floor and the restaurant on the first floor. The
  wine list has more than 1,750 references, from accessible house wines to
  extremely rare bottles. With the Coravin system you can taste exclusive wines
  by the glass that are normally only available by the bottle. All wine is
  served in top glassware from Riedel, Zalto and Spiegelau, and kept in a
  climate-controlled wine room. Named Best Medium-Sized Wine List in Europe by
  The World of Fine Wine. Chef Danny Mouthaan combines classic French techniques
  with modern accents: lobster salad with Baeri caviar, ceviche of young
  mackerel, grilled entrecôte with béarnaise. All dishes also come in half
  portions.
