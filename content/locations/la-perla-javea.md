Ook gegrild vlees en vis van het rooster aanwezig. De wijnkaart telt meer dan 150 referenties, met nadruk op Iberische wijnen: frisse Albariño, mineralige Mediterrane witte wijnen en gestructureerde roden, aangevuld met Champagne en klassieke Europese namen. description_en: >-
  Right on the promenade, with a bright, modern interior and a terrace
  overlooking the Mediterranean. The window and terrace tables are in particular
  demand at sunset, so book them at least two to three weeks ahead at weekends.
  In the middle of the room is a built-in wine cellar with temperature and
  humidity control, visible to all guests. The kitchen buys fresh every day at
  the Xàbia fish auction. The speciality is the arroces and fideuàs, up to
  twelve variations, for a minimum of two people. Recommended: the turbot with
  pil-pil sauce. There's also grilled meat and fish. The wine list has more than
  150 references, with an emphasis on Iberian wines (fresh Albariño, mineral
  Mediterranean whites and structured reds), supplemented with Champagne and
  classic European names. The sommelier is happy to advise on wines for rice
  dishes or grilled fish.
