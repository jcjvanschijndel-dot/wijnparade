description_en: >-
  Vieira de Sousa has made Port for five generations, since
  great-grandfather José Silvério Vieira de Sousa founded the brand. The
  house now farms more than 60 hectares across four quintas in Peso da
  Régua, the birthplace of the Douro — the world's first demarcated and
  regulated wine region. Tastings take place in a restored, centuries-old
  winery, where old stone wine presses and traditional Douro barrels can
  be seen.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's Douro and Port wines. Recommended
  for anyone wanting to visit a restored historic cellar in the birthplace
  of the Douro.
