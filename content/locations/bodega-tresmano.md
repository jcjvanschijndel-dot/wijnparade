description_en: >-
  Bodega Tresmano lies in Padilla de Duero, in Ribera del Duero's
  "golden mile", about five kilometres from Peñafiel. The roughly
  two-hour tour leads through the bodega and the vineyards to a viewing
  terrace with a 360-degree view, passing an olive tree around 1,500
  years old.


  A visit includes tasting three of the estate's wines with a snack.
  Recommended for anyone wanting to combine a walk through the vineyards
  with a spectacular view over Ribera del Duero.
