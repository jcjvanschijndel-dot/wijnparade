description_en: >-
  Château Suduiraut in Preignac is one of the great historic estates of
  Sauternes and was classified as a Premier Cru Classé in 1855. The estate has
  borne the name Suduiraut since 1580. The seventeenth-century château is on the
  French heritage register, and the French-style gardens were designed by Le
  Nôtre, the landscape architect of Versailles.


  Since 1992 the estate has belonged to AXA Millésimes. Visits are by
  appointment, Monday to Saturday: you see the vineyard and the cellar and taste
  several vintages. There is also a workshop on the aromas of Sauternes. A
  magnificent address for lovers of sweet wine.
