description_en: >-
  The Mellot family has been making wine in Sancerre for five centuries; at the
  end of the seventeenth century César Mellot was wine adviser to Louis XIV. The
  house of Joseph Mellot grew by acquiring other estates and now offers wines
  from across the Centre-Loire, from Sancerre and Pouilly-Fumé to smaller
  appellations.


  In the historic cellar and shop in the town of Sancerre you get a one-hour
  tour and taste four wines, including a prestige cuvée, served with crottin de
  Chavignol. Book a day in advance. A lovely place to taste the region's
  sauvignon blanc, in the middle of Sancerre.
