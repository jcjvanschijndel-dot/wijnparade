description_en: >-
  On the Hardebollenstraat, a side street of the Voorstraat close to the Neude,
  Julian Cleton opened this wine and tea shop in September 2024. Cleton also
  owns the wine bar VinVin, a few metres away, and the shop follows the same
  approach: around eighty European wines, organic or natural, from small
  producers who take care of nature. He also sells tea from Palais des Thés.


  Tastings and workshops are held in the shop. Combine a visit with a glass at VinVin, around the corner. Recommended for anyone in Utrecht's city centre looking for natural wine with personal advice.
