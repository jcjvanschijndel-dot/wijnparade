  zoete wijnen van Banyuls en Banyuls Grand Cru gemaakt, en de droge wijnen van

  Een gids neemt je in veertig minuten mee langs de geschiedenis van de

  Daarna proef je Collioure in rood, wit en rosé, en Banyuls. De mooiste wijnen
  rijpen in een ondergrondse kelder, een voormalige commanderij van de

description_en: >-
  Above Banyuls-sur-Mer, on the Côte Vermeille close to the Spanish border, lies
  the winery of Terres des Templiers. The steep terraces of Banyuls were built
  and maintained by generations of winegrowers. This is where the fortified
  sweet wines of Banyuls and Banyuls Grand Cru are made, along with the dry
  wines of Collioure.


  A guide takes you through the history of the vineyard and the way the wines
  are made and aged, partly in casks in the open air, in forty minutes. You then
  taste Collioure in red, white and rosé, and Banyuls. The finest wines age in
  an underground cellar, a former Templar commandery. Recommended on the Catalan
  coast.
