description_en: >-
  With almost 48 hectares of vines, Château de Sales is the largest wine estate
  in Pomerol. The ninety-hectare estate has been in the same family for
  centuries; since 2017 fourteen cousins from the De Lambert family have run it
  together. The seventeenth-century château has been a listed monument since
  1996.


  Visits are by appointment, in French or English. You hear about the family's
  history and the terroir, and taste the house's two wines: Château de Sales and
  Château Chantalouette. A quiet, historic address in Pomerol, just outside
  Libourne.
