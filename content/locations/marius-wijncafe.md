description_en: >-
  In the Zeeheldenkwartier, Marius has been a wine shop on the Piet Heinstraat
  since 2005, with more than 800 wines, most of them bought directly from the
  winegrower. Right next door is the wine café of Marius van der Vliet and Luuk
  van Amerongen, and the 2 spaces are connected by a passage.


  That makes for a great concept: choose a bottle in the shop and have it opened at your table for a fixed corkage fee. There are also wines and saké by the glass on the chalkboards, and chef Ricardo van Ede cooks dishes to go with them. Recommended for a wine evening in The Hague.
