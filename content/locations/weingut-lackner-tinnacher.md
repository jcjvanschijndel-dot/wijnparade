description_en: >-
  Weingut Lackner-Tinnacher in Gamlitz has made wine for generations; the
  family's winemaking history goes back to 1770. Since 1978 the house
  has carried the name of Wilma Lackner and Fritz Tinnacher, after their
  marriage; their daughter Katharina Lackner now runs the estate.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's wines, generally Monday to
  Saturday from 10am to 5pm (late March to late November). Recommended
  for anyone wanting to combine South Styria with a long winemaking
  history.
