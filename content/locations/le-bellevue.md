description_en: >-
  High on a hill above Champillon, halfway between Reims and Épernay, stands the
  Royal Champagne Hotel & Spa, a former coaching inn where Napoleon once
  stopped. Today it's a five-star hotel with two restaurants: the
  Michelin-starred Le Royal and the chic brasserie Le Bellevue. The hotel lies
  in the golden triangle of Champagne, ten minutes from Épernay and twenty
  minutes from Reims cathedral. Le Bellevue offers a 360-degree panorama over
  the vineyards of the Marne valley: the most spectacular terrace in the whole
  Champagne region. Open from morning to evening: breakfast, lunch, afternoon
  tea and dinner. The crystal chandelier with a thousand hanging pendants turns
  the room into a fairy-tale setting after sunset. An aperitif on the terrace
  with champagne tapas is the most popular moment of the day. Bar Abysse serves
  cocktails and champagnes, from Leclerc Briant onwards, until late in the
  evening. Le Bellevue serves accessible brasserie classics: Reims ham, herb
  chicken, elegant desserts. Chef Christophe Raoux (Meilleur Ouvrier de France
  2015) runs the Michelin-starred restaurant Le Royal, for the real gastronomic
  experience, by reservation. The hotel's champagne list is recognised by Star
  Wine List and focuses on small grower producers you rarely come across
  elsewhere, guided by a team of passionate sommeliers.
