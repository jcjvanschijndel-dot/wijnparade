description_en: >-
  Weingut Tschermonegg, now in its fifth generation, farms 33 contiguous
  hectares of vineyard around Glanz. The estate includes a Buschenschank,
  right among its own vineyards, with seating for a hundred guests
  indoors and out, open from late March to mid-November.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's wines, optionally combined with
  a simple meal at the Buschenschank. Recommended for anyone wanting to
  pair wine tasting with the typical Styrian Buschenschank tradition.
