description_en: >-
  In Bussia, near Monforte d'Alba, lies Cascina Sòt. Maria and Giuseppe worked
  here for years as sharecroppers before buying the farm in 1975; with their son
  Leonardo and his wife Silvana, wine became the main business. The family
  estate has vineyards in some of the best Barolo crus. The top wine is the
  Barolo Bricco San Pietro.


  On a tour of the vineyard and the cellar you taste the Barolos from Monforte,
  among other wines, with a board of cured meats and cheese. A personal address
  for a Barolo tasting in the Langhe, between Monforte d'Alba and Barolo.
