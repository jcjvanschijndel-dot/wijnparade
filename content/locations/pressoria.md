description_en: >-
  In a former Pommery pressing centre from 1902, in Aÿ, you will find Pressoria:
  a visitor centre about Champagne that appeals to all the senses. In ten rooms
  you discover at your own pace how Champagne is made, from the vineyard and
  pressing to fermentation and ageing, with scents, sounds and images.


  The tour takes one and a half to two hours and ends with a tasting of
  Champagne, or grape juice for children. That makes it suitable for families
  too. Pressoria is ten minutes from Épernay and a good starting point for a day
  visiting the wine houses of Aÿ and the Vallée de la Marne.
