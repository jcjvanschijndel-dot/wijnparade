description_en: >-
  Felipe Gutiérrez de la Vega has translated his love of music, opera and
  literature directly into his wines: every cuvée bears the name of a classical
  piece of music. His wine Casta Diva Cosecha Miel, named after the famous aria
  from Bellini's Norma, was chosen in 2004 as the dessert wine for the wedding
  of King Felipe and Queen Letizia of Spain. From 2010 Felipe deliberately left
  the DO Alicante appellation. Two options: a classic tour of the winery, the
  labelling workshop and the impressive barrel room 5 metres underground, ending
  with a tasting of three wines (white, red and sweet). Or the special option:
  an eno-cultural tour led by Felipe himself, in which he lets you taste
  exclusively selected wines from his personal collection.
