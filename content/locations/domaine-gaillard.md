description_en: >-
  In Davayé, in the south of the Mâconnais, Roger Gaillard started out in 1981
  with a single hectare of Pouilly-Fuissé. His son Romain joined in 2015, and
  the domaine now covers 11 hectares. It is a true family business: Roger,
  Romain and Véronique Gaillard welcome visitors themselves. The range
  includes Saint-Véran, Pouilly-Fuissé, Mâcon-Villages, Mâcon Charnay and
  Crémant de Bourgogne, and the Guide Hachette rates, among others, the
  Pouilly-Fuissé Premier Cru Au Vignerais.





  You can taste the wines in the stone tasting cellar, and there are also
  tours of the vineyards and the cellar, in English if you wish. It is also an
  ideal place to stock up on good Chardonnay at reasonable prices when you're
  passing through. A lovely stop on a wine trip through southern Burgundy.
