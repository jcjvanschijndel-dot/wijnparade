description_en: >-
  In 2008 Erik Leferink came across a dilapidated farm in the South Limburg
  hamlet of Holset, close to the Three-Country Point and the highest point in
  the Netherlands. Together with Marianne Blijdesteijn he decided to turn it
  into a sparkling wine house, a bold choice in the Netherlands. The vineyard
  was planted, the farm thoroughly rebuilt with circular and ecological
  materials, and in 2017 the first guests could be welcomed. Domein Holset is
  now known as the sparkling wine house of the Low Countries, and in 2023 it
  took over the neighbouring Wijngaard Raarberg. Walk through the vineyard,
  taste the sparkling wines on the terrace with a view, and combine it with the
  Route des Vins Vijlen-Holset past several wine estates in the area. Or rent an
  e-bike and ride to the Three-Country Point. The tasting room and wine shop are
  open daily from 11.00-17.00, and you can simply walk in.
