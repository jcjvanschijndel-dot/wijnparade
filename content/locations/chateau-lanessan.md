description_en: >-
  Since 1793 Château Lanessan has been owned by the Bouteiller family. The
  380-hectare estate lies in the Haut-Médoc, near Cussac-Fort-Médoc, between
  Margaux and Pauillac. On your way you see two châteaux: first Château
  Lachesnaye and then Château Lanessan itself, with its buildings from the late
  nineteenth century.


  A highlight is the horse museum, with the family's carriages, a complete tack
  room and historic stables with marble feeding troughs. You also visit the
  vineyard and the cellar and learn how to taste. Recommended for families on a
  trip through the Médoc.
