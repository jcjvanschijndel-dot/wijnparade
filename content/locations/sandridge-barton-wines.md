description_en: >-
  Sandridge Barton began in 1981 as Sharpham Wine on the Sharpham Estate
  near Totnes, one of the earliest pioneers of English winemaking. In
  2008 a second vineyard was planted across the river at Sandridge
  Barton, and in 2020 the whole winery moved there, into a converted
  dairy farm.


  You taste sparkling, still and red wines on the bank of the River
  Dart. Recommended for anyone wanting to discover the history of
  English winemaking in South Devon.
