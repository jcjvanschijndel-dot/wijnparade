description_en: >-
  In Soussans, at the northern end of the Margaux appellation, lies Château Haut
  Breton Larigaudière. The De Schepper family bought it in 1964 and the third
  generation is now at the helm. The fifteen hectares of vines lie on some of
  the best terroirs of Margaux. The estate is certified HVE.


  On a tour you see the vat room and the barrel cellar, then taste three or four
  wines, from Margaux and another Bordeaux appellation. There are also versions
  with tapas or with chocolate. An approachable address to taste Margaux, about
  an hour's drive from Bordeaux.
