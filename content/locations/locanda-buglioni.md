description_en: >-
  The Buglioni family did good business in the textile industry before switching
  to wine. Today the family runs an entire wine universe in San Pietro in
  Cariano, 10 km from Verona and 15 km from Lake Garda: organic vineyards in the
  Valpolicella Classica, a wine restaurant, a wine relais and even an osteria
  and enoteca in the centre of Verona, where they pour only their own wines.
  Locanda Buglioni is the wine restaurant, within walking distance of the Dimora
  Buglioni, the wine relais in a restored 19th-century farmhouse. Everything is
  cooked over fires fuelled by vine prunings and vineyard waste. Special
  experiences: the Mathusalem Experience (cellar visit + vineyard + drying loft
  for appassimento + 5 courses with 5 wines), the Balthazar Experience (aperitif
  in the enoteca + overnight stay with breakfast + cellar tour + food and wine
  tasting) and the Ristotto Experience (cellar + vineyard + 8 wines with the
  Locanda's iconic risotto dish).
