description_en: >-
  In the village of Viens, 580 metres up in a protected valley in the heart of
  the Luberon, a new generation has built one of the most honest and complete
  wine tourism ecosystems in Provence. In July and August La Terrasse des Davids
  is open on Friday and Saturday evenings from 18.00 to 23.00 for wine tastings
  with views of the vineyards and the mountains of the Luberon. Regional dishes
  are served with the wines, along with a variety of vegetables, charcuterie and
  cheeses. The estate also offers accommodation in renovated stone hamlets
  spread across the property, fully furnished and surrounded by vineyards and
  nature. Ideal if you want to experience the estate at a slower pace.
