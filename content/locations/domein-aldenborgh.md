description_en: >-
  Domein Aldenborgh in Eys is an organic winery on the south-facing slope
  of the Eys stream valley, with a soil of Gulpen chalk and Kunrade
  limestone. Peter Pelzer is the winegrower and winemaker; his son Chris
  joined in 2017 after studying biology. In 2005 the estate was named
  Vineyard of the Year for both Limburgs by the international Confrérie.


  A visit includes tasting the estate's organic wines, including Riesling,
  Pinot Gris and Auxerrois. Recommended for anyone wanting to discover
  organic winemaking in South Limburg.
