description_en: >-
  Small, cosy and instantly familiar: that's how Bar Babar on the
  Utrechtsestraat feels. Owners Ruben Charbon and Django Reinhart deliberately
  work with Amsterdam specialists: bread from Mater, cheese from Zuivelhoeve,
  wines from Pieksman and Vleck. Natural wines, a French bistro atmosphere,
  candlelight. Almost all the wines are available by the glass, so you can try
  lots of different things. The atmosphere is reminiscent of a small Parisian
  bistro: wine-coloured candles, warm-toned walls, small tables and bar stools.
  It's always pleasantly busy, so getting a seat feels like a gift. Walk-in
  only, no reservations.
