description_en: >-
  In Eguisheim, the cradle of Alsace winegrowing, Domaine Emile Beyer carries on
  a wine tradition of more than fourteen generations. Christian and Valérie
  Beyer have run the estate since 2004 and farm organically and biodynamically.
  A third of the seventeen hectares lies in the grands crus Pfersigberg and
  Eichberg.


  The estate is on the castle square, in the middle of the circular medieval
  village. A tasting takes you on a journey through the history and terroirs of
  the estate, in small groups. Recommended for grand cru riesling, ten minutes
  from Colmar.
