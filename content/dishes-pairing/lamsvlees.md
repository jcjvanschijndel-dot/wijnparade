intro_en: >-
  Lamb has a full flavour and some fat, so it goes well with a red wine with
  tannin. Rioja, made from Tempranillo aged in oak, is the classic with lamb
  chops and roast lamb. Ribera del Duero is more robust and goes with the
  suckling lamb (lechazo) of Castile. Cabernet Sauvignon goes with lamb with
  herbs or a jus. Côtes du Rhône, made from Grenache and Syrah, goes with lamb
  with rosemary, thyme and garlic. With slow-cooked lamb, try an Amarone.
