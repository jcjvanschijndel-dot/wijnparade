  A steak has fat and protein, and these soften the tannin in red wine. That is
  why robust red wines work well. Cabernet Sauvignon from Bordeaux is the

  pepper. A Côtes du Rhône is an affordable choice. Barolo goes with a
  Piedmontese steak (fassone) or a bistecca. Pinot Noir goes with a lean cut,
  such as fillet, or with steak tartare.
