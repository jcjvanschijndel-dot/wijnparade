intro_en: >-
  With chocolate the wine must be at least as sweet as the dessert, or it will
  taste sour and bitter. Banyuls, a fortified red wine from Roussillon, is the
  classic with dark chocolate. Pedro Ximénez sherry, dark and syrupy, goes with
  chocolate cake and mousse. Madeira (Bual or Malmsey) has a touch of acidity
  that works well with chocolate with nuts or caramel. Muscat goes better with
  milk chocolate or chocolate with fruit. A Cabernet Sauvignon only works with
  very dark chocolate with little sugar.
