intro: >-
  Mosselen zijn zilt en licht zoet, en meestal gekookt met witte wijn, sjalot

  pan. Muscadet uit de Loire is de klassieker: licht, strak en zilt. Sauvignon

intro_en: >-
  Mussels are briny and slightly sweet, and are usually cooked with white wine,
  shallots and herbs. Choose a fresh, dry white wine, ideally the same one you
  used in the pan. Muscadet from the Loire is the classic: light, taut and
  saline. Sauvignon Blanc goes with mussels with lots of herbs or garlic.
  Chablis is a little fuller and goes with mussels in a cream sauce. With
  mussels in curry, a more aromatic wine such as a dry Riesling works well.
