intro_en: >-
  Oysters call for a wine with freshness and a saline touch: dry, taut and
  without oak. The classic is Muscadet from the Loire, which grows around Nantes
  close to the Atlantic oyster beds. Chablis, made from Chardonnay on limestone
  full of fossilised oyster shells, has the same mineral tautness. Champagne
  works thanks to its acidity and bubbles, especially a Brut Nature or Extra
  Brut. A fresh Sauvignon Blanc, such as a Sancerre, is also a good match. Avoid
  oaked or sweet wines: they overpower the oyster.
