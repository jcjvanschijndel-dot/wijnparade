intro_en: >-
  Brussels sprouts are slightly bitter and have a sulphurous flavour that many
  wines don't handle well. A fresh Sauvignon Blanc with green notes works best,
  especially with roasted sprouts or sprouts with bacon. Don't choose a red wine
  with a lot of tannin: it makes the sprouts more bitter.
